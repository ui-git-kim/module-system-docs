A **schedule** says whose time it is, over and over: who has the children which nights, who is on which shift. It is laid out on its **cycle** (a fortnight, a roster week, a summer holiday) rather than on dates, so a proposal can be worked out before anyone knows when it would start. Added in v1.22.0.

An admin sets up each **kind of schedule** once. Each person then lays out their own schedules of that kind on their cycles.

<Aside type="note" title="What is built so far">
v1.22.0 is the admin setup: the kinds of schedule, and the fields Tasks adds to Structure's kinds for them. Laying a schedule out on its cycle, comparing schedules, holidays and special occasions, and **Add to calendar** come next: see the [Roadmap](/modules/tasks/roadmap/#schedules-by-their-cycle). Calendars that colour their days, set up before v1.22.0, keep working and are listed under **Calendars**.
</Aside>

## What a schedule is made of

| Piece | A care arrangement | A roster |
|---|---|---|
| **The schedule**: the kind of entry that holds each one | Arrangement | Roster |
| **What sets whose time it is**: a moment (from here, someone else's time) or a block (a start, an end and whose time it is) | A changeover | A shift |
| **Whose time it is**: a link to an entry, or a choice from a list | The person it goes to | The staff member, or Early, Late, Night |
| **Other kinds shown**: a dot on their days | Appointments, school events | Training days |
| **Alternative blocks**: periods that set the usual pattern aside | School holidays, special occasions | Public holidays |

Nothing is decided by a kind's or a field's name. The admin chooses each piece.

## Set up a kind of schedule

In **Admin › Tasks**, open **Views**, then **Schedules**, and choose **New kind of schedule**.

<Steps>

1. **The schedule**

   - **Its name** and **its short name** (chosen once).
   - **The schedule is a**: the kind of entry in Structure that holds each schedule.

2. **What sets whose time it is**

   - **It is set by**: the kind of entry that marks whose time it is. **Add one** makes a new kind for everyone in Structure, filed under the schedule's kind, with a Who field of its own if you ask for one.
   - Tasks adds **Starts** and **Ends** to this kind when you save. An entry with no end is a **moment**: from that time, the time is someone else's until the next one. An entry with an end is a **block**: its time is someone's from start to end. The person chooses each time; the admin doesn't.
   - **Whose time it is**: the field on it that names who: a link to an entry (a person), a choice from a list, or text. **Add a Who field** adds one for you.

3. **Also on the schedule**

   - **Other kinds shown**: pick as many as you like. They show as a dot on their days and never change whose time it is.
   - **Alternative blocks**: switch on, then choose the kinds whose entries mark a period under **Periods come from**, such as School holidays. Tasks adds Starts and Ends to these too, so each period has its start and finish times. Holidays are entered once and every schedule shares them. Make summer holidays a kind of its own if they split differently from shorter breaks.

4. **Arrangements**

   Shown once the schedule's kind is chosen.

   - **Its type is in**: a list field on the schedule's kind, such as Type, with a choice for each sort of arrangement. For each choice, say what it means: **The regular pattern**, **Holidays** or **Special occasions**. Holidays and special occasions are arrangements of their own, so one set can be used by several schedules: the current arrangement and a proposal, say.
   - **What it uses is in**: a link field on the schedule's kind naming the holiday and special-occasion arrangements a regular one uses.

5. **Several schedules**

   - **Compare up to**: how many schedules a person can see side by side. 4 unless you change it.

6. **Save**

   Tasks adds Starts and Ends to the kinds that need them. Anything still to sort out is listed at the top of the setup and in the list's **Needs a look** column.

</Steps>

## The fields Tasks adds

Tasks adds its fields through Structure's **field sources** (Structure 1.82 and later). They are real fields: shown in Structure's forms and on each entry's page, and read by formulas, conditions and exports.

| Field | Kept by | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Starts** | Tasks | When the moment is, or when the block starts |
| **Ends** | Tasks | When the block ends. Empty for a moment |
| **Who** (when you choose **Add a Who field**) | The kind | Whose time it is. Handed to the kind as its own straight away |

- **Starts and Ends are kept by Tasks** while it runs: Structure's type editor shows them under "Fields from other modules", and an admin can move, relabel or switch them off there, but not change what they are. People fill them in as usual.
- **Who is the kind's own field.** Structure locks a field a module keeps, so Tasks adds Who and hands it over at once. Set it up in Structure like any of its fields: which kinds it can link to, and **Only entries where…** to narrow who can be chosen. Inside a matter, the picker already offers only that matter's people.
- **A kind no schedule uses any more** keeps Starts and Ends as its own fields, with every entry's times as they were.

## Colours

Each person (or each value) has one colour on every schedule.

- **A link to an entry** takes that entry's colour. In Structure, open the person's kind, switch on **Custom Colour** on its **Properties** tab, and give each person their colour on their own page. The setup says so if it's off.
- **A choice from a list** gets a calm colour from Tasks, the same one everywhere.

## What needs a look

The setup checks itself every time it is read, and says what to do in plain words, without blocking anything:

- **Starts and Ends aren't on a kind yet**: save the schedule to add them.
- **No field says whose time it is**: choose one, or add one.
- **Custom Colour is off** on the kind Who links to.
- **The kind that sets whose time it is can't be filed under the schedule's kind**: allow it on the **Hierarchy** tab of either kind in Structure.
- **A kind or field it uses has gone or changed** in Structure.

Saving is refused only for something that can't work: a kind that isn't there, a Who field that isn't a link, list or text, or the same kind as both the schedule and what sets whose time it is.

## Good to know

- **Structure 1.82 or later** is needed for the fields Tasks adds. Tasks itself needs Structure 1.88.
- **Kinds for everyone only.** A schedule's kinds are app-level kinds of entry, so every person can have schedules of them.
- **Export and import.** Kinds of schedule are part of **Admin › Tasks › Data**. Starts and Ends are added once an import has finished, never during its practice run.
- **Removing a kind of schedule** leaves its schedules and their entries in Structure.
