A **schedule** says whose time it is, over and over: who has the children which nights, who is on which shift. It is laid out on its **cycle** (a fortnight, a roster week, a summer holiday) rather than on dates, so a proposal can be worked out before anyone knows when it would start. Added in v1.22.0.

An admin sets up each **kind of schedule** once. Each person then lays out their own schedules of that kind on their cycles.

<Aside type="note" title="What is built so far">
v1.22.0 is the admin setup: the kinds of schedule, and the fields Tasks adds to Structure's kinds for them. v1.22.1 is the person's side: [laying schedules out on their cycles](#lay-out-a-schedule) and comparing them. v1.22.2 adds [holidays and special occasions](#holidays-and-special-occasions) and a schedule on real dates, and v1.22.3 [Add to calendar](#add-to-calendar). What might come after is on the [Roadmap](/modules/tasks/roadmap/#schedules-by-their-cycle). Calendars that colour their days, set up before v1.22.0, keep working and are listed under **Calendars**.
</Aside>

## What a schedule is made of

| Piece | A care arrangement | A roster |
|---|---|---|
| **The schedule**: the kind of entry that holds each one | Arrangement | Roster |
| **What sets whose time it is**: a moment (from here, someone else's time) or a block (a start, an end and whose time it is) | A changeover | A shift |
| **Whose time it is**: a link to an entry, or a choice from a list | The person it goes to | The staff member, or Early, Late, Night |
| **Other kinds shown**: a dot on their days | Appointments, school events | Training days |
| **Alternative blocks**: periods that set the usual pattern aside | School holidays, special occasions | Public holidays |

Nothing is decided by a kind's or a field's name. The admin chooses each piece.

## Set up a kind of schedule

In **Admin › Tasks**, open **Views**, then **Schedules**, and choose **New kind of schedule**. (Schedules are set up here, not in **Entry types**, which is for kinds used as tasks.)

<Steps>

1. **The schedule**

   - **Its name** and **its short name** (chosen once).
   - **The schedule is a**: the kind of entry in Structure that holds each schedule. Tasks adds **Starts**, **Ends**, **Cycle length (days)** and **Repeats** to it when you save, so you can place them on its page (see [The fields Tasks adds](#the-fields-tasks-adds)).

2. **What sets whose time it is**

   - **It is set by**: the kind of entry that marks whose time it is. **Add one** makes a new kind in Structure, filed under the schedule's kind, with a Who field of its own if you ask for one. It is for everyone, or your own when the schedule's kind is your own.
   - Tasks adds **Starts** and **Ends** to this kind when you save. An entry with no end is a **moment**: from that time, the time is someone else's until the next one. An entry with an end is a **block**: its time is someone's from start to end. The person chooses each time; the admin doesn't.
   - **Whose time it is**: the field on it that names who: a link to an entry (a person), a choice from a list, or text. **Add a Who field** adds one for you.

3. **Also on the schedule**

   - **Other kinds shown**: pick as many as you like. They show as a dot on their days and never change whose time it is.
   - **Alternative blocks**: switch on so holidays and special occasions can set the usual pattern aside.
   - **Periods come from** (optional): a kind of entry for holidays that come round again, such as **School holidays**, with an entry for each one (Term 1 holidays 2027, say). Make the kind in Structure first; a name is all it needs, and Tasks adds Starts and Ends to it, so each holiday has its start and finish times. Holidays are entered once and every schedule shares them. Make summer holidays a kind of its own if they split differently from shorter breaks.

4. **Arrangements**

   Shown once the schedule's kind is chosen.

   - **Its type is in**: a list field on the schedule's kind, such as Type, with a choice for each sort of arrangement. For each choice, say what it means: **The regular pattern**, **Holidays** or **Special occasions**. Holidays and special occasions are arrangements of their own, so one set can be used by several schedules: the current arrangement and a proposal, say.
   - **What it uses is in**: a link field on the schedule's kind naming the holiday and special-occasion arrangements a regular one uses.

5. **Several schedules**

   - **Compare up to**: how many schedules a person can see side by side. 4 unless you change it.

6. **Save**

   Tasks adds Starts and Ends to the kinds that need them. Anything still to sort out is listed at the top of the setup and in the list's **Needs a look** column.

</Steps>

## The fields Tasks adds

Tasks adds its fields through Structure's **field sources** (Structure 1.82 and later). They are real fields: shown in Structure's forms and on each entry's page, and read by formulas, conditions and exports.

On the kind that sets whose time it is, and on each kind of period:

| Field | Kept by | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Starts** | Tasks | When the moment is, or when the block starts |
| **Ends** | Tasks | When the block ends. Empty for a moment |
| **Who** (when you choose **Add a Who field**) | The kind | Whose time it is. Handed to the kind as its own straight away |

On the schedule's own kind (an Arrangement, say), added in v1.22.9:

| Field | Kept by | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Starts** | Tasks | When it begins. Its date is the first day of its cycle |
| **Ends** | Tasks | When it finishes. Empty if it carries on. For one that runs once, its last day |
| **Cycle length (days)** | Tasks | How many days its pattern covers before it starts again (14 for a new one) |
| **Repeats** | Tasks | Off, it runs once, from Starts to Ends (on for a new one) |

They hold the same values as **Change the cycle**, so you can set them in either place. Put them in a section of their own with Structure's page designer, show them in lists, or use them in formulas and conditions.

- **Starts and Ends are kept by Tasks** while it runs: Structure's type editor shows them under "Fields from other modules", and an admin can move, relabel or switch them off there, but not change what they are. People fill them in as usual.
- **Who is the kind's own field.** Structure locks a field a module keeps, so Tasks adds Who and hands it over at once. Set it up in Structure like any of its fields: which kinds it can link to, and **Only entries where…** to narrow who can be chosen. Inside a matter, the picker already offers only that matter's people.
- **A kind no schedule uses any more** keeps Starts and Ends as its own fields, with every entry's times as they were.

## Colours

Each person (or each value) has one colour on every schedule.

- **A link to an entry** takes that entry's colour, as Structure shows it: the entry's own colour, else its kind's colour for a field's value, else its kind's colour. In Structure (1.88.5 or later), open the person's kind, switch on **Each entry can have its own colour** on its **Basic** tab, then give each person their colour under **Colour** on their edit form or their Settings tab. The setup says so if it's off. (The older **Custom Colour** switch never did anything, and Structure 1.88.5 removed it.)
- **A choice from a list** gets a calm colour from Tasks, the same one everywhere.

## What needs a look

The setup checks itself every time it is read, and says what to do in plain words, without blocking anything:

- **Starts and Ends aren't on a kind yet**: save the schedule to add them.
- **No field says whose time it is**: choose one, or add one.
- **"Each entry can have its own colour" is off** on the kind Who links to.
- **The kind that sets whose time it is can't be filed under the schedule's kind**: allow it on the **Hierarchy** tab of either kind in Structure.
- **A kind or field it uses has gone or changed** in Structure.

Saving is refused only for something that can't work: a kind that isn't there, a Who field that isn't a link, list or text, or the same kind as both the schedule and what sets whose time it is.

## Lay out a schedule

Each person lays out their own schedules. Added in v1.22.1.

### Where

- **Schedules** in the view switcher at the top of every Tasks page, once an admin has set up a kind of schedule. It shows all of the person's arrangements of a kind (with more than one kind, choose which).
- **A tab on the entries arrangements are filed under**, named after the kind of schedule: on a matter's page, say. It shows only the arrangements filed under that entry. The tab is on the kinds of entry the schedule's kind can sit under (its **Hierarchy** tab in Structure).
- **On an arrangement's own page**, as a widget an admin adds (v1.22.7). In Structure, open the schedule's kind (an Arrangement, say) in the page designer and add the widget named after the kind of schedule. It shows that one arrangement, laid out on its cycle, with everything the Schedules page offers for it: its cycle and pattern, what it uses, its real dates and **Add to calendar** (or its special occasions or holiday rules). It shows only where it has been added.

### The list, and comparing

Every arrangement is listed with its cycle in words and what it is (Regular, Holidays, Special occasions, from its type field). **Open** shows one below the list. **New arrangement** makes another (a proposal, say), filed under the entry you're on; it starts as a two-week cycle with nothing on it yet.

Tick two or more and choose **Compare the ticked ones** to see them side by side, week by week, up to the number the admin chose. A ringed day is one whose night goes to someone else than in the first; arrangements whose cycles are different lengths aren't marked against each other. **Back to one** returns to the arrangement you had open.

### The cycle

**Change the cycle** sets:

- **How long it is**: 1, 2, 3 or 4 weeks, a number of days (a 10-day roster, say), or **Once, from one date to another** (a summer holiday).
- **It starts on**: the weekday its first day falls on. Monday unless you change it.
- **It repeats**: off, it runs once.
- **Its start date**: optional. A proposal can have none until you know when it would start. A start date sets the weekday for you.
- **It ends on**: optional, for one that repeats: when it stops (Add to calendar can run until then). One that runs once has a first and a last day instead.

These are the arrangement's **Starts**, **Ends**, **Cycle length (days)** and **Repeats** fields too (v1.22.9), so whatever you set on its page in Structure shows here, and the other way round. A time in Starts or Ends is kept: changing the date here keeps it.

Shortening a cycle keeps the pattern: anything past the new last day waits until the cycle is longer again.

### The pattern

The cycle shows as weeks of day tiles. Each tile is washed in the colour of whoever has the time as the day ends (whose night it is), and its bottom line shows both colours on a day the time changes hands.

Choose a day to see what happens on it, and to add:

- **A changeover**: a time, and who the time passes to. It stays theirs until the next changeover. In a cycle that repeats, the time before the first changeover belongs to whoever the last one names (it carries round); in one that runs once, to no one.
- **A block**: a start, an end (the same day, the next day, or a few days later) and whose time it is. A block that ends the same day leaves the night where it was: it shows as a dot in that person's colour and counts as hours. One that runs past midnight changes whose night it is.

**Remove** takes one away. Every change saves straight away.

### Start from a set-up

**Start from a set-up**, beside **Change the cycle**, fills the days from a common set-up, so you only change what's different (v1.22.11). It works on any arrangement, regular or holidays.

| Set-up | What it fills in |
|---|---|
| **Week about** | A week each, changing over on the first day of the cycle |
| **Alternate weekends** | With who it starts with, except every second weekend, Friday to Monday, with the other |
| **Every weekend** | Weekdays with who it starts with, every weekend, Friday to Monday, with the other (a one-week cycle) |
| **2-2-3** | Two days, two days and three days, then the other way round the next week |
| **2-2-5-5** | Monday and Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, then five days each: Friday to Tuesday, and Wednesday to Sunday |
| **3-4-4-3** | Three days and four, then four and three the next week |
| **Split in half** | The first half of the cycle with who it starts with, the second half with the other |
| **One person throughout** | All of it with one person |
| **A copy of** another arrangement | Its days and changeovers, copied (changing either later doesn't change the other) |

- Choose **who it starts with**, **who has the other turns**, and the time the changeovers happen (**Back at** for the Monday after a weekend).
- **It replaces what's laid out now.** Change any day by hand after.
- A cycle that repeats takes the set-up's length (two weeks for most). One that runs once keeps its dates, and the set-up repeats over them: week about over a five-week Christmas, say.
- Weekend set-ups land on the weekends whatever weekday the cycle starts on.

Under the tiles: **Nights each cycle**, and **Time within a day** in hours. They're facts about the schedule, worked out from the pattern alone, so a proposal with no dates works the same.

### Who can be chosen

The choices come from the admin's **Whose time it is** field, and are the ones Structure's own form would offer for a new entry filed there:

- **A link to an entry**: you pick with Structure's own picker, given the field's own settings: the kinds it links to (and kinds built on them), the kinds it leaves out, its **Only entries where…** (whatever it tests, such as "picked in a matter's Focus parent field"), and the space of the entry you're on. Each keeps its own colour from Structure.
- **A list**: its typed choices, then its **Values already entered** (read through Structure, such as the people named in a matter's Focus parent and Other parent fields) and its **Records of a type**. Records chosen by a condition of their own can't be listed here yet: the setup says so.
- **Text**: typed, with what's been entered before offered as you type.

Updated in v1.22.6. Before, Tasks read only a list's typed choices and a very simple **Only entries where…**, so a list "from values" or a link with any other condition offered no one.

**With Structure 1.89.1 or later** (Tasks v1.22.8), Structure works the choices out itself (`getFieldChoices`), with the same code that checks a save, as for a new entry filed under the arrangement. So every choice listed (in the colours, the legend and **Each in turn**) is one Structure would accept, whatever the field's conditions, and records chosen by a condition are listed too.

<Aside type="caution">
**"Picked in" and "parent → field" tests offer no one.** Structure reads those parts of an **Only entries where…** as blank when it works out a new entry's choices, in its own picker too. To offer the people named on the entry an arrangement is filed under (a matter's **Focus parent** and **Other parent**, say), make **Who** a list whose options come from **Values already entered** on those fields instead.
</Aside>

## Holidays and special occasions

Holidays and special occasions are **arrangements of their own**, so one set can be used by several regular arrangements: the current one and a proposal. What an arrangement is comes from its type field (as the admin matched each choice). Added in v1.22.2.

### Special occasions

An arrangement whose type means **Special occasions** lists its occasions instead of a cycle. Each one has:

- **When, each year**: a date (25 December), a day around Easter (Good Friday, Easter Saturday, Sunday or Monday), or a weekday of a month (the second Sunday in May, the last Monday in May).
- **From and to**: a time on a day around it, such as the day before at 4:00 pm to the day at 2:00 pm.
- **Who has it**: one person; **each in turn, by year**, said as who has it first and in which year; or **whoever doesn't have the time** just then, which suits a few hours on a birthday.

### Holidays

An arrangement whose type means **Holidays** works like a regular one (v1.22.10): the same planner, its cycle laid out day by day, and **Change the cycle**. Below the planner, **When it applies** says what happens:

- **During its own dates**: from its **Starts** to its **Ends** (a Christmas proposal, say). No kind of period is needed for this.
- **During each kind of holiday** the admin chose (School holidays, Summer holidays). Those holidays are entries of those kinds in Structure, with their Starts and Ends, entered once and shared.

For each, choose one:

- **Its days as laid out above** (the starting choice for its own dates): the planner's pattern, changed by hand however you like.
- **The regular pattern carries on** (the starting choice for a kind of holiday).
- **Split in half**, changing hands at a time in the middle; who has the first half can take turns by year.
- **Take turns every few days**: a week each, a fortnight each, or a few days each, from the holiday's start.
- **One person throughout**, or each in turn by year.

**As usual.** When you add a changeover or a block to a holidays arrangement's planner, tick **As usual** to leave that time to the regular schedule. A regular Saturday can carry on through the holidays this way, whatever the regular schedule says that week. Time before a holiday's first changeover is as usual too.

**Its days line up with** (shown when any choice is **Its days as laid out above**):
- **The start of each holiday**: the holiday's first day is the planner's first day.
- **The regular schedule**: the planner's days fall in step with the regular cycle, so its week 1 is the regular week 1. Use this with a cycle that repeats and is the same length as the regular one (**A copy of** the regular arrangement is a quick start). A cycle that runs once always starts with each holiday.

**On real dates**, a holidays arrangement is shown on its own: time it leaves to the regular schedule shows as no one's. Open a regular arrangement that uses it to see them together. **Add to calendar** is done from the regular one.

Where holidays overlap, the one that began last decides; a special occasion decides over any holiday.

### What a regular one uses

Under a regular arrangement, **It uses** lists the holiday and special-occasion arrangements: tick the ones it follows. It needs the admin's **What it uses is in** field.

### On real dates

Under a regular arrangement's cycle, **On real dates** shows a month at a time. Each day's tile is washed in the colour of whoever has the night; a dashed line along the top marks a holiday, a solid one a special occasion, and each day's label says which. Where they overlap, a special occasion wins, then a holiday, then the regular cycle.

- **No start date yet?** Choose **Try it from** to see how it would fall. Nothing is saved.
- **Nights each** for the year the month is in: the regular schedule with its holidays. Tick **Count special occasions too** to see them counted in.

## Add to calendar

**Add to calendar**, beside **Change the cycle**, turns a regular arrangement's pattern into real dated entries. Added in v1.22.3.

- **What it makes**: an entry of the kind that sets whose time it is (a Changeover) for each time the time changes hands, filed under the arrangement, each a moment with **Starts** and **Who**, named for who and when ("Alex, 1 March 2027 9:00 am"). The first one says who has the time as the stretch begins.
- **From**: the arrangement's start date, unless you choose another. It needs a start date and a pattern.
- **Until**: a year from then, the end of the arrangement (for one that runs once), or a date you choose. Up to two years at once.
- **Include**: the holidays and special occasions it uses, on unless you switch it off.
- **Running it again** replaces what an earlier run made from the same arrangement, from that date on, except any entry you've changed or written on since: those stay as they are, and nothing is added over them.
- **A kind that has to have more filled in** (another required field) can't be added to this way: the dialog says which field.

The entries are ordinary entries: they show on any calendar that shows their kind, in Structure's lists, and on the arrangement's page, and you can open one to add a note.

## Good to know

- **The pattern lives on the arrangement** (in its data), not as one entry per changeover, so a fortnight is set out once. Real dated entries are made only by **Add to calendar**.
- **Structure 1.82 or later** is needed for the fields Tasks adds. Tasks itself needs Structure 1.88.
- **Your own kinds too.** The choices are the kinds for everyone, the kinds you built for yourself in Structure (marked "your own"; they work for you only, as in Structure), and kinds switched off in Structure (marked "switched off": switch one on there before its entries can be made). A kind you can't find here may be a connection type, or Tasks' own task or category kind, which a schedule can't use.
- **Export and import.** Kinds of schedule are part of **Admin › Tasks › Data**. Starts and Ends are added once an import has finished, never during its practice run.
- **Removing a kind of schedule** leaves its schedules and their entries in Structure.
