A **schedule** says whose time it is, over and over: who has the children which nights, who is on which shift. It is laid out on its **cycle** (a fortnight, a roster week, a summer holiday) rather than on dates, so a proposal can be worked out before anyone knows when it would start. Added in v1.22.0.

An admin sets up each **kind of schedule** once. Each person then lays out their own schedules of that kind on their cycles.

<Aside type="note" title="What is built so far">
v1.22.0 is the admin setup: the kinds of schedule, and the fields Tasks adds to Structure's kinds for them. v1.22.1 is the person's side: [laying schedules out on their cycles](#lay-out-a-schedule) and comparing them. v1.22.2 adds [holidays and special occasions](#holidays-and-special-occasions) and a schedule on real dates. **Add to calendar** comes next: see the [Roadmap](/modules/tasks/roadmap/#schedules-by-their-cycle). Calendars that colour their days, set up before v1.22.0, keep working and are listed under **Calendars**.
</Aside>

## What a schedule is made of

| Piece | A care arrangement | A roster |
|---|---|---|
| **The schedule**: the kind of entry that holds each one | Arrangement | Roster |
| **What sets whose time it is**: a moment (from here, someone else's time) or a block (a start, an end and whose time it is) | A changeover | A shift |
| **Whose time it is**: a link to an entry, or a choice from a list | The person it goes to | The staff member, or Early, Late, Night |
| **Other kinds shown**: a dot on their days | Appointments, school events | Training days |
| **Alternative blocks**: periods that set the usual pattern aside | School holidays, special occasions | Public holidays |

Nothing is decided by a kind's or a field's name. The admin chooses each piece.

## Set up a kind of schedule

In **Admin › Tasks**, open **Views**, then **Schedules**, and choose **New kind of schedule**.

<Steps>

1. **The schedule**

   - **Its name** and **its short name** (chosen once).
   - **The schedule is a**: the kind of entry in Structure that holds each schedule.

2. **What sets whose time it is**

   - **It is set by**: the kind of entry that marks whose time it is. **Add one** makes a new kind for everyone in Structure, filed under the schedule's kind, with a Who field of its own if you ask for one.
   - Tasks adds **Starts** and **Ends** to this kind when you save. An entry with no end is a **moment**: from that time, the time is someone else's until the next one. An entry with an end is a **block**: its time is someone's from start to end. The person chooses each time; the admin doesn't.
   - **Whose time it is**: the field on it that names who: a link to an entry (a person), a choice from a list, or text. **Add a Who field** adds one for you.

3. **Also on the schedule**

   - **Other kinds shown**: pick as many as you like. They show as a dot on their days and never change whose time it is.
   - **Alternative blocks**: switch on, then choose the kinds whose entries mark a period under **Periods come from**, such as School holidays. Tasks adds Starts and Ends to these too, so each period has its start and finish times. Holidays are entered once and every schedule shares them. Make summer holidays a kind of its own if they split differently from shorter breaks.

4. **Arrangements**

   Shown once the schedule's kind is chosen.

   - **Its type is in**: a list field on the schedule's kind, such as Type, with a choice for each sort of arrangement. For each choice, say what it means: **The regular pattern**, **Holidays** or **Special occasions**. Holidays and special occasions are arrangements of their own, so one set can be used by several schedules: the current arrangement and a proposal, say.
   - **What it uses is in**: a link field on the schedule's kind naming the holiday and special-occasion arrangements a regular one uses.

5. **Several schedules**

   - **Compare up to**: how many schedules a person can see side by side. 4 unless you change it.

6. **Save**

   Tasks adds Starts and Ends to the kinds that need them. Anything still to sort out is listed at the top of the setup and in the list's **Needs a look** column.

</Steps>

## The fields Tasks adds

Tasks adds its fields through Structure's **field sources** (Structure 1.82 and later). They are real fields: shown in Structure's forms and on each entry's page, and read by formulas, conditions and exports.

| Field | Kept by | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Starts** | Tasks | When the moment is, or when the block starts |
| **Ends** | Tasks | When the block ends. Empty for a moment |
| **Who** (when you choose **Add a Who field**) | The kind | Whose time it is. Handed to the kind as its own straight away |

- **Starts and Ends are kept by Tasks** while it runs: Structure's type editor shows them under "Fields from other modules", and an admin can move, relabel or switch them off there, but not change what they are. People fill them in as usual.
- **Who is the kind's own field.** Structure locks a field a module keeps, so Tasks adds Who and hands it over at once. Set it up in Structure like any of its fields: which kinds it can link to, and **Only entries where…** to narrow who can be chosen. Inside a matter, the picker already offers only that matter's people.
- **A kind no schedule uses any more** keeps Starts and Ends as its own fields, with every entry's times as they were.

## Colours

Each person (or each value) has one colour on every schedule.

- **A link to an entry** takes that entry's colour. In Structure, open the person's kind, switch on **Custom Colour** on its **Properties** tab, and give each person their colour on their own page. The setup says so if it's off.
- **A choice from a list** gets a calm colour from Tasks, the same one everywhere.

---

## What needs a look

The setup checks itself every time it is read, and says what to do in plain words, without blocking anything:

- **Starts and Ends aren't on a kind yet**: save the schedule to add them.
- **No field says whose time it is**: choose one, or add one.
- **Custom Colour is off** on the kind Who links to.
- **The kind that sets whose time it is can't be filed under the schedule's kind**: allow it on the **Hierarchy** tab of either kind in Structure.
- **A kind or field it uses has gone or changed** in Structure.

Saving is refused only for something that can't work: a kind that isn't there, a Who field that isn't a link, list or text, or the same kind as both the schedule and what sets whose time it is.

## Lay out a schedule

Each person lays out their own schedules. Added in v1.22.1.

### Where

- **Schedules** in the view switcher at the top of every Tasks page, once an admin has set up a kind of schedule. It shows all of the person's arrangements of a kind (with more than one kind, choose which).
- **A tab on the entries arrangements are filed under**, named after the kind of schedule: on a matter's page, say. It shows only the arrangements filed under that entry. The tab is on the kinds of entry the schedule's kind can sit under (its **Hierarchy** tab in Structure).

### The list, and comparing

Every arrangement is listed with its cycle in words and what it is (Regular, Holidays, Special occasions, from its type field). **Open** shows one below the list. **New arrangement** makes another (a proposal, say), filed under the entry you're on; it starts as a two-week cycle with nothing on it yet.

Tick two or more and choose **Compare the ticked ones** to see them side by side, week by week, up to the number the admin chose. A ringed day is one whose night goes to someone else than in the first; arrangements whose cycles are different lengths aren't marked against each other. **Back to one** returns to the arrangement you had open.

### The cycle

**Change the cycle** sets:

- **How long it is**: 1, 2, 3 or 4 weeks, a number of days (a 10-day roster, say), or **Once, from one date to another** (a summer holiday).
- **It starts on**: the weekday its first day falls on. Monday unless you change it.
- **It repeats**: off, it runs once.
- **Its start date**: optional. A proposal can have none until you know when it would start. A start date sets the weekday for you.

Shortening a cycle keeps the pattern: anything past the new last day waits until the cycle is longer again.

### The pattern

The cycle shows as weeks of day tiles. Each tile is washed in the colour of whoever has the time as the day ends (whose night it is), and its bottom line shows both colours on a day the time changes hands.

Choose a day to see what happens on it, and to add:

- **A changeover**: a time, and who the time passes to. It stays theirs until the next changeover. In a cycle that repeats, the time before the first changeover belongs to whoever the last one names (it carries round); in one that runs once, to no one.
- **A block**: a start, an end (the same day, the next day, or a few days later) and whose time it is. A block that ends the same day leaves the night where it was: it shows as a dot in that person's colour and counts as hours. One that runs past midnight changes whose night it is.

**Remove** takes one away. Every change saves straight away.

Under the tiles: **Nights each cycle**, and **Time within a day** in hours. They're facts about the schedule, worked out from the pattern alone, so a proposal with no dates works the same.

### Who can be chosen

The choices come from the admin's **Whose time it is** field:

- **A link to an entry**: your entries of the kinds it links to, in the same space as the entry you're on, narrowed by a simple **Only entries where…** on the field (such as Role is Parent). Each keeps its own colour from Structure.
- **A list**: its choices.
- **Text**: what's already been used.

## Holidays and special occasions

Holidays and special occasions are **arrangements of their own**, so one set can be used by several regular arrangements: the current one and a proposal. What an arrangement is comes from its type field (as the admin matched each choice). Added in v1.22.2.

### Special occasions

An arrangement whose type means **Special occasions** lists its occasions instead of a cycle. Each one has:

- **When, each year**: a date (25 December), a day around Easter (Good Friday, Easter Saturday, Sunday or Monday), or a weekday of a month (the second Sunday in May, the last Monday in May).
- **From and to**: a time on a day around it, such as the day before at 4:00 pm to the day at 2:00 pm.
- **Who has it**: one person; **each in turn, by year**, said as who has it first and in which year; or **whoever doesn't have the time** just then, which suits a few hours on a birthday.

### Holiday rules

An arrangement whose type means **Holidays** has a rule for each kind of period the admin chose (School holidays, Summer holidays). The periods themselves are entries of those kinds in Structure, with their Starts and Ends, entered once and shared. During each one:

- **The regular pattern carries on** (the starting choice).
- **Split in half**, changing hands at a time in the middle; who has the first half can take turns by year.
- **Take turns every few days**: a week each, a fortnight each, or a few days each, from the period's start.
- **One person throughout**, or each in turn by year.

### What a regular one uses

Under a regular arrangement, **It uses** lists the holiday and special-occasion arrangements: tick the ones it follows. It needs the admin's **What it uses is in** field.

### On real dates

Under a regular arrangement's cycle, **On real dates** shows a month at a time. Each day's tile is washed in the colour of whoever has the night; a dashed line along the top marks a holiday, a solid one a special occasion, and each day's label says which. Where they overlap, a special occasion wins, then a holiday, then the regular cycle.

- **No start date yet?** Choose **Try it from** to see how it would fall. Nothing is saved.
- **Nights each** for the year the month is in: the regular schedule with its holidays. Tick **Count special occasions too** to see them counted in.

## Good to know

- **The pattern lives on the arrangement** (in its data), not as one entry per changeover, so a fortnight is set out once. Real dated entries come later with **Add to calendar**.
- **Structure 1.82 or later** is needed for the fields Tasks adds. Tasks itself needs Structure 1.88.
- **Kinds for everyone only.** A schedule's kinds are app-level kinds of entry, so every person can have schedules of them.
- **Export and import.** Kinds of schedule are part of **Admin › Tasks › Data**. Starts and Ends are added once an import has finished, never during its practice run.
- **Removing a kind of schedule** leaves its schedules and their entries in Structure.
