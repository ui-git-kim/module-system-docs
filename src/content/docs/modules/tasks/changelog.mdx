## v1.22.2 — 2026-11-13

**Schedules: holidays, special occasions, and real dates.** See [Holidays and special occasions](/modules/tasks/schedules/#holidays-and-special-occasions).

**Added**
- **Special occasions**, as an arrangement of their own: each a span of time every year around a date, a day around Easter, or a weekday of a month (the second Sunday in May), going to one person, to each in turn by year (said as who has it first), or to whoever doesn't have the time just then.
- **Holiday rules**, as an arrangement of their own: for each kind of period the admin chose, split in half, take turns every few days, one person throughout, or the regular pattern carries on.
- **It uses**: a regular arrangement ticks the holiday and special-occasion arrangements it follows.
- **On real dates**: a month at a time, each day in the colour of whoever has the night, a dashed line for a holiday and a solid one for a special occasion; **Try it from** a date for one with no start date (nothing saved); and the year's nights each, the regular schedule with its holidays, and with special occasions on a tick.
- Compare now sets only regular arrangements side by side.

**For developers**
- New: `tasks.dated.ts` (pure: `occasionDay`, `datedDays`, `nightsOver`, and the reads and checks for occasions and holiday rules), `holderFinder` in `tasks.cycle.ts`, the routes `GET …/arrangements/:id/dated` and `PUT …/occasions`, `…/holidays` and `…/uses`, and the actions `tasks.arrangement.occasionsChanged`, `holidaysChanged` and `usesChanged`. Occasions and rules live in an arrangement's data under `tasks.occasions` and `tasks.holidays`; dates are worked out on every read and never stored.

**Upgrade notes**
- Run `npx tasks update`. No schema change.

Verified: `check:templates`, `conformance:check`, `type-check` and the build pass; 386 runtime tests pass (7 new: occasions' days each year, the cycle on dates, holiday rules taking turns by year and week about, occasions over everything and counted apart, the checks, and the service). Rendered into a copy of an app on starter v2.16.8 with Structure v1.88.3: both halves type-check with no errors, and the styling audit passes on all 79 Tasks files. Not yet done: a click-through in the running app.

## v1.22.1 — 2026-11-13

**Schedules, by their cycle: the person's side.** Each person lays their schedules out on their cycles, and compares them. See [Lay out a schedule](/modules/tasks/schedules/#lay-out-a-schedule).

**Added**
- **Schedules** in the view switcher (once an admin has set up a kind of schedule), and **a tab for each kind of schedule** on the entries its arrangements are filed under, such as a matter. Both show the same panel.
- **The arrangements list**, with each one's cycle in words and what it is. It doubles as the way to compare: tick some and see them side by side, week by week, up to the admin's number, with a ringed day where the night goes to someone else than in the first.
- **New arrangement**: a two-week cycle to lay out, filed under the entry you're on, its type set if you say what it is.
- **The cycle**: 1 to 4 weeks, a number of days, or once from one date to another; the weekday it starts on (Monday unless changed); whether it repeats; and a start date only when there is one.
- **The pattern, drawn on the cycle**: day tiles washed in the colour of whoever has the night, a line showing both colours on a day the time changes hands. Choose a day to add a changeover (a time, and who it passes to) or a block (a start, an end, whose time it is), or to remove one.
- **Nights each cycle**, and time within a day in hours, worked out from the pattern alone, so a proposal with no dates works the same.

**For developers**
- New: `tasks.cycle.ts` (pure: `readCycle`, `cycleProblem`, `readPattern`, `patternProblem`, `workOut`), `tasks.arrangements.ts`, the routes `GET /schedules`, `GET` and `POST /schedules/:setupId/arrangements` and `PUT …/arrangements/:id/cycle` and `…/pattern`, the actions `tasks.arrangement.created`, `cycleChanged` and `patternChanged`, `components/Arrangements.tsx`, `pages/SchedulesPage.tsx` and `registrations/schedules.registration.tsx` (tab ids `tasks-schedule-<setupId>`).
- An arrangement's cycle and pattern live in its data under `tasks.cycle` and `tasks.pattern`; whose each day is is worked out on every read and never stored.
- `schedules` joins the view list, so a Tasks section of sidebar links can include it.

**Upgrade notes**
- Run `npx tasks update`. No schema change.

Verified: `check:templates`, `conformance:check`, `type-check` and the build pass; 379 runtime tests pass (10 new for the cycle sums and a person's arrangements). Rendered into a copy of an app on starter v2.16.8 with Structure v1.88.3: both halves type-check with no errors, and the styling audit passes on all 79 Tasks files. ## v1.22.0 — 2026-11-13

**Schedules, by their cycle: the admin setup.** A schedule says whose time it is, over and over, and is now laid out on its cycle (a fortnight, a roster week) rather than on dates. This first release is the admin side: each kind of schedule is set up once. Laying a schedule out on its cycle, comparing, holidays and special occasions, and Add to calendar follow. See [Setting Up Schedules](/modules/tasks/schedules/).

**Added**
- **Admin › Tasks › Views › Schedules** lists the kinds of schedule (a care arrangement, a roster), each set up in one panel of settings: the kind of entry that holds each schedule, the kind that sets whose time it is, the field that says who, other kinds shown on a schedule (pick several), alternative blocks and the kinds of period they follow (pick several), what each choice of an arrangement's own type field means (the regular pattern, holidays or special occasions), the field naming the arrangements one uses, and how many schedules can be compared (4 unless changed).
- **Tasks adds Starts and Ends** to the kind that sets whose time it is and to each kind of period, as real Structure fields. An entry with no end is a moment (from then on, the time is someone else's); with an end it is a block. The person chooses each time.
- **Add one** makes a new kind for everyone in Structure that sets whose time it is, filed under the schedule's kind, with a Who field if asked for.
- **Add a Who field** adds one to a kind and hands it over as the kind's own, so you set up who can be chosen in Structure.
- **What needs a look**, in plain words: fields not added yet, no Who field, Custom Colour off on the kind Who links to, a kind that can't be filed under the schedule's kind, a kind or field that has gone.

**Changed**
- **Views › Schedules** used to list calendars that colour their days. Those are still listed under **Calendars** and work as before.
- **Kinds of schedule** are part of the configuration export and import (Admin › Tasks › Data).

**For developers**
- Tasks now needs **Structure ^1.88.0** (was ^1.77.0), and is a Structure **field source** (`structureService.fields`, Structure 1.82): `tasks.starts` and `tasks.ends` on the kinds a schedule setup uses; a kind no setup uses gets them as its own fields (`releaseTypeFields` with `keep`).
- New: `tasks.schedules.ts`, the routes `GET /admin/schedules`, `PUT` and `DELETE /admin/schedules/:id`, `POST /admin/schedules/kinds` and `POST /admin/schedules/who-field`, and the actions `tasks.schedule-setup.created`, `updated` and `deleted`, `tasks.schedule-kind.created` and `tasks.schedule-who.added`. Setups live in `tasks_config.settings.schedules`; no schema change.
- The screen uses the starter's `combobox` (added to the module's shadcn list).

**Upgrade notes**
- Update Structure to 1.88 or later first, then run `npx tasks update`. No schema change.

Verified: `check:templates`, `conformance:check`, `type-check` and the build pass; 369 runtime tests pass (8 new for kinds of schedule and 1 for their export and import, against a test Structure that now has field sources and app-level kind creation). Rendered into a fresh copy of an app on starter v2.16.8 with Structure v1.88.3: both halves type-check with no errors, and the styling audit passes on all 76 Tasks files. ## v1.21.2 — 2026-11-13

**Admin › Tasks on the starter's shared pieces.** The last of the drift the rules check found: Admin › Tasks now uses the same list, settings and import and export pieces as every other admin page. What each setting does is unchanged.

**Changed**
- **Every list in Admin › Tasks is the starter's table**: entry types, stage templates, timelines, calendars, schedules, flows and Today's rules. Each has a search box, the add button in its toolbar, its actions on each row, and the table's own "Are you sure?" before anything is removed. Entry types can be filtered by what they are used for, and the kinds Tasks uses come first. Clicking a kind of entry opens its panel; clicking a calendar in Flows opens its flows. Today's rules keep their order, because they are read from the top.
- **Every setting is the starter's settings row**: the sidebar choice (the views with their own link are a list of switches), how many Now, Next, Later shows, each kind's Tasks setup, Try it, the stage editor's move rules switch and the calendar editor's nineteen on/off choices. A switch is now announced by its row's title and described by its line underneath.
- **The Data tab is the starter's configuration export and import.** **Export** downloads Admin › Tasks as a file: stage templates, entry types, views and the app-wide settings (the sidebar, quick capture, Now, Next, Later and Today's rules). **Import** checks the file first and shows, item by item, what would be added, changed or removed before anything is written, with **Add new only**, **Merge** and **Replace**; a real import asks for the emailed code. Each part is checked the way its screen checks it. Something this app doesn't have yet (a kind of entry, a field a view uses, a rule that tests one) comes in with a note in the review, and shows "Needs a look" until it's set right. Replace never removes Tasks' own type. Nothing in the file names a person.

**Removed**
- **The old Data tab's export and import of your own settings and check-ins.** Each person's own Tasks settings, check-ins and stage templates already leave with their account data download, as before. There is no import of check-ins any more: the starter keeps import for an admin's configuration, never a person's own records.

**For developers**
- New: `tasks.config-transfer.ts` (the ConfigTransfer definition, sections `stageTemplates`, `entryTypes`, `views` and `settings`), mounted at `/api/v1/tasks/admin/config` with step-up, and the `tasks.config.imported` action (`userId`, `strategy`, `sections`).
- Removed: `GET /api/v1/tasks/export`, `POST /api/v1/tasks/import`, `exportUserData` / `importUserData`, the export envelope types, `useExportTasksData` / `useImportTasksData`, and the `tasks.data.exported` / `tasks.data.imported` actions.
- Tests run the definition through a verbatim copy of the starter's engine in `tests/host-stubs`, as Structure's do.

**Upgrade notes**
- Run `npx tasks update`. No schema change. Needs starter v2.16.0 or later for ConfigTransfer (Tasks already needs v2.16.3).
- A file from the old Data tab can't be imported: it held one person's settings and check-ins, not the configuration.

Verified: `check:templates`, `conformance:check`, `type-check` and the build pass; 361 runtime tests pass (12 new: the export's portable form, a round trip that changes nothing, a practice run that writes nothing, a whole configuration brought into an empty app, Replace, a template's new version, a refused template, notes for what isn't here, and Today's rules). Rendered into a copy of an app on starter v2.16.4 with Structure v1.77.1: the frontend's only error is the one in Structure's own file in that app, the styling audit passes on all 75 Tasks files, and the backend's only errors are the `settings` column that copy's database client doesn't have yet. ## v1.21.0 — 2026-11-13

**Today's rules.** Release SR1 of the [Suggest and Capacity Rules Plan](/modules/tasks/rules-plan/). See [Setting Up Today's Rules](/modules/tasks/today-rules/).

**Added**
- **Admin › Tasks › Suggest & capacity**, a new tab: Today's rules in plain words, **Change the rules** in Structure's own rule builder, and **Put back the starting rules**.
- **Rules on The day:** test the kind of day, the check-in's feeling, whether someone checked in, the day of the week and the time of day; then choose which sizes, only essentials, the order, how many at a time (one to five), one from Later, and categories or kinds of entry to prefer or leave out.
- **Rules on Each task:** test a task's size, essential, days until it's due, category, Now / Next / Later, kind of entry and **any field of its kind** (an Energy field, say); then suggest it, leave it out, bring it forward, put it after the others, or offer it from Later.
- **How rules combine:** every rule that holds applies, and for each setting the highest one wins; leave it out beats suggest it.
- **Try it:** describe a day and see each setting, the rule that set it, and what Today would suggest from your own tasks.
- **The starting rules** are what Today has always done, so nothing changes until an admin changes them.

**Changed**
- **Today's "Show me the next three"** follows how many at a time a rule chose ("Show me the next two").

**For developers**
- Needs **Structure 1.88.0** or later to change the rules (its `structure.rule-builder` slot and `structureService.evaluateRules`). Tasks' dependency stays `^1.77.0`: on an older Structure the rules can't be changed and Today follows the starting rules.

**Upgrade notes**
