**Now, Next, Later** is a calm way to see your tasks: what you're on now, what is next, and what can wait. Each part shows only its first few, and when one is done or moved, the next one comes up. Added in v1.20.0.

It came from a suggestion the owner found for people who get overloaded easily: in place of a Gantt chart, where one missed milestone makes the whole thing feel broken, keep three short lists and keep Now small.

---

## What each part means

| Part | Means | Today |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Now** | What you're on. | Suggested first, so it stays in view. |
| **Next** | Soon: today, but not this minute. | Suggested, sized to the kind of day. |
| **Later** | It can wait. It doesn't have to be done today. | Never suggested. |

Waiting, finished and no-longer-needed tasks aren't on the page.

---

## Using the page

Open **Now, Next, Later** from the switcher at the top of any Tasks page.

- **Each part shows its first few** (three unless an admin chose otherwise), in the order they arrived.
- **Done** ticks a task off. It goes, and the next one in that part comes up.
- **Move a task** with the buttons beside it: from Later to **Next**, from Next to **Now** or **Later**, and from Now back to **Next**.
- **Now never takes more than it shows from here.** When Now is full, the page says so and offers no Now button: finish one, or move one back to Next, to bring another in.
- **There are more** links to the Board when a part has more than it shows. It never says how many.
- **Capture** at the top adds a task where your kind of task starts: Later, in a fresh app.

<Aside type="tip" title="Later is not a backlog to clear">
Nothing in Later is overdue or waiting on you. It is there so it isn't in your head. Bring something up to Next whenever you like, or leave it be.
</Aside>

## Later, the stage meaning

Every stage Tasks uses has a meaning. Since v1.20.0 there are six: **Later**, Not started, Doing, Waiting, Done and No longer needed. Later means "not for today": Today never suggests from it, but All tasks, the Board and Now, Next, Later show it.

On Today, when nothing can be suggested but something waits in Later, Today says so calmly and links to Now, Next, Later.

---

## A fresh app

In an app that installs Tasks from v1.20.0, Tasks' own task type starts with **Later, Next, Now, Done** and **No longer needed**. Something captured starts in **Later**, since it doesn't have to be done today. Giving it a size doesn't move it: a size isn't a promise to do it soon.

## An app that already has Tasks

Its stages stay as they are (Inbox, To do, Doing and Done, or whatever an admin set). To switch a kind of entry to Now, Next, Later:

<Steps>
1. In **Admin › Tasks › Stage templates**, start a template from **Now, Next, Later** and save it.
2. Choose **Use for…** and tick the kind of entry.
3. Say where each old stage goes (for example Inbox to Later, To do to Next, Doing to Now). Tasks moves the entries for you, and nothing is lost.
</Steps>

A kind keeps any stages of its own you choose to keep; Now, Next, Later needs only one stage that means Later for the Later part to appear.

---

## The admin's setting

In **Admin › Tasks › Views › Now, Next, Later**, **How many each part shows at once** is one to five, three unless changed. It applies to everyone.

---

## Good to know

- **It reads every kind of entry you use for tasks**, in the space you have open, like Today.
- **A kind with no Later stage** simply has nothing in Later; the Later part shows only when at least one of your kinds has one.
- **Nothing is counted** anywhere on the page.
- **What's next for it** (on the [Roadmap](/modules/tasks/roadmap/)): a Parking lot for things not sorted yet, designed with trauma-informed care; Suggest and capacity rules, where an admin could let Today bring something up from Later on a full-energy day; and help for when someone is in a hyperfocus.
