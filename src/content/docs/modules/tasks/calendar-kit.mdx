The calendar kit is a set of calendar pieces that Tasks, and any other module, can put on a page: a month, a week or a day of hours, an agenda list, a small month for picking a day, the chosen day as a card, and one calendar that puts them together. They're built only from the starter's shadcn components (Card, Button, Toggle Group, Popover, Item and shadcn's own Calendar), so they take the app's theme like every other screen. Added in v1.22.22.

<Aside type="note">
Tasks' calendar (the one an admin sets up under **Views › Calendars**) is drawn with the kit since v1.22.23, and a schedule's **On real dates** since v1.22.24. The main Tasks page moves onto it in a later release.
</Aside>

## Why a kit

The owner asked for calendar components first, to be applied where they're needed: "we can have a few different calendar components that can be slotted in", kept calm with the shadcn components so everything "can be styled from a app level and be consistent". Because every piece is a shadcn component, a theme change (corners, colours, spacing) changes the calendar with the rest of the app.

## The pieces

| Piece | What it shows | Slot id |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| **Event calendar** | The pieces together: the calendar's card (its toolbar right above the days: Earlier, Today, Later, and Month, Week, Day or Agenda; a key under them), with a sidebar on its left of a small month, the page's own sections and the chosen day's card (under it where it's narrow, without the small month) | `tasks.event-calendar` |
| **Month** | Whole weeks; each day with its events as titles, a few at a time, the rest under **More**, or as dots (the whole day one button, a small mark after a few) | `tasks.month-calendar` |
| **Week or day of hours** | Hours down the side, a column per day; events at their times, side by side where they overlap, and a strip for events across whole days | `tasks.time-calendar` |
| **Agenda** | Only the days with something on them, in order, each event a row | `tasks.agenda-calendar` |
| **Small month** | shadcn's own Calendar for picking a day, with a small dot on days that have something | `tasks.mini-calendar` |
| **Day card** | The chosen day: its name, what the page says about it, and its events, the chosen one opened | `tasks.day-card` |

## Kept calm

- **No counts.** A day with more than fits says **More**, never a number. Nothing says how many events a day or a week has.
- **Nothing reads as late.** No red, no "overdue"; a day in the past is just a day.
- **Today** is the theme's own primary mark on its date, nothing louder.
- **Colour is never the only sign.** Every event shows its title, every colour dot has a border, and each day's button says its date, its name and its events in words for a screen reader.
- **The day's own look.** A day can be washed in a colour (whose day it is), with weekdays at 80% of a weekend as on Tasks' other calendars, a line along its bottom (two colours where the day changes), and a name (a marked day, a holiday). An event can show as an outline instead of a chip: a dashed line along the top of each day it covers, as a period does.
- **The keyboard.** On the month, the arrow keys move the chosen day; the small month is shadcn's Calendar, keyboard and all.

## Using a piece

A page reads its events for the days the view shows and hands them over, and the page keeps the chosen day. Inside Tasks, the kit works out which days those are (`visibleRange`); another module gets only the kit's types from the Tasks barrel, so it works out the days itself (a month's whole weeks, the week, the day, or 30 days from the date for the agenda).

An event is:

```ts
{
  id: 'unique-on-the-calendar',
  title: 'Hearing',
  start: '2026-11-20T10:00',   // or a whole day: '2026-11-20'
  end: '2026-11-20T11:30',     // optional; 00:00 ends as that day begins
  color: '#4F7DD9',            // optional, from the data (a person's, a type's)
  look: 'chip',                // or 'outline' for a period across days
  detail: 'Court 3',           // optional line under the title
  data: { nodeId: 'abc' },     // optional: anything the page wants back when it's chosen
}
```

Dates and times are wall-clock values with no zone, as Structure keeps them, and every sum is whole-day arithmetic, so a daylight-saving change never moves a day.

### From another module

Each piece is a slot in the starter's component registry. Render one with `EmbeddedSlot`, which shows its fallback where Tasks isn't installed, and type its props with the types the Tasks barrel exports:

```tsx
import { EmbeddedSlot } from '@/components/EmbeddedSlot';
import type { EventCalendarProps } from '@/features/tasks';

<EmbeddedSlot
  id="tasks.event-calendar"
  props={{ events, date, onDateChange: setDate, today } satisfies EventCalendarProps<unknown>}
  fallback={null}
/>
```

Each piece loads only where it's shown.

### Inside Tasks

Tasks' own pages import the pieces from `components/calendar`. The month, the hours and the agenda each work on their own, and the event calendar takes:

- `view` and `onViewChange` to keep the view on the page (it keeps its own otherwise), and `views` to offer fewer than all four;
- `weekStart` (`'monday'` unless `'sunday'`), `selectedEvent` and `onSelectEvent` for the chosen event, `agendaDays` (how many days the agenda lists, 30 unless set), and `label` (what a screen reader calls it, "Calendar" unless set);
- `days`, a look per date (`color`, `bar`, `note`, `words` for a screen reader, and `top`: a dashed line along the top for a stretch of dates, a solid one for a day of its own);
- for the month: `display` (`'titles'` unless `'dots'`), `perDay`, `month` (a month other than the chosen day's, so choosing a day of the next month on this one's grid keeps this one), `outsideDays` (`'blank'` leaves the days of the months either side empty, for a month read on its own), and `weekEnd` (a column at the end of each week: Tasks' calendar puts the nights each that week there);
- `renderEvent` for what shows under the chosen event in the day's card, or `eventRow` to draw every event the page's own way (Tasks' calendar uses its entry cards, with **Repeat** and **Log this one**); `dayInfo` for what the page says about the day, `dayActions` for the day's own buttons (**Add**), `dayFooter` for more after the day's events, and `dayEmpty` for what a day with nothing on it says;
- `header` for a page's own toolbar in the card (`CalendarToolbar` takes any choices), or `toolbar={false}` for none; `body` for what the card shows in place of the view (a way to show the kit doesn't have); `footer` under the days (the key);
- the sidebar (v1.22.26): `mini` (the small month, on unless switched off), `sidebar` (the page's own sections, after the small month), and `side` to leave out the day's card, whose title is the theme's quieter card title;
- `toolbarExtra` for the page's own controls in the kit's toolbar, `now` (the device's time unless given; `null` for no line), and `loading`.

## Style hooks

Every kind of day square carries a `data-slot`, so a theme or a Style snippet can reach it without touching Tasks' code (v1.22.46). They are hand-built squares, not shadcn pieces, so they have no slot of their own otherwise.

| Hook | Where |
|---|---|
| `tasks-day-tile` | A day on the month, in either look (the dots look's button, or the titles look's cell) |
| `tasks-all-day-tile` | A day's All day row on the week or the day |
| `tasks-compare-tile` | A day of one row on side by side |
| `tasks-cycle-tile` | A day of a schedule's planner (a button where a day can be chosen) |
| `tasks-glance-tile` | A day on at a glance |
| `tasks-fortnight-tile` | A day on the schedule card on an entry's page |

Two wrappers carry one too: `tasks-event-calendar` around the event calendar (its card and sidebar), and `tasks-calendar` around Tasks' own calendar page, tab and widget.

A day square also says what state it's in: `data-selected` (the chosen day), `data-outside` (a day of the month before or after) and, on at a glance and the fortnight card, `data-today`. For example, `[data-slot="tasks-day-tile"][data-selected]` is the chosen day on the month. Keep a snippet to borders, radius and spacing: the wash and the line along the bottom carry whose day it is, and are set from the data.
