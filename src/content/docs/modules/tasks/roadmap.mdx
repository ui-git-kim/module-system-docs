This page tracks known issues, planned work, and completed milestones for the Tasks module. Items move from **Planned** down to **Completed** as they ship. Each entry includes enough context that a future Claude Code session can pick it up without extra explanation.

<Aside type="note" title="Everything planned keeps the no-guilt rules">
Whatever ships next has to feel achievable: no overdue badges or red for anything late, no "X remaining" counts, no percentages, no streaks, and none of the words "overdue", "priority", "must" or "failed" in the interface.
</Aside>

## Known Issues

### Not yet clicked through in a real app
- **Description:** v1.1.2 has been installed with `npx tasks init` into a starter app and type-checked there with no Tasks errors (2026-10-28), then updated in place to v1.1.4 with `npx tasks update`; both halves type-check clean, the styling audit passes, and the update was reviewed against that app's own registries and components. Before that it was type-checked inside a host built from a real app's sources, passed the starter's styling audit, and its backend runs against an in-memory database in `npm test`. What hasn't been done yet is a walk through each screen in the running app.
- **Severity:** Low
- **Next step:** in the installed app, walk Today (including the check-in), Done, All tasks, Board, Timelines (My tasks, and a timeline an admin sets up, as a page and as an entry's tab), the node Tasks tab, settings, Admin › Tasks (entry types, stage templates and an opt-in update, and the timeline editor), the dashboard widget, quick capture, the Energy widget on a person's own profile page, a timeline's "coming up" widget on an entry's page, a calendar (the month, its legend, a day's entries, the list, and the month as a widget), repeats (make an entry repeat from its day, pause it during a period, skip a day and put it back, change it from a day on, change just one day), day colours (the bar and its legend, a day of change, the day in words, setting a day by hand and clearing it), adding from a day (the short form, "Add and open", and a kind that is sent to its own form), the log (Log this one, Same as last time, and the log under its entry), and the new day tile (its wash and line, a period's dashed outline, the buttons that set a day, a dot in a linked entry's colour). v1.2.1 was updated into the owner's legal app (veradai) with `npx tasks update` on 2026-10-28; it hasn't been clicked through there either, and see the next issue first.

### Journeys haven't been clicked through in a running app
- **Description:** Journeys (v1.22.48) are tested on the server (`tests/journeys.test.ts`: dates and their words, the checks on save, starting and running, anchor moves, move steps, the three ways to unfold, if-steps and follow-ups, triggers, prompts, calendars) and type-checked in a fresh copy of the owner's app, with the styling audit passing. Nobody has walked the screens yet: Admin › Tasks › Schedules & journeys › Journeys and Prompts, Start a journey (from the header, the Journeys tab and a calendar's Not scheduled yet), the Journeys tab and its notes, the prompt dialog, and a journey on a calendar. Today, the Board and the lists hiding the steps a journey isn't showing yet use the same check the tests cover, but have no end-to-end test of their own.
- **Severity:** Low
- **Next step:** set up the [Journeys Plan](/modules/tasks/journeys-plan/#walkthroughs) walkthroughs in the app and click through each.

### A kind that needs more than a title and a date is added without its date
- **Description:** Since v1.7.0 an entry can be added from a calendar's day. Where the kind has to have more than the short form holds, Tasks links to that kind's own form in Structure, but Structure's form can't be handed the chosen day, and on a calendar that belongs to one entry it can't be told where the new entry belongs (so there the link isn't offered, and the form says to add it from the entry's page).
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Fixed in v1.22.38:** Structure 1.89.12 answered the 2026-11-11 request with pre-filled create links (`createLinkHref`). The form now opens with the day, where it's filed and the calendar's entry filled in, and **Log this one** opens its form before anything is made. A changeover's note opens the changeover's own entry instead (v1.22.39: it is the schedule's, not a new one).

### An entry's date and details aren't changed from the calendar
- **Description:** Each entry on a calendar opens its own page, where its date, title and other fields are changed. From the calendar itself you can add an entry, set how it repeats, change or skip one of a repeat's days, and set a day's colour by hand.
- **Severity:** Low
- **Planned fix:** moving an entry to another day from the calendar (the plan's "dragging", which will also have a keyboard way), in a later release.

### A repeat follows only the periods its own calendar shows
- **Description:** A rule names kinds of period ("pause during school holidays"). Which entries count comes from the calendar it is read on, so the same entry on a second calendar that doesn't show that kind isn't paused there. This is deliberate (a matter's calendar should follow that matter's periods), and is listed so it isn't a surprise.
- **Severity:** Low
- **Planned fix:** none planned. Show the period kind on each calendar that should follow it.

### A new timeline's tab shows on an open entry once you move on
- **Description:** Structure works out an entry's tabs once per entry and doesn't watch for tabs added later. Tasks registers a tab for each timeline after sign-in (and remembers them in the browser for next time), so a timeline an admin has just set up shows its tab when you open another entry or reload, not on the entry already on screen. A link straight to that tab can open on the first tab the first time. See [Needs from Structure](#needs-from-structure).
- **Severity:** Low

### The "Add task" header button hasn't been tried in a running app
- **Description:** Structure draws module actions in an entry page's header since its v1.66, so Tasks' **Add task** button now shows there, wherever a kind of task can be filed under the entry (since v1.22.61, further down too). v1.3.2 fixed the address it opens: Structure addresses an entry by its slug now, and the old address lost the request to focus the add field. It has been type-checked, not clicked.
- **Severity:** Low

### An entry outside every space can't be changed
- **Description:** In an app with an enforced space (Structure's primary scope), an entry of a kind the space keeps has to sit inside one. Structure leaves an entry of such a kind that sits outside every space readable but read-only to everyone except the system, with a message like "… isn't in a Matter yet. Assign it to one before editing it." Tasks can't size, move or finish a task in that state. New tasks aren't affected: a kind kept in a space can't be set to capture at the top level, and since v1.3.2 Tasks' own type captures into the open space where a space keeps it. A task captured at the top level before that, or made there by an import, needs filing into a space first.
- **Severity:** Low
- **Workaround:** open the entry in Structure and assign it to a space.

### A widget can show without being placed
- **Description:** On a type that still uses Structure's older page with an Overview tab, Structure shows every widget registered for the type. So the Energy widget appears there on a person's own profile even where an admin hasn't placed it (still on that person's own profile only), and a timeline's widget appears on its entries once it is switched on for the timeline.
- **Severity:** Low
- **Workaround:** switch it off for the type on the type's **Integrations** tab in Structure.

### Dates follow the device, not the person's saved time zone
- **Description:** Tasks works out "today" from the browser and formats dates the Australian way. The starter (v2.16.3) keeps each person's language and time zone and has date helpers that follow them. Since v1.4.1 timelines and calendars convert times to the person's saved time zone, but Today, Done and the check-in still follow the device, and dates are still worded the Australian way. So Tasks' day can differ from the rest of the app for someone whose saved zone isn't their device's.
- **Severity:** Low
- **Planned fix:** move the rest onto the starter's helpers with the calendar work.

### An admin can change "Done at" by hand in Structure's forms
- **Description:** Tasks marks its **Done at** and **Not now until** fields as system-only. Since Structure v1.47.2 that tier includes admins, so an admin can change those two values on an entry's form without Tasks' checks. Other people still can't.
- **Severity:** Low

### The module's build checks don't run on a push
- **Description:** From the cleanup and security audit (2026-11-16). GitHub has the scaffold's checks (template escaping, the contract, type-check, the test suite), but in a cloned module they run only by hand or when the repository variable `CI_AUTO_RUN` is `true`, and it isn't set: every push shows them as skipped. They also don't run the build or `npm audit`.
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Fixed on 2026-11-16 (the owner: "Turn checks on"):** the repository variable `CI_AUTO_RUN` is `true`, so the scaffold's checks run on every push (both Node 22 and 24), and the module's own `.github/workflows/ci.yml` builds the module and runs `npm audit --audit-level=high`, with its actions pinned to commits. The first run passed. Left for the module starter: its `scaffold-ci.yml` refers to `actions/checkout@v4` and `actions/setup-node@v4` by tag, which a scan flags (a tag can be moved); pinning them is the starter's change to make.

### An admin's schedule save rewrites everyone's entries in one go, with no second check
- **Description:** From the audit. Saving a kind of schedule in Admin › Tasks can fill the worked-out fields of up to 2,000 entries owned by anyone (`fillWorkedFields`, as the system) and trim every entry's Starts and Ends to their day (`trimToDays`, which can't be undone), inside the one request, behind the admin check alone.
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Fixed in v1.22.40:** saving and removing a kind of schedule ask for the starter's step-up (`requireStepUp`), and each fill and trim records how many entries it changed with `audit: true`. Still to decide: step-up on applying a stage template, and running the fill as a background job.

### An impossible date on a schedule's dates can trip the server
- **Description:** From the audit. A schedule's On real dates, its references and Add to calendar check a date's shape only, so "2026-13-01" gets through: the server can answer with an error page (a date a year on can't be worked out) or Add to calendar can run over a stretch nobody chose. The screens send real dates, so this needs a hand-made request.
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Fixed in v1.22.40:** every schedule date is checked to exist (`isRealDate`, shared with the calendar's routes), and times have to read like 15:00.

### Some screens can show a raw check message
- **Description:** From the audit. Four of the six places that check what a screen sent (entry types, stage templates, timelines and calendars, tasks and categories) pass the checker's own words through where a field has no message of its own, such as `Too big: expected string to have <=255 characters`. The schedule screens already turn them into plain words (`plainIssues`).
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Fixed in v1.22.42:** one shared check (`parseInput` in `my-feature.input.ts`) words every refusal plainly, and a failure that isn't a refusal says "something unexpected went wrong" with its detail in the server log.

### A failure can hide behind an old message or nothing at all
- **Description:** From the audit. Saving a kind of schedule retries any refused field set without the Nights each group and then says "Nights each need Structure 1.89.6", although Tasks needs Structure 1.89.12. The done-at backstop skips silently when the kinds can't be read; the configuration export silently leaves out a kind it can't read; and a few entry reads on calendars treat a database failure as "an entry that can't be opened".
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Fixed:** the old retry went in v1.22.40; since v1.22.42 the backstop and the start-up registry log a failure, the config transfer stops on one, and a calendar's entry reads re-throw anything that isn't a refusal.

### Seeding can't run if someone has a personal "task" or "task-category" type
- **Description:** Tasks creates its category type (and, in a fresh app, its `task` type) the first time the backend starts. Since v1.3.0 it moves to a free slug when an app-level type already holds the name, but it can't see a type another person owns. Structure refuses an app-level type whose slug someone already uses for a personal type, so the setup is refused and retried a minute later, with a "Tasks could not set up its task types" message meanwhile.
- **Severity:** Low
- **Workaround:** rename the clashing personal type in Structure.
- **Possible fix:** when Structure refuses the slug, try the next free one (`tasks-category`, `tasks-task`).

### The generated routes file holds three control characters
- **Fixed in v1.22.83:** the pattern keeps its escapes, and a test checks that no generated file holds a control character.
- **Description:** found by the standards audit of 2026-11-25. The template for `tasks.routes.ts` writes the route-id check's pattern with single backslashes (`\u0000-\u001f\u007f`), so the build turns them into the real characters (NUL, 0x1F, DEL) and every app gets them in its routes file. The check still works, but git treats the file as binary: in the owner's app a change to it shows only as "Bin 54736 -> 66639 bytes", so its changes can't be read or reviewed, and some editors and tools stumble on the NUL.
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Workaround:** none needed for it to run.
- **Possible fix:** double the backslashes in the template; ask the module starter for `check:templates` to flag a single-backslash `\u` escape (it catches only `${`).

### Stopping calendar sync with "remove" also deletes your own calendar's events
- **Description:** from the cleanup and security audit (2026-11-26). Since v1.22.71 Tasks can bring a person's own calendar events in as copies (calendar sync, CS3), and keeps a row for each copy in the same table as the events it made. **Stop** with "remove the ones Tasks made" (`removeKeptEvents` in `my-feature.calendar-sync.ts`) goes through every row in that table, so it also asks Google or Microsoft to delete each event it had brought in (up to 600): the person's own appointments, in any calendar they can change. Deleting a meeting they organise sends cancellations to everyone invited. The copies' rows go first, so the copies themselves are then left behind in Tasks. The docs promise those events stay; the test of reading in stops without remove, so this path wasn't tested.
- **Severity:** High
- **Workaround:** until it's fixed, stop calendar sync with "leave them" (the default), never "remove".
- **Fixed in v1.22.86:** Stop removes only the events Tasks made (its `entry:` and `journey:` rows), takes the copies out first, and a test reads two calendars in, stops with remove and checks the person's own events are untouched.

### A copied entry shares the original's calendar event
- **Description:** from the audit. Tasks notes the calendar event it made on the entry itself (`tasks.calendar_event`). Structure's **Duplicate**, and Tasks' own "from this day on" copy of a repeat, copy that note with everything else, so the copy takes over the original's event: renaming or re-dating the copy changes the original's event, and finishing the copy deletes it. The original still thinks its event is in step, so Sync now doesn't put it back.
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Fixed in v1.22.86:** a note counts only where Tasks' own row for the entry names the same event (or, for an event made before rows were kept, where no other row does), and Tasks' own copies leave the note behind.

### Choosing "Busy" doesn't reach events already in people's calendars
- **Description:** from the audit. When an admin switches **What an event says** to Busy (for privacy), events Tasks already put in people's calendars keep the task's name until that person presses Sync now or changes the entry. And an event the person moves in their calendar keeps its real name for good, because pulling the move back notes it as "Busy" without changing the event.
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Fixed in v1.22.86:** the person's link notes what their last full sync said (`sentAs`), and the next catch-up after a change syncs in full; pulling a move back keeps what the event says and sends anything that differs.

### One person's journey kinds can be used for another's
- **Description:** from the audit. Journeys keep the list of kinds they work with for 30 seconds (`kindsFor` in `my-feature.journey-runs.ts`), but in one list for everyone, although it includes the first person's own kinds. In an app with more than one person, someone else's Journeys tab can, for those 30 seconds, offer the first person's kinds, refuse their own ("That kind of entry isn't available"), or work their steps out with the first person's stages where both have an own kind of the same name. An app with one person is not affected.
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Fixed in v1.22.87:** the list is kept per person (`journeyKindsFor`), and the next prompt reads it too.

### Applying a stage template doesn't ask an admin to confirm it's them
- **Description:** from the audit. Applying a stage template, or saying where stray stages count, moves everyone's entries off the stages it retires on their next visit, and that can't be undone. Since v1.22.40 an admin action that rewrites many people's entries asks for the starter's step-up (as saving a kind of schedule does); these two don't.
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Fixed in v1.22.87:** both ask for step-up (`stepUpUnlessDryRun` for applying, which a dry run skips), and a test reads the routes to keep it so.

### A few costly actions have no limit on how often they run
- **Description:** from the audit. Bite sized's **Suggest pieces** runs an AI model each time (AI Core asks the module to limit in-process calls); calendar sync's **Sync now**, starting sync, the all-day time and choosing calendars to read each run a full sync with Google or Microsoft; the journeys' next prompt reads every run. Only the catch-ups are limited. A script, or a lost device token, could run up AI costs, or use up the app's Google or Microsoft allowance so everyone's sync pauses.
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Fixed in v1.22.87:** Suggest pieces, ten a minute for one person; a full calendar sync, six in a couple of minutes (`perPersonLimit` in `my-feature.input.ts`, a plain 429 with when to try again); full syncs one at a time for a person; starting again keeps the calendar called Tasks it made. The next prompt reads the kinds from the short-lived per-person list rather than being limited (its screen asks once a visit).

### A step locked to admins can still be changed from its entry
- **Description:** from the audit. A journey can lock a step (its date, skipping it, removing it) so only an admin changes it. Tasks enforces that on the Journeys tab, but the step also follows its entry: changing the entry's date on Structure's own form, moving its stage or deleting it changes the step, and a note's "put back" or "move" rewrites locked dates.
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Next step:** a decision for the owner (see [Decisions needed](#decisions-needed)): should a locked step's date be written back to its entry instead, or is the lock a guide only on Tasks' screens?

#### Journeys: what comes next (after v1.22.48)

Journeys shipped in v1.22.48 ([changelog](/modules/tasks/changelog/#v12248--2026-11-17), [guide](/modules/tasks/journeys/), [plan](/modules/tasks/journeys-plan/)), replacing flows. Decided by the owner on 2026-11-16 ([TASK-008 to TASK-026](/modules/tasks/decisions/#journeys-decided-2026-11-16)). Left for later, none built:

- **For the owner to decide:**
  - **The word for a date that matters.** "Deadline"? The owner is choosing a term that doesn't weigh on an ADHD brain; until then Tasks still says "Due", and nothing was renamed.
  - **A cleared anchor:** steps keep their dates and a note says so (now, as "When an anchor moves" says), or the step waits undated, "When Event date is set" (what the plan's walkthrough expected).
  - **The move step's rule:** it counts as done when its entry reaches the stage or a later one. To be refined once the owner sets real journeys up.
- **How firm a date is**, with the capacity work in the [Suggest and Capacity Rules Plan](/modules/tasks/rules-plan/) (TASK-022 to TASK-024): the dials for size, energy, firmness and essential, dates that show early, and a journey's weight.
- **Reminders and nudges** for if-steps and journeys, once the starter's Notifications can send at a set time, cancel, keep quiet hours and be switched off per person (logged on the starter's [upstream issues](/starter-template/upstream-issues/)).
- **The catch-up may change.** Journeys are worked out again on every visit and from Structure's hooks, with no background jobs. A cancellable, replaceable delayed job, or a schedule that lets the database sleep, would let an if-step act on its day even when nobody comes in.
- **Repeating journeys:** one per changeover, or a yearly cycle.
- **Business days, weekends and public holidays** in offsets, including court "clear days".
- **More branching:** conditions beyond an if-step's "mark a step not needed" (an Else, choosing between two sets of steps).
- **A follow-up that also gets no reply** (a chain of follow-ups).
- **Making a journey from a past run** ("save as a journey"), or from a document such as court orders or a course outline (with AI).
- **Letting AI chat read whole journeys** for its context builder (to work out with AI chat; TASK-021).
- **Journeys knowing about each other**, or about a busy season.
- **Delegation on survival days** (a maybe).
- **Reordering the steps of a journey that's going**, and giving one step more than once (also left from flows).
- **Tests end to end** of Today, the Board and the lists leaving out the steps a journey isn't showing yet, and a click-through of every journeys screen (see Known Issues).

#### Tasks' fields as real Structure fields (the owner, 2026-11-16)

- **What's wanted:** "we need to have a set of structure fields that tasks put into structure, for any node type that is turned into a 'task'." A kind switched on for tasks would get Tasks' fields (size, essential, due date, when it happened, category, not now until, done at, the checklist) as real Structure fields, laid out and used like any other.
- **Where it stands:** Tasks' `tasks.*` values (`FIELDS` in `my-feature.structure.ts`, `TASK_FIELDS` in the frontend's `utils/structure.ts`) are registered in the browser only, through `structureRegistry` for each person's kinds (`applyTaskTypes`). Structure's backend doesn't know them, so it can't check them, and its formulas, conditions, merge fields and the Fields tab's layout can't use them; the Tasks service checks every write itself.
- **How:** Structure's field-source slot (Structure v1.82.0: `fieldSourceRegistry`, `registerTypeFields`, writes with `asSource`), which schedules already use since v1.22.0 for Starts and Ends and a schedule's own fields (`reconcileTiming`, `ensureFieldSource` in `my-feature.structure.ts`). The same pattern would register a task field set on every kind with tasks on, release it with `keep: true` from a kind switched off, and skip fields a kind already has as its own. Things to settle first: the field ids (data keys must stay `tasks.*` so existing values carry over), which fields people can edit (Done at stays Tasks' own), the checklist (Structure has no checklist field), and how it meets the dials of [TASK-022](/modules/tasks/decisions/#task-022--today-suggests-tasks-and-reads-dials), where the admin can map a kind's own field instead.
- Replaces the older note under "Set a type up in Structure, then switch Tasks on" below, which waited for the slot.
- **Built in v1.22.60 (the owner, 2026-11-19: "Tasks switched on", and these to start: "we need to be clever and smart about this"):** Size, Essential, Due, Happened on and Done on, on every kind switched on for tasks, kept as the kind's own when it's switched off. Done on is written by Tasks with each move, so it isn't locked. Category, Not now until and the checklist are still Tasks' own; the owner is collecting what else a task kind needs.

#### Cleanup and security audit (2026-11-26)

Nobody can reach another person's tasks, journeys, prompts, check-ins, decisions or calendar rows: every route takes the person from their session, checks every id, and checks ownership before writing. The faults are in calendar sync (one serious, under Known Issues), a shared cache, missing limits and step-up, and leftovers. Severity: 🔴 loses data, 🟡 fix soon, 🟢 tidiness.

**Suggested flows of events (the owner's idea, 2026-11-11)** — replaced by [journeys](/modules/tasks/journeys/) in v1.22.48 (kept here as history)

  - **The flow is a suggestion, not a rule:** steps can be skipped, repeated, reordered or added, and an event that isn't in the flow is added the usual way. Changing the template later doesn't change flows already started, as with stage templates.

- Help with time spent caring for someone, and with plans that span two homes.

**Connection notifications**
- Let people know when something they're connected to changes.

**Export or print a chronology**
- A clean, printable chronology of events (for example for an appointment, a case or a handover). Now part of the [Timelines plan](/modules/tasks/timelines-plan/#releases) (Later).

**More from Wysli, if they earn their place**
- **Tasks that give energy back:** an optional "This usually lifts me" switch, so a survival day may offer one light lifting task alongside the essentials (a rule in the day-type defaults).
- **One, two or three at a time:** a personal setting; three stays the default, and one at a time helps on overwhelmed days. Still a batch, never a count.
- **Your better times of day:** "When does your energy usually feel best?" (morning, midday, afternoon, evening chips); the My tasks week view lightly tints those hours. From stated preferences only, never from completion rates.
- **"How was that?" after Done:** an optional one tap (lighter than expected / about right / heavier) to help sizes get easier to guess, feeding "Learn from check-in history". Never a score.
- **Mix it up:** when it fits the kind of day, vary categories within the three (Wysli's "balanced" flow, for people who get bored with repetition).
- **Voice capture:** speak a thought into capture (Wysli had the button, never the feature).

**Swipe gestures on the cards**
- Swipe to "break it down", not now, or done.

**Navigation links an admin turns on**
- If the starter adds a "hidden until an admin shows it" option for sidebar links, Tasks could register every view link that way and let Admin › Navigation do all of it, retiring the Sidebar card in Admin › Tasks.

**Storage usage**
- Register a `usageRegistry` provider for Tasks' own tables (tiny next to Structure's own figure, which already counts tasks).

**Toasts**
- Hosts on starter v2.16.2 or later mount a toast area; earlier ones don't, so Tasks still gives feedback inline. The acknowledgements could move there (still quiet).
