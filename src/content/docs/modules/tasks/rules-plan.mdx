This page is the **proposed** plan for the **Suggest and capacity** tab in Admin › Tasks. The owner asked for it on 2026-11-13: "if energy is this, then do that", set up by an admin. Nothing here is built yet. The questions at the end need the owner's answers first.

<Aside type="note" title="Nothing changes until an admin changes it">
The starting rules are exactly what Today does now. An app that never opens the tab behaves as it always has.
</Aside>

---

## Where things stand

Today already follows rules, but they are written in the code:

| Kind of day | What Today suggests now |
|-------------|-------------------------|
| **Survival** | Only essentials, lightest first. |
| **Steady** | Light and medium tasks. |
| **Full energy** | Any size, biggest first. |

In every kind of day, something already in Now comes first, then the nearest due date, then the oldest capture. Three at a time, "Show me the next three" for more, and nothing from Later.

The kind of day comes from the check-in (a feeling, mapped to a kind of day by each person's own settings), or from the person's usual day when they skip it. Another module can already change the rules in code (the `tasks.dayTypeRules` filter).

---

## What the tab does

**Rules an admin can read and change**, each one a short sentence:

> **When** the kind of day is **Survival**
> **then** suggest **only essentials**, **lightest first**, **one at a time**.

> **When** it is **Saturday or Sunday**
> **then** prefer tasks in **Home**.

> **When** the kind of day is **Full energy**
> **then** also offer **one thing from Later**.

### The When side: what a rule can test

| Release | Tests |
|---------|-------|
| First | The kind of day; the feeling and how it felt (from the check-in); whether the person checked in or skipped; the day of the week; the time of day (morning, afternoon, evening, by the person's own clock). |
| Later | Capacity on a scale, if the check-in asks it; what a calendar says about today (a calendar that colours its days, such as "a day with the kids"); whether something is due today; a focus the person has chosen (see Hyperfocus below). |

Tests combine as **all of these** or **any of these**, like Structure's conditions.

### The Then side: what a rule can do

| Release | Actions |
|---------|---------|
| First | Which sizes can be suggested; only essentials; the order (lightest first, biggest first, nearest due date, oldest first); how many at a time (one to five); also offer one thing from Later; prefer a category or a kind of entry; leave out a category or a kind of entry. |
| Later | A quiet line on Today (an admin's own words, checked against the calm-words list); "That's plenty for today" after a chosen amount is done; whether "Show me the next three" is offered. |

### How rules combine

The rules are a list, read from the top. **Every rule that matches applies, and for each setting the highest rule that mentions it wins.** So "Survival: only essentials" and "Weekends: prefer Home" both apply on a survival Saturday, and a rule near the top can override one below it. The last rule is **Always**, which fills in anything no other rule set.

### Try it

Choose a kind of day, a feeling, a weekday and a time. The tab shows which rules match, what each setting comes out as and which rule set it, and the three things Today would suggest from your own tasks. Nothing is saved by trying.

### Starting rules and putting them back

The starting rules are the three in the table above, plus Always. **Put back the starting rules** returns to them after a confirmation.

## The check-in, configured

A second part of the tab, in its own release:

- **Which questions it asks:** the feeling (on now), how it felt (on now), **capacity on a scale** from empty to full (off unless switched on), and **one thing that would make today feel good** (off unless switched on).
- **Feeling to kind of day:** the app's starting map, which each person can still change for themselves, as now.
- **Whether it opens by itself** on the first visit of the day (on now), and the usual day when it's skipped.
- **The names of the kinds of day**, if an app wants its own words (Survival, Steady and Full energy unless changed).

---

## Capacity

The part of the tab that protects a day, in its own release:

- **That's plenty for today.** After a chosen amount is done, Today says so kindly and stops offering more unless asked. Off unless switched on. It never shows the number.
- **Now stays small.** Now, Next, Later already shows a few at a time and offers no move into a full Now (v1.20.0). This would let an admin set it per kind of day: one in Now on a survival day, say.
- **Hard days:** the rules can offer the essentials, then something that helps, and nothing else, with the admin's own gentle words ("Looking after yourself counts too").

## Hyperfocus

The owner raised it on 2026-11-13: sometimes only one project feels possible, while other things still need doing. A first idea, for the owner to shape:

- A person can say **"I'm focused on this for now"** on a project or entry.
- A rule can test it: **When** focused on something, **then** suggest from it first, and offer one essential from elsewhere only on days the rules allow, with no counting of what waits.
- It ends when the person says so, never by itself, and nothing is lost meanwhile.

## Storage and other modules

- The rules live in `tasks_config.settings` (the column v1.20.0 added), under `suggest`, as plain data in the shape Structure's rule builder writes. They name kinds of day, feelings, categories and kinds of entry by their ids, never by their words, and are checked against what still exists every time they're read. A rule whose category is gone switches off and the tab says why, as timelines do.
- The `tasks.dayTypeRules` filter keeps working on the result, so another module can still adjust Today in code.

## Built on Structure's rule builder

Structure's **If, Then, Else** conditions dialog is being generalised for other modules (the owner relayed Structure's progress on 2026-11-13). Structure registers it three ways: inline on a module's page, as a dialog, and as a full rule builder, the slot `structure.rule-builder`, with the same look as Structure's own dialog. The other module supplies the pieces:

| Structure's piece | What Tasks supplies |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Targets** (optional) | None for Today's rules, so there's no Target step. Later releases may use targets for the separate parts: Today, the check-in, capacity. |
| **What the If rows can test** | Tasks' own facts: the kind of day, the feeling, how it felt, checked in or skipped, the day of the week, the time of day; later capacity on a scale, a calendar's colour for today, something due today, a chosen focus. Each gets Structure's usual tests (text, number, dates with the "today" tests, on/off, "is one of"). |
| **Actions for Then and Else** | Suggest light / medium / big (each on/off), only essentials (on/off), the order (a pick from a list), how many at a time (a number, one to five), also offer one from Later (on/off), prefer or leave out a category or kind of entry (a pick), and later a quiet line on Today (text). |
| **The checker** | Structure works out which actions apply, in the browser for Try it and on the server for Today, following Structure's own read rules. Tasks then settles each setting (the highest rule that mentions it wins) and ranks the tasks as now. |
| **The check before saving** | Run on every save, with Tasks' own checks after it (calm words, values that still exist). |

None of Tasks' facts is about lateness, whatever examples other modules use: there is no "days overdue" in Tasks.

**What this means for the releases:** SR1 waits for Structure's release with the rule builder, and Tasks' dependency on Structure rises to that version (veradai is on Structure 1.77.1, so it would update Structure first). Tasks builds no editor of its own.

## Rules that hold throughout

- **Calm by default.** No rule can make anything red, say anything is late, or show a count. An admin's own words are refused if they use "overdue", "priority", "must" or "failed".
- **Generic.** Nothing in the rules names a domain: no "parent", "court" or "care" in the code or the tab. Examples in these docs may.
- **The person can always change their day.** Rules shape the suggestions; the kind of day stays a tap away on Today.
- **Admin settings with defaults** for every judgement call, and the defaults change nothing.

## Questions for the owner

Each has a recommendation, which becomes the setting's default if the owner agrees.

1. **How rules combine.** Recommended: every matching rule applies, and for each setting the highest rule wins (above). The alternative is "the first rule that matches decides everything", which is simpler but can't combine "Survival" with "Weekends".
2. **Who sets the rules.** Recommended: an admin, for the whole app, while each person keeps their own feeling map and usual day as now. In a one-person app the admin is the person.
3. **Structure's editor.** Settled by Structure's own work: Tasks uses the `structure.rule-builder` slot once it's released. Recommended: wait for it rather than build a stopgap editor.
4. **Kinds of day.** Recommended: keep three, with names an admin can change, and add capacity on a scale as a separate check-in question that rules can test.
5. **"That's plenty for today".** Recommended: offered as a setting in SR3, off unless switched on, never showing a number. Or leave it out.
6. **Hyperfocus.** Is "I'm focused on this for now" the right first shape, and where should a person say it: on the project's page, on Today, or both?
