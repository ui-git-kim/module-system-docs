This page is the **approved** plan for the **Suggest and capacity** tab in Admin › Tasks (the owner approved it on 2026-11-13). The owner asked for it on 2026-11-13: "if energy is this, then do that", set up by an admin, and then shaped it the same day: rules can test a task's own fields (an Energy field, say), people can have their own kinds of day and rules where an admin allows it, and being focused on something for now is not the same as hyperfocus. **SR1, Today's rules, shipped in v1.21.0** (see [Setting Up Today's Rules](/modules/tasks/today-rules/)); SR2 to SR5 are planned. Structure's rule builder, which it builds on, shipped in Structure v1.88.0.

<Aside type="note" title="Nothing changes until an admin changes it">
The starting rules are exactly what Today does now. An app that never opens the tab behaves as it always has.
</Aside>

## Where things stand

Today already follows rules, but they are written in the code:

| Kind of day | What Today suggests now |
|-------------|-------------------------|
| **Survival** | Only essentials, lightest first. |
| **Steady** | Light and medium tasks. |
| **Full energy** | Any size, biggest first. |

In every kind of day, something already in Now comes first, then the nearest due date, then the oldest capture. Three at a time, "Show me the next three" for more, and nothing from Later.

The kind of day comes from the check-in (a feeling, mapped to a kind of day by each person's own settings), or from the person's usual day when they skip it. Another module can already change the rules in code (the `tasks.dayTypeRules` filter).

## What the tab does

**Rules an admin can read and change**, each one a short sentence. There are two kinds.

**Rules about the day** shape Today as a whole:

> **When** the kind of day is **Survival**
> **then** suggest **only essentials**, **lightest first**, **one at a time**.

> **When** it is **Saturday or Sunday**
> **then** prefer tasks in **Home**.

**Rules about each task** test the task's own fields, so a kind of entry with an **Energy** field (deep focus, steady, low-energy win, say) can be matched to the day:

> **When** the kind of day is **Survival** and the task's **Energy** is **Low-energy win**
> **then** suggest it.

> **When** the kind of day is **Survival** and the task's **Energy** is **Deep focus**
> **then** leave it out.

> **When** the kind of day is **Full energy** and the task is in **Later** and its **Energy** is **Deep focus**
> **then** offer it.

### The When side: what a rule can test

| Release | Tests |
|---------|-------|
| First | **About the day:** the kind of day; the feeling and how it felt (from the check-in); whether the person checked in or skipped; the day of the week; the time of day (morning, afternoon, evening, by the person's own clock). **About a task:** any field its kind of entry has in Structure (an Energy field, a list, a tick-box, a date, a number), and Tasks' own details: its size, essential, due date, category, and whether it's in Now, Next or Later. |
| Later | Capacity on a scale, if the check-in asks it; what a calendar says about today (a calendar that colours its days, such as "a day with the kids"); whether something is due today; something the person has chosen to focus on for now (below). |

Tests combine as **all of these** or **any of these**, like Structure's conditions. A rule about each task can test the day too, as in the examples.

### The Then side: what a rule can do

| Release | Actions |
|---------|---------|
| First | **About the day:** which sizes can be suggested; only essentials; the order (lightest first, biggest first, nearest due date, oldest first); how many at a time (one to five); also offer one thing from Later; prefer or leave out a category or a kind of entry. **About a task:** suggest it (even where the day's sizes or essentials-only would leave it out); leave it out; bring it forward; put it after the others; offer it from Later. |
| Later | A quiet line on Today (an admin's own words, checked against the calm-words list); "That's plenty for today" after a chosen amount is done; whether "Show me the next three" is offered. |

Some things no rule can change: a finished task, one waiting on someone, and one put off with "Not now" are never suggested.

### How rules combine

The rules are a list, read from the top. **Every rule that matches applies, and for each setting the highest rule that mentions it wins.** So "Survival: only essentials" and "Weekends: prefer Home" both apply on a survival Saturday, and a rule near the top can override one below it. The last rule is **Always**, which fills in anything no other rule set.

Rules about each task go after the day's settings: "leave it out" beats "suggest it" when both match the same task, and the highest rule wins between two that move it in the order.

### Try it

Choose a kind of day, a feeling, a weekday and a time. The tab shows which rules match, what each setting comes out as and which rule set it, which of your own tasks a task rule changed and why, and the three things Today would suggest. Nothing is saved by trying.

### Starting rules and putting them back

The starting rules are the three in the table above, plus Always. **Put back the starting rules** returns to them after a confirmation.

## Kinds of day and the check-in

A second part of the tab, in its own release:

- **Kinds of day are a list an admin keeps.** It starts with Survival, Steady and Full energy, and an admin can rename them, add more ("Recovery day", "A day with the kids") and put them in order. A kind of day that rules or check-ins still use can't be removed without saying where they go instead.
- **Which questions the check-in asks:** the feeling (on now), how it felt (on now), **capacity on a scale** from empty to full (off unless switched on), and **one thing that would make today feel good** (off unless switched on).
- **Feeling to kind of day:** the app's starting map, which each person can still change for themselves, as now.
- **Whether it opens by itself** on the first visit of the day (on now), and the usual day when it's skipped.

## People's own kinds of day and rules

The owner, 2026-11-13: people can set their own kinds of day and rules, where an admin allows it.

- **An admin's switch:** **People can set their own kinds of day and rules**, off unless switched on.
- **Where a person does it:** in their own Tasks settings, with the same rule builder and Try it, reading only their own tasks.
- **How theirs sit with the app's:** a person's own rules go above the app's, so theirs win where they say something and the app's fill in the rest. Their own kinds of day are offered alongside the app's, and they can hide any of the app's they don't use.
- **Private:** a person's rules and kinds of day are their own, like a check-in. An admin doesn't see them, and they go with the person's account.

## Capacity

The part of the tab that protects a day, in its own release:

- **That's plenty for today.** After a chosen amount is done, Today says so kindly and stops offering more unless asked. Off unless switched on. It never shows the number.
- **Now stays small.** Now, Next, Later already shows a few at a time and offers no move into a full Now (v1.20.0). This would let an admin set it per kind of day: one in Now on a survival day, say.
- **Hard days:** the rules can offer the essentials, then something that helps, and nothing else, with the admin's own gentle words ("Looking after yourself counts too").

---

## Focused on something for now

A choice a person makes: "for now, this is where my attention goes".

- A person says **"I'm focused on this for now"** on a project's (or any entry's) own page, or on Today. Both, as the owner chose.
- A rule can test it: **When** focused on something, **then** suggest from it first, and offer one essential from elsewhere only on days the rules allow, with no counting of what waits.
- It ends when the person says so, never by itself, and nothing is lost meanwhile.

## Hyperfocus

**Not the same thing** (the owner, 2026-11-13). Focused on something is a choice; hyperfocus is a state someone finds themselves in, absorbed and hard to pull out of, which can last hours and crowd out food, rest and other things that matter. What helps there is different: gentle awareness of time passing (the planned timer could help), reminders for the essentials, and an easy way back to the rest of the day, all without breaking the focus by force. It needs its own design with the owner, informed by the research the owner is gathering, and is not part of these releases.

## Storage and other modules

- The rules live in `tasks_config.settings` (the column v1.20.0 added), under `suggest`, as plain data in the shape Structure's rule builder writes. They name kinds of day, feelings, categories and kinds of entry by their ids, never by their words, and are checked against what still exists every time they're read. A rule whose category is gone switches off and the tab says why, as timelines do.
- The `tasks.dayTypeRules` filter keeps working on the result, so another module can still adjust Today in code.

## Built on Structure's rule builder

Structure's **If, Then, Else** conditions dialog was generalised for other modules in **Structure v1.88.0** (2026-11-13). Structure registers it three ways: inline on a module's page, as a dialog, and as a full rule builder, the slot `structure.rule-builder`, with the same look as Structure's own dialog. The other module supplies the pieces:

| Structure's piece | What Tasks supplies |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Targets** (optional) | **Today** (rules about the day) and **each kind of entry used for tasks** (rules about each task of that kind, so the If rows can offer that kind's own fields, such as an Energy field). |
| **What the If rows can test** | For a task: its kind's own fields, which Structure supplies and reads under its own rules. For both: Tasks' own facts: the kind of day, the feeling, how it felt, checked in or skipped, the day of the week, the time of day, and for a task its size, essential, due date, category and Now, Next or Later; later capacity on a scale, a calendar's colour for today, something due today, a chosen focus. Each gets Structure's usual tests (text, number, dates with the "today" tests, on/off, "is one of"). |
| **Actions for Then and Else** | About the day: suggest light / medium / big (each on/off), only essentials (on/off), the order (a pick from a list), how many at a time (a number, one to five), also offer one from Later (on/off), prefer or leave out a category or kind of entry (a pick), and later a quiet line on Today (text). About a task: suggest it, leave it out, bring it forward, put it after the others, offer it from Later. |
| **The checker** | Structure works out which actions apply, in the browser for Try it and on the server for Today, following Structure's own read rules. Tasks then settles each setting (the highest rule that mentions it wins) and ranks the tasks as now. |
| **The check before saving** | Run on every save, with Tasks' own checks after it (calm words, values that still exist). |

None of Tasks' facts is about lateness, whatever examples other modules use: there is no "days overdue" in Tasks.

**What this means for the releases:** Tasks' dependency on Structure rises to ^1.88.0 with SR1 (veradai is on Structure 1.77.1, so it updates Structure first). Tasks builds no editor of its own. Structure keeps the rules as a plain list (each with an optional target, an If, a Then and an Else), checks a list before a save, and works a rule out the same way in the browser and on the server; a fact Tasks supplies is tested under its own key, beside the fields of the kinds of entry a target names.

## Releases

| Release | What it holds |
|---------|---------------|
| **SR1 — Today's rules** (shipped in v1.21.0; needs Structure v1.88.0) | The tab, Structure's rule builder fed with Tasks' facts and actions, rules about the day and about each task (its own fields, such as an Energy field), the starting rules (today's behaviour), how rules combine, Try it, and putting the starting rules back. |
| **SR2 — Kinds of day and the check-in** | Kinds of day as a list an admin keeps, the check-in's questions (capacity on a scale, one thing for today), the starting feeling map, opening by itself, and capacity as a test. |
| **SR3 — People's own** | The admin's switch, and a person's own kinds of day and rules in their settings, above the app's. |
| **SR4 — Capacity** | That's plenty for today, Now per kind of day, quiet lines on Today, and whether more is offered. |
| **SR5 — From elsewhere** | Tests that read a calendar's day colour and what's due today, and "focused on something for now" on an entry's page and on Today. |

Hyperfocus is designed separately with the owner, after these.

As built in v1.21.0 (SR1):

- **Two targets, The day and Each task.** One "Each task" target covers every kind of entry, with **Kind of entry** as a fact to test, rather than a target per kind.
- **A task's own fields are Tasks' facts.** Structure's rule builder can read a record's fields itself, but Structure would then read each task again for every rule check. Tasks already holds each task's data when Today chooses, so it offers the kinds' fields (text, numbers, lists, tick-boxes, dates; never a secret) as facts and fills them in. Every rule is still worked out by Structure's own checker.
- **Tasks' dependency on Structure stays ^1.77.0.** On an older Structure the rules are shown but can't be changed, and Today follows the starting rules.
- **Categories are tested and preferred by name**, since each person has their own.
- **Prefer and leave out gather from every rule that holds**, rather than the highest one winning.
- **"Days until it's due"** goes by the person's own date; dates in a kind's own fields use Structure's "today" tests, which go by the server's date.
- **Try it** in the tab uses the admin's own tasks; Structure's dialog keeps its own Try it for one rule.

## Rules that hold throughout

- **Calm by default.** No rule can make anything red, say anything is late, or show a count. An admin's own words are refused if they use "overdue", "priority", "must" or "failed".
- **Generic.** Nothing in the rules names a domain: no "parent", "court" or "care" in the code or the tab. Examples in these docs may.
- **The person can always change their day.** Rules shape the suggestions; the kind of day stays a tap away on Today.
- **Admin settings with defaults** for every judgement call, and the defaults change nothing.

## The owner's answers (2026-11-13)

- **Rules test fields too:** a rule can test a task's own fields, such as an Energy field (rules about each task, above).
- **Who sets the rules:** an admin, for the whole app, and people can set their own kinds of day and rules where an admin switches that on (above).
- **Where to say "focused on this for now":** both, on the entry's page and on Today. **It is not hyperfocus**, which is designed separately.
- **Structure's editor:** Structure is building its rule builder for other modules, so Tasks uses it and builds no stopgap.

**Approved as recommended (2026-11-13):**

1. **How rules combine:** every matching rule applies, and for each setting the highest rule wins; between rules about a task, "leave it out" beats "suggest it".
2. **Kinds of day:** a list an admin keeps, starting with the three, with capacity on a scale as a separate check-in question that rules can test.
3. **A person's own rules and kinds of day:** their rules go above the app's, their kinds of day sit alongside the app's, and they can hide the app's ones they don't use.
4. **"That's plenty for today":** a setting in SR4, off unless switched on, never showing a number.
5. **"Suggest it" from a task rule:** it lets a task through the day's sizes and essentials-only, but never brings back something finished, waiting, or put off with "Not now".
