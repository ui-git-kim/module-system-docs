## Making a task regular

In the task editor, under **How it gets done**, **Rhythm** is **Once** (the usual) or **Regular**:

- **How often**: every day, week, fortnight, month or year. A weekly or fortnightly one can fall on several days (Monday and Thursday, say).
- **From**: its first day. Unless changed, its due date, else today.
- **Ends**: it keeps going, or on a day.

**Keep it** saves it, and the editor says it in words ("Every week on Friday"). **Once** makes it a task done once again.

The rule is the one calendars use, kept on the task under the same keys, so a calendar that shows the task's kind shows its days too (where the calendar reads that kind's repeats, and the task has a date in the calendar's start field).

---

## On Today

- **Its days only.** A regular task is offered on the days its rule falls on, like any task in Not started or Doing (it needs What it takes, as every task does).
- **Done marks the day.** It stays where it is (it isn't moved to Done) and Tasks notes the day. Today says "Done for today", and it comes back on its next day without a new entry each time.
- **Undo** puts the day back, as Undo always does.
- **The done list** shows it on the day it was done.

Moving a regular task to a Done stage yourself (on the Board, or its stage in the editor) finishes it for good, like any task.

<Aside type="note" title="Calm rules">
Nothing is counted: no streaks, no "3 days in a row", and a day it wasn't done is just a day. Tasks keeps the last 60 days it was done, for itself.
</Aside>
