A timeline shows chosen kinds of Structure entry in date order: a case's chronology, say, with its incidents, documents and court dates. An admin makes one in **Admin › Tasks**, in the **Timelines** card.

Each person's own tasks are already on **My tasks**, so there's nothing to set up for those.

<Aside type="note" title="Simpler since v1.3.4">
There is no separate first step any more. Choose any kind of entry in the timeline editor, and saving the timeline switches that kind on for Timelines. (In v1.3.0 to v1.3.3 a kind had to be switched on in **Entry types** first.)
</Aside>

---

## What a kind of entry needs

- **A date or date-time field.** It places each entry on the timeline. If a kind has none, the editor says so; add one in Structure's App Structure first.
- **To be switched on in Structure.** A disabled type isn't offered.
- **Not a connection type, and not one of Tasks' own** (its `task` type and categories). My tasks already covers Tasks' own.
- **The right owner.** An app-level kind works for everyone: each person sees their own entries of it, and a shared kind shows everyone's. A kind you built for yourself in Structure works for you only, as it does there: only you see its entries on the timeline.

---

## Make the timeline

In **Admin › Tasks**, go to the **Timelines** card and choose **New timeline**. The editor asks one thing at a time, with **Back** and **Next**.

<Steps>

1. **Name**

   What people see in the list of timelines, on its tab and on its widget. Its web address (`/tasks/views/…`) comes from the name and can't change once saved. **Ready for people to use** off keeps it a draft only admins can open.

2. **What it shows**

   Up to eight kinds of entry. For each one:

   - **Its date**: the date or date-time field that places it.
   - **Title**: its name, or a text field.
   - **Summary**: a text field, or none.
   - **Show**: up to four more fields, shown exactly as recorded.
   - **Called**: what this kind is called on the timeline (its plural name unless you change it).
   - **Colour**: its colour in Structure, its stage's colour, or none.
   - **Its times are in**: for a date that has a time, the time zone it was recorded in. See [time zones](#time-zones) below.

3. **Where it shows**

   - **Everything each person can see**: each person sees their own entries of these kinds, and any shared with everyone.
   - **One entry's own**: for example a case's chronology. Under **It belongs to each**, tick the kinds of entry it belongs to. Then, for each kind it shows, choose how its entries belong: **Filed under it in Structure**, through a reference field that points at it, or **They don't: the same ones show for every entry** (v1.11.0). The last is for something every entry shares, such as school holidays: add them once, and each entry's timeline and calendar shows them. In an app with spaces, a kind a space keeps is shared inside the entry's own space; any other kind is shared across everything the person can see.
     - **Show it as a tab on each entry it belongs to** adds a tab named after the timeline.
     - **A widget for the entry's page** offers a short list from the timeline for that entry's page: **What's coming up** (the next few from today on) or **The latest** (the most recent up to today). See [the widget](#a-widget-on-the-entrys-page) below.
   - **List it under Timelines**: next to My tasks, at the top of the Timelines page.
   - **It starts with** the oldest or the newest.

4. **Preview and save**

   A preview of your own entries. For one entry's own, choose an entry to preview. Nothing is saved until you save.

</Steps>

Saving switches each kind the timeline shows on for Timelines. You can see that in the **Entry types** card afterwards, and switch a kind off again there: the timeline then leaves that kind out and shows **Needs a look**.

## Where people find it

- **Timelines** in the view switcher at the top of every Tasks page: **My tasks** first, then each timeline listed under Timelines.
- **A tab on each entry it belongs to**, for one entry's own with the tab switched on.
- **A widget on the entry's page**, once an admin has placed it (below).
- **Inside another module**, through the `tasks.timeline` component slot (props `viewId`, `entryId`).

A timeline is read as the person viewing it, so it never shows anything they couldn't see in Structure.

<Aside type="tip" title="In an app with spaces">
Where entries live inside a space (a matter, say), a timeline over "everything each person can see" shows the open space's entries, and nothing when no space is open. For a matter's own chronology, use **One entry's own** belonging to each matter.
</Aside>

## A widget on the entry's page

A timeline that belongs to an entry can put a short list on that entry's page in Structure. Added in v1.3.4.

<Steps>

1. In the timeline editor's **Where it shows** step, set **A widget for the entry's page** to **What's coming up** or **The latest**, and save.
2. In Structure's **App Structure**, open the type the timeline belongs to (the case type, say) and go to **Pages**, then **Detail page**.
3. Choose **Add a widget…** and pick the widget named after the timeline. Drag it where you want it and choose its size.
4. Save the type.

</Steps>

The widget shows up to five entries with their date and time, each opening its own page, and **Open the timeline** for the rest. It has no counts, and a date that has passed is a plain date. When there is nothing to show it says "Nothing coming up." or "Nothing here yet."

An entry that [repeats](/modules/tasks/calendars/#repeats) is listed on each of its days in the widget (v1.5.0). On the timeline itself it is listed once, on its first day, with how it repeats.

On a type that still uses Structure's older page with an Overview tab, a widget shows there by itself once it is switched on for the timeline. To hide it for a type, use the type's **Integrations** tab.

## Time zones

A court event can be listed in Adelaide time while you are in New South Wales. Structure stores a time with no time zone, so the kind says which zone its times are in. Added in v1.4.1.

- **Its times are in.** In **What it shows**, for a kind whose date has a time, type a place to find its time zone (typing "Adelaide" offers Australia/Adelaide). Leave it empty when times are recorded in each person's own time.
- **Or the time zone named on each entry.** Where different entries are in different zones, choose a list or text field on the kind that holds the zone, such as a "Court time zone" list whose values are zone names like Australia/Adelaide and Australia/Perth. An entry that leaves it empty, or holds something that isn't a time zone, uses the kind's own.
- **What people see.** Their own time, with the recorded time beside it: "10:30 am (10:00 am Adelaide time)". When it falls on another day for them, the entry sits on their day and says the recorded day too: "1:30 am (11:30 pm Thu 2 Jul, Perth time)".
- **Whose time is "their own".** The time zone saved in the person's settings, which is their device's until they choose one.
- A date with no time is never moved, and daylight saving is followed on both sides.

## When something changes

- **A field it uses is removed in Structure.** Only what used that field switches off. The timeline shows **Needs a look** in the Timelines card, and the editor says what it took off when you open it. Save to settle it.
- **A kind is switched off for Timelines** in Entry types. The timeline leaves that kind out, and the Timelines card shows **Needs a look** with the reason. Switch it back on, or open the timeline and save it again.
- **A new timeline's tab** shows when you open another entry or reload, not on the entry already on screen. See the [Roadmap](/modules/tasks/roadmap/#a-new-timelines-tab-shows-on-an-open-entry-once-you-move-on).

## Example: a case chronology

Say the app has a **Case** kind, and **Incident** and **Document** kinds with a date field each. Incidents are filed under their case, and documents point at their case through a reference field called **Case**.

<Steps>

1. **New timeline**, named "Chronology".
2. **What it shows**: Incidents (its date field, with a description as the summary), then Documents (the date received).
3. **Where it shows**: **One entry's own**, belonging to each **Case**. Incidents are **Filed under it in Structure**; documents belong through **Case**. Switch on the tab.
4. **Preview and save**: pick a case to preview, then save.

</Steps>

Each case now has a **Chronology** tab, and the timeline is listed under Timelines if you switched that on.

## Example: what's coming up on a matter

For a matter's upcoming court events:

<Steps>

1. **New timeline**, named "Coming up".
2. **What it shows**: Court events, with the field that holds the court date.
3. **Where it shows**: **One entry's own**, belonging to each **Matter**, with **A widget for the entry's page** set to **What's coming up**.
4. Save, then place the "Coming up" widget on the Matter type's page in Structure (**Pages › Detail page › Add a widget…**).

</Steps>
