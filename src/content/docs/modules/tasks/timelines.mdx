A timeline shows chosen types of Structure entry in date order: a case's chronology, say, with its incidents, documents and court dates. An admin makes one in **Admin › Tasks**, on the **Views** tab, under **Timelines**.

Each person's own tasks are already on **My tasks**, so there's nothing to set up for those.

<Aside type="note" title="Simpler since v1.3.4">
There is no separate first step any more. Choose any entry type in the timeline editor, and saving the timeline switches that type on for Timelines. (In v1.3.0 to v1.3.3 a type had to be switched on in **Entry types** first.)
</Aside>

---

## What an entry type needs

- **A date or date-time field.** It places each entry on the timeline. If a type has none, the editor says so; add one in Structure's App Structure first.
- **To be switched on in Structure.** A disabled type isn't offered.
- **Not a connection type, and not one of Tasks' own** (its `task` type and categories). My tasks already covers Tasks' own.
- **The right owner.** An app-level type works for everyone: each person sees their own entries of it, and a shared type shows everyone's. A type you built for yourself in Structure works for you only, as it does there: only you see its entries on the timeline.

## Make the timeline

In **Admin › Tasks**, open the **Modes** tab (Views before v1.22.97), then **Timelines**, and choose **New timeline**. The editor asks one thing at a time, with **Back** and **Next**.

<Steps>

1. **Name**

   What people see in the list of timelines, on its tab and on its widget. Its web address (`/tasks/views/…`) comes from **Short name for links**, which is filled in from the name and can be changed until the first save; it can't change once saved. **Ready for people to use** off keeps it a draft only admins can open.

2. **What it shows**

   Up to eight entry types. For each one:

   - **Its date**: the date or date-time field that places it.
   - **Title**: its name, or a text field.
   - **Summary**: a text field, or none.
   - **Show**: up to four more fields, shown exactly as recorded.
   - **Columns in the table** (v1.22.98, where the timeline offers the table): **The same as Also show**, or up to twelve of the type's fields of your own (never a secret one). Fields of different types with the same name share a column. The journey map's rows are the same fields (it says **Rows in the journey map** where the timeline offers only the map).
   - **On the milestone line** (v1.22.100, where the timeline offers it): ticked for every type unless you untick one.
   - **Lanes in the Gantt** (v1.22.99, where the timeline offers the Gantt): **Its group on the key**, or a field whose value gives each entry its lane: a link to one entry (who it's with, the matter it's for), a list (an area) or a text field. An entry with no value sits in its type's group.
   - **Called**: what this type is called on the timeline (its plural name unless you change it).
   - **Colour**: its colour in Structure, **A colour I choose**, its stage's colour, **The colour of an entry it links to** (offered where the type has a link field), or none.
   - **Its times are in**: for a date that has a time, the time zone it was recorded in. See [time zones](#time-zones) below.

3. **Where it shows**

   - **Everything each person can see**: each person sees their own entries of these types, and any shared with everyone.
   - **One entry's own**: for example a case's chronology. Under **It belongs to each**, tick the entry types it belongs to. Then, for each type it shows, choose how its entries belong: **Filed under it in Structure**, through a reference field that points at it, or **They don't: the same ones show for every entry** (v1.11.0). The last is for something every entry shares, such as school holidays: add them once, and each entry's timeline and calendar shows them. In an app with spaces, a type a space keeps is shared inside the entry's own space; any other type is shared across everything the person can see.
     - **Show it as a tab on each entry it belongs to** adds a tab named after the timeline.
     - **A widget for the entry's page** offers a short list from the timeline for that entry's page: **What's coming up** (the next few from today on) or **The latest** (the most recent up to today). See [the widget](#a-widget-on-the-entrys-page) below.
   - **List it in Tasks**: lists it under **Timelines**, beside My tasks, at the top of the Timelines page. A timeline shown as a calendar is listed there too (the Calendar page shows only the default calendar since v1.22.82).
   - **As a timeline, people see** (v1.22.98): **Cards**, **A table**, **A Gantt chart** (v1.22.99), **A milestone line** (v1.22.100) and **A journey map** (v1.22.102), and with more than one, **It opens on** one of them. The cards, the table or the journey map always stay: a chart is never the only way to read a timeline. A new timeline offers the cards and the table and opens on the cards; one saved before v1.22.98 shows the cards alone until you tick more. With the Gantt, **The Gantt starts at** weeks, months (unless changed) or quarters; with the milestone line, **The milestone line starts at** weeks, months (unless changed) or years. With the cards, **The cards** go **Down one line** (unless changed) or **Either side of the line** on a wide screen. See [the table](#the-table), [the Gantt](#the-gantt), [the milestone line](#the-milestone-line) and [the journey map](#the-journey-map) below.
   - **It starts with** the oldest or the newest.

4. **Preview and save**

   A preview of your own entries. For one entry's own, choose an entry to preview. Nothing is saved until you save.

</Steps>

Saving switches each type the timeline shows on for Timelines. You can see that in the **Entry types** card afterwards, and switch a type off again there: the timeline then leaves that type out and shows **Needs a look**.

## Where people find it

- **Timelines**, a mode on the bar at the top of every Tasks page: **My tasks** first, then each timeline listed under Timelines. An admin can switch it off with **Timelines on the Tasks page** (Admin › Tasks › Modes › Timelines; v1.22.95); tabs and widgets on entries stay. A timeline that offers more than one view shows **Cards | Table | Gantt | Milestones | Journey map** (those it offers) beside its Filter (v1.22.98), on the Tasks page and on its tab alike.
- **Print** (v1.22.97), on My tasks and on a timeline on the Tasks page: the cards as shown, for the dates and filters on screen, in a print window of their own (light, colours kept, no buttons), running over as many pages as they need. The print window offers Save as PDF, for handing a chronology to someone.
- **A tab on each entry it belongs to**, for one entry's own with the tab switched on.
- **A widget on the entry's page**, once an admin has placed it (below).
- **Inside another module**, through the `tasks.timeline` component slot (props `viewId`, `entryId`).

A timeline is read as the person viewing it, so it never shows anything they couldn't see in Structure.

### Reading a timeline

- **Filter** (it says **Filtered** while one is set) narrows the list: **From** and **To** dates, **Show** to tick the entry types (where there's more than one), and **Oldest first** or **Newest first**. **Clear filters** goes back to everything. On the timeline's own page the filters sit in the web address, so a filtered list can be shared or reloaded; on a tab they're kept while you're there.
- **A page at a time.** The list shows its first entries, then **Show later entries** (oldest first) or **Show earlier entries** (newest first) reads on. Nothing is counted.
- Where the timeline is also a calendar, **Timeline** and **Calendar** switch between the two.

### The table

A timeline that offers it can be read as a table (v1.22.98, the chronology of the [Modes and Views Plan](/modules/tasks/modes-plan/)): a row for each entry, in date order. **Cards | Table** switches between the two, keeping the same dates and filters. On a Tasks page it opens on the one you used last there (your browser keeps it); otherwise on the admin's **It opens on**.

- **Columns:** **Date** (with the time, and the last day for an entry that runs on), the type where there are several, **Title**, **Summary** and **Stage** where any entry has one, then the columns the admin offers for each type. **Columns** hides or shows any of them but the date and title, and your browser keeps your choice for that timeline, as yours only (it's forgotten when you sign out).
- **Sort** by any column; **Search the words** finds a row by any of its words. The dates and types come from the timeline's **Filter**, as for the cards.
- **A row opens its entry ready to edit.**
- **Print** puts the table on paper as it's shown (its columns, search and order), in landscape, with no buttons. **Save as a spreadsheet** saves a CSV of every row shown for these dates, with every column, for a spreadsheet to sort and hide in its own way. A cell that would start like a formula is saved as plain text.
- Undated entries aren't in it: they wait in **Not scheduled yet** on the calendar.
- The table is the starter's shared table, which says how many rows it shows (for example "12 entries"). That's the one count on the page, kept by the owner's choice.
- My tasks has the cards only for now.

### The Gantt

A timeline that offers it can be read as a Gantt chart (v1.22.99, the [Modes and Views Plan](/modules/tasks/modes-plan/)'s MV3): an entry with an end is a bar from its first day to its last, and one without is a diamond on its day, each with its title.

- **Lanes:** a row for each lane, named down the left: a value of the field the admin chose for each type (an area, who it's with), or the type's group. Entries that would overlap in a lane take a line each.
- **Scale:** **Weeks**, **Months** or **Quarters**, starting at the admin's choice. The chart scrolls sideways; the lanes' names stay in view.
- **Show today** draws a dashed line on today's date. It's off unless you turn it on, so a bar that ended before today never looks late.
- **A bar or diamond opens its entry ready to edit.** Each one is a link named in full (its title, when, and its lane), so the chart can be read with a keyboard or a screen reader, and the cards or the table are always one click away.
- An entry's colour is a stripe at the start of its bar (or the diamond's fill), never the only sign: titles and lanes are written out. Nothing is red, and nothing is counted.
- **Print** fits the whole chart onto one landscape page (choose a wider scale first for a long stretch), with no buttons.
- **Join a journey's steps** (v1.22.101) draws an arrow from each journey step to the next one on the chart, where the chart has steps of the same journey. It's off unless you turn it on. Moving one thing never moves anything else.

### The milestone line

A timeline that offers it can be read as a milestone line (v1.22.100, the [Modes and Views Plan](/modules/tasks/modes-plan/)'s MV4): the entries of its milestone types along a line in date order, each with its card above or below the line, taking turns.

- **Placed by date**, so the space between two cards is the time between them. Cards that would overlap step further from the line.
- **Scale:** **Weeks**, **Months** or **Years**, starting at the admin's choice. The line scrolls sideways.
- **On a narrow screen** (a phone), it's the cards down one line instead.
- **A card opens its entry ready to edit**: its date, title and type, named in full for a screen reader. An entry's colour fills its dot on the line, beside the written words. There's no "today" mark and nothing is counted.
- **Print** fits the whole line onto one landscape page.

**The cards either side of the line** (v1.22.100) is a look of Cards an admin can choose: on a wide screen the days take turns left and right of the line; on a phone they stay down one line.

### The journey map

A timeline that offers it can be read as a journey map (v1.22.102, the [Modes and Views Plan](/modules/tasks/modes-plan/)'s MV6): the table turned on its side. Each entry is a column, in date order, with its date and title on top; the rows are the type where there are several, the summary and stage where any entry has one, then the same fields as the table's columns (what happens, where, what you noticed, ideas: whatever fields the type has in Structure).

- **An entry's title opens it ready to edit.**
- It scrolls sideways; the fields' names stay in view down the left.
- It's a real table, with a heading for each entry and each field, so it reads word for word with a screen reader. That's why, unlike the charts, it can be a timeline's only view.
- **Print** fits it onto one landscape page.
- No feeling line (the owner), nothing counted, nothing marked late. A set-up over the types a journey makes draws that journey this way too.

<Aside type="tip" title="In an app with spaces">
Where entries live inside a space (a matter, say), a timeline over "everything each person can see" shows what Structure shows the person: the open space's entries, their own entries in no space yet, and types no space keeps. With no space open, a kept type shows only the person's entries in no space yet. For a matter's own chronology, use **One entry's own** belonging to each matter.
</Aside>

## A widget on the entry's page

A timeline that belongs to an entry can put a short list on that entry's page in Structure. Added in v1.3.4.

<Steps>

1. In the timeline editor's **Where it shows** step, set **A widget for the entry's page** to **What's coming up** or **The latest**, and save.
2. In Structure's **App Structure**, open the type the timeline belongs to (the case type, say) and go to **Pages**, then **Detail page**.
3. Choose **Add a widget…** and pick the widget named after the timeline. Drag it where you want it and choose its size.
4. Save the type.

</Steps>

The widget shows up to five entries (three at its smallest size) with their date and time, each opening its own page, and **Open the timeline** for the rest. It has no counts, and a date that has passed is a plain date. When there is nothing to show it says "Nothing coming up." or "Nothing here yet."

An entry that [repeats](/modules/tasks/calendars/#repeats) is listed on each of its days in the widget (v1.5.0). On the timeline itself it is listed once, on its first day, with how it repeats.

On a type that still uses Structure's older page with an Overview tab, a widget shows there by itself once it is switched on for the timeline. To hide it for a type, use the type's **Integrations** tab.

## Time zones

A court event can be listed in Adelaide time while you are in New South Wales. Structure stores a time with no time zone, so the type says which zone its times are in. Added in v1.4.1.

- **Its times are in.** In **What it shows**, for a type whose date has a time, type a place to find its time zone (typing "Adelaide" offers Australia/Adelaide). Leave it empty when times are recorded in each person's own time.
- **Or the time zone named on each entry.** Where different entries are in different zones, choose a list or text field on the type that holds the zone, such as a "Court time zone" list whose values are zone names like Australia/Adelaide and Australia/Perth. An entry that leaves it empty, or holds something that isn't a time zone, uses the type's own.
- **What people see.** Their own time, with the recorded time beside it: "10:30 am (10:00 am Adelaide time)". When it falls on another day for them, the entry sits on their day and says the recorded day too: "1:30 am (11:30 pm Thu 2 Jul, Perth time)".
- **Whose time is "their own".** The time zone saved in the person's settings, which is their device's until they choose one.
- A date with no time is never moved, and daylight saving is followed on both sides.

## When something changes

- **A field it uses is removed in Structure.** Only what used that field switches off. The timeline shows **Needs a look** in its list on the Modes tab, and the editor says what it took off when you open it. Save to settle it.
- **A type is switched off for Timelines** in Entry types. The timeline leaves that type out, and its list on the Modes tab shows **Needs a look** with the reason. Switch it back on, or open the timeline and save it again.
- **A new timeline's tab** shows when you open another entry or reload, not on the entry already on screen. See the [Roadmap](/modules/tasks/roadmap/#a-new-timelines-tab-shows-on-an-open-entry-once-you-move-on).

## Example: a case chronology

Say the app has a **Case** type, and **Incident** and **Document** types with a date field each. Incidents are filed under their case, and documents point at their case through a reference field called **Case**.

<Steps>

1. **New timeline**, named "Chronology".
2. **What it shows**: Incidents (its date field, with a description as the summary), then Documents (the date received).
3. **Where it shows**: **One entry's own**, belonging to each **Case**. Incidents are **Filed under it in Structure**; documents belong through **Case**. Switch on the tab.
4. **Preview and save**: pick a case to preview, then save.

</Steps>

Each case now has a **Chronology** tab, and the timeline is listed under Timelines if you switched that on.

## Example: what's coming up on a matter

For a matter's upcoming court events:

<Steps>

1. **New timeline**, named "Coming up".
2. **What it shows**: Court events, with the field that holds the court date.
3. **Where it shows**: **One entry's own**, belonging to each **Matter**, with **A widget for the entry's page** set to **What's coming up**.
4. Save, then place the "Coming up" widget on the Matter type's page in Structure (**Pages › Detail page › Add a widget…**).

</Steps>
