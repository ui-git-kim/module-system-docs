A timeline shows chosen kinds of Structure entry in date order: a case's chronology, say, with its incidents, documents and court dates. An admin sets one up in **Admin › Tasks** in two steps. First switch each kind of entry on for Timelines, then make the timeline.

Each person's own tasks are already on **My tasks**, so there's nothing to set up for those.

<Aside type="note" title="New in v1.3.0">
A kind of entry has to be switched on for Timelines before a timeline can show it. Timelines made before v1.3.0 keep working: the update switched on the kinds they use. From v1.3.1 the editor lists only the kinds that are switched on.
</Aside>

---

## What a kind of entry needs

- **A date or date-time field.** It places each entry on the timeline. If a kind has none, the editor says so; add one in Structure's App Structure first.
- **To be switched on in Structure.** A disabled type isn't offered.
- **Not a connection type, and not one of Tasks' own** (its `task` type and categories). My tasks already covers Tasks' own.
- **The right owner.** An app-level kind works for everyone: each person sees their own entries of it, and a shared kind shows everyone's. A kind you built for yourself in Structure works for you only, as it does there: only you see its entries on the timeline.

---

## 1. Switch the kind on for Timelines

<Steps>

1. Go to **Admin › Tasks**. The **Entry types** card is at the top.
2. Find the kind. Kinds already in use are under **In use**; open **Other kinds of entry** for the rest.
3. Turn on its **Timelines** switch. It saves straight away.

</Steps>

Switching on Timelines doesn't make the kind a task. Its **Tasks** setup (under **Tasks and stages**) is separate, so a kind can be on for Tasks, Timelines, both or neither.

You only switch on the kinds a timeline *shows*. The kind a timeline *belongs to* (the case, in a case chronology) doesn't need it.

---

## 2. Make the timeline

In the **Timelines** card, choose **New timeline**. The editor asks one thing at a time, with **Back** and **Next**.

<Steps>

1. **Name**

   What people see in the list of timelines and on its tab. Its web address (`/tasks/views/…`) comes from the name and can't change once saved. **Ready for people to use** off keeps it a draft only admins can open.

2. **What it shows**

   Up to three kinds of entry. Only kinds switched on for Timelines are listed. For each one:

   - **Its date**: the date or date-time field that places it.
   - **Title**: its name, or a text field.
   - **Summary**: a text field, or none.
   - **Show**: up to four more fields, shown exactly as recorded.
   - **Called**: what this kind is called on the timeline (its plural name unless you change it).
   - **Colour**: its colour in Structure, its stage's colour, or none.

3. **Where it shows**

   - **Everything each person can see**: each person sees their own entries of these kinds, and any shared with everyone.
   - **One entry's own**: for example a case's chronology. Under **It belongs to each**, tick the kinds of entry it belongs to. Then, for each kind it shows, choose how its entries belong: **Filed under it in Structure**, or through a reference field that points at it. **Show it as a tab on each entry it belongs to** adds a tab named after the timeline.
   - **List it under Timelines**: next to My tasks, at the top of the Timelines page.
   - **It starts with** the oldest or the newest.

4. **Preview and save**

   A preview of your own entries. For one entry's own, choose an entry to preview. Nothing is saved until you save.

</Steps>

---

## Where people find it

- **Timelines** in the view switcher at the top of every Tasks page: **My tasks** first, then each timeline listed under Timelines.
- **A tab on each entry it belongs to**, for one entry's own with the tab switched on.
- **Inside another module**, through the `tasks.timeline` component slot (props `viewId`, `entryId`).

A timeline is read as the person viewing it, so it never shows anything they couldn't see in Structure.

---

## When something changes

- **A field it uses is removed in Structure.** Only what used that field switches off. The timeline shows **Needs a look** in the Timelines card, and the editor says what it took off when you open it. Save to settle it.
- **A kind is switched off for Timelines.** The timeline leaves that kind out, and the Timelines card shows **Needs a look** with the reason. Switch it back on, or edit the timeline to remove the kind.
- **A new timeline's tab** shows when you open another entry or reload, not on the entry already on screen. See the [Roadmap](/modules/tasks/roadmap/#a-new-timelines-tab-shows-on-an-open-entry-once-you-move-on).

## Example: a case chronology

Say the app has a **Case** kind, and **Incident** and **Document** kinds with a date field each. Incidents are filed under their case, and documents point at their case through a reference field called **Case**.

<Steps>

1. In **Entry types**, switch **Timelines** on for **Incident** and **Document**. Case doesn't need it: it's what the timeline belongs to.
2. **New timeline**, named "Chronology".
3. **What it shows**: Incidents (its date field, with a description as the summary), then Documents (the date received).
4. **Where it shows**: **One entry's own**, belonging to each **Case**. Incidents are **Filed under it in Structure**; documents belong through **Case**. Switch on the tab.
5. **Preview and save**: pick a case to preview, then save.

</Steps>

Each case now has a **Chronology** tab, and the timeline is listed under Timelines if you switched that on.
