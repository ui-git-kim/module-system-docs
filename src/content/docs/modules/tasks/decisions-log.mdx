P5 of the [Task Parts Plan](/modules/tasks/task-parts-plan/), in v1.22.66. The owner: "i think decisions might need to be their own node type". So a decision is an entry of a kind you make in Structure, and Tasks works with it. (Tasks' own design choices are on [Design Decisions](/modules/tasks/decisions/).)

---

## Setting it up

<Steps>

1. In Structure, make a kind of entry for decisions (**Decision**, say) with the fields you want: the decision, the reason, the people, and pros and cons as two lists or a field group. On its Hierarchy tab, let it sit under the kinds you use for tasks.
2. In **Admin › Tasks › General › Decisions**, choose it under **Decisions are kept as**. A kind used for tasks can't be the Decision kind.
3. Tasks adds three fields to it: **Decided on** (empty while it's still to decide), **Follow up** (something to come back to) and **Follow up in** (In 3 days, In a week, In 2 weeks, In a month, In 3 months). They show on the decision's page with its own fields.

</Steps>

---

## A task's decision log

In a task's editor, under **What it needs** (beside Comes after), **Decisions** lists the decisions filed under the task: still to decide first, then the ones made, latest first. Each opens on its page, ready to edit. **Add a decision** opens Structure's own form, filled in to file it under the task. Nothing shows there until an admin has chosen a Decision kind.

---

## Follow-ups that make tasks

Give a decision a **Follow up** and a **Follow up in**, and when it's saved Tasks makes a task, once:

- titled with the follow-up's words ("Review how renting is going");
- due that long after **Decided on** (or the day the decision was made, if that's empty);
- filed under the decision's task, where a kind of task can go there (otherwise where quick capture files one);
- sized Brief and put in a stage that means Not started, so Today can suggest it.

Changing the decision afterwards makes nothing more. The decision log says when a follow-up's task is made.

<Aside type="note" title="Whoever saves it">
The follow-up is made whichever way the decision is saved: on its page in Structure, through an import, or by another module. It's made as the decision's owner.
</Aside>
