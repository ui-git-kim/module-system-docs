This plan was **approved** on 2026-11-28 ("yes go ahead"), with the owner's answers to its [questions](#questions-to-settle-first). **The plan is built** (MV1 to MV7, v1.22.97 to v1.22.103, [below](#mv1-as-built-v12297)); what's left is under Later. MV5 was cut back to arrows on the Gantt ([below](#mv5-what-comes-after-what-is-a-journey)). The rule for every choice in it (the owner): "Nothing should be hardcoded in, if it requires a judgment call, then it becomes an admin config." Where a view would read which entry another entry is inside, it follows the [Connections, Not Parents Plan](/modules/tasks/connections-plan/) instead, since Structure is removing parents and children. It follows the switches built in v1.22.95 (Admin › Tasks › Views: a switch for each of Calendar, Timelines, Schedules and Board on the Tasks page), and the owner's next thought: "maybe the problem is that calendars timelines schedules boards (to an extent) arent views. Because timelines can have different views under timelines, like table, or flow chart type, etc."

<Aside type="note" title="The words">
The words, as the owner settled them (2026-11-28): an **entry** is one thing someone keeps (Structure's code calls it a node), and a **type** is where it's all configured: its fields, stages, page and widgets (Structure's code calls it a node type; Tasks used to say kind, and is changing to type). A **set-up** is Tasks' own: a timeline or calendar an admin configures in Admin › Tasks (which types it shows, their dates and fields).
</Aside>

<Aside type="note" title="Who it's for">
One tenant, one user (the owner: "it's not about teams, it's to help the user organise what they need to organise"). Nothing here assigns work to people, gives anyone else a way in or shows another person's side. "Who" in a view means whatever the person records (who an event was with, whose turn it is), never an account. **But they may share what they make** (the owner: "they might use this to share with others, but this is for them only"): a chronology for their lawyer, a journey map for a client. So every view has to print well and save as a file, and that comes with each view rather than later.
</Aside>

The owner's references: a horizontal milestone line with a card for each year; a month band of chevrons with cards above and below; cards alternating either side of a vertical line; a swimlane chart with bars by month in rows by area; a list of timeline types (Gantt, PERT, historical, Kanban timeline, calendar based, critical path, milestone, vertical); a chronology table (date, event, who was involved, what was done, the outcome); vertical cards with a dot each; a process in weeks with cards above and below; and the chronology and journey-map products of CaseFleet, Harvey and UXPressia. From those products, the parts that suit one person: one set of facts shown as a table or as a picture in one click, filters by type, dates and the fields, and a scale to zoom the picture in and out.

**What it replaces.** The owner's ClickUp, used for a photo booth business: social media posts in a list grouped by status (a category as a coloured label, a suggested and a publish date), a post's page with its fields, subtasks and dependencies, maintenance lists, and setup checklists where one task holds several named checklists. The owner: "as you can see it's overwhelming". To keep: grouping by stage, columns the person chooses, coloured labels, named checklists. To leave out: a number on everything (how many in a group, "5/12", "0 of 11", "33 open"), progress bars and percentages, red "days" badges, banners, and many columns and panels at once.

## What's already there

- **A set-up** (`tasks_view`, made in Admin › Tasks): chosen types, each with its start and end date fields, a group, a title, a summary, up to four more fields and a colour; over every entry the person can see, or one entry's own. It can already be shown as a timeline, a calendar or both (`layouts`), with a switch on its tab. So **a timeline on a calendar works today**.
- **Timeline views:** one, the cards by day (gap labels such as "3 weeks later", year headings), plus My tasks.
- **Calendar views:** Month, Week, Day, List, At a glance and Side by side.
- **Schedules' views:** the planner, On real dates, and side by side.
- **Board:** one, every task in a column for its stage.
- **Journeys:** steps in order, timed from anchors, shown as a numbered trail on an entry's page.
- **What comes after what:** Comes after on tasks (`tasks.after`), and a journey's order and anchors.
- The approved [Timelines and Calendars Plan](/modules/tasks/timelines-plan/) (2026-11-11) already set out "one view, several layouts", phases (R2) and a Gantt with arrows (R4); neither was built. This plan takes them over.

## Modes and their views

| Mode | What it arranges | Views (built) | Views (proposed) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Doings** | The person's own things to do (entries of the types used for tasks) | Board (as Now, Next, Later, and as a column per stage), List (All tasks), Done, Waiting | |
| **Calendar** | Dated entries on a grid of days | Month, Week, Day, List, At a glance, Side by side | |
| **Timelines** | Entries in date order, as a picture or a record | Cards | Table, Milestones, Gantt (with a journey's steps joined), Journey map |
| **Schedules** | Whose time it is, by cycle | Planner, On real dates, Side by side | |
| **Boards** | Entries in columns | Doings' Board | Any set-up by its stage or a list field; a board over time |

**Today** isn't in Doings for now: it stays as it is until day planning is designed, when it becomes a page of its own that helps plan the day. A note on the name: apps set up before v1.20 have stages called Inbox, To do, Doing and Done, so there a Doing column can sit inside Doings.

### One set-up, any mode

The set-up stays the one thing an admin makes. It is shown in one or more modes; for each, the admin chooses which views are offered and which comes first, and the person moves between them. In the data, `layouts` would become modes (`timeline`, which today is `chronology`; `calendar`; later `board`), each with its views and its first. A set-up saved before reads exactly as it does now: its timeline shows Cards, its calendar shows what it shows.

## Timeline views (proposed)

### Table (the chronology)

The starter's shared data table. A row per entry, in date order: **Date** (and time), the type, the title, the summary, then the columns the admin offers: any of the types' own fields (never a secret one), chosen per set-up. The person shows or hides them (remembered in the browser). Sort by any column, filter by type, dates and the fields' values. A row opens its entry ready to edit. Switching between the table and the cards keeps the same dates and filters. **Print** (the columns and filters on screen, on paper, as a schedule's planner prints today) and **Save as a spreadsheet** (CSV), for handing a chronology to someone else. Undated entries aren't in it: they wait in Not scheduled yet, as now.

### Milestones

A line in date order with a marker for each entry and its card above or below, taking turns. It scrolls sideways, with a scale to zoom between weeks, months and years. On a narrow screen it turns into the vertical cards. The admin can choose which types are milestones (every one unless chosen). Cards alternating either side of a vertical line (one of the references) would be a look of Cards on a wide screen.

### Gantt

Each entry with an end as a bar from start to end, one without an end as a marker. Rows (lanes) come from a field the admin chooses (a link or a list: an area, who it's with, the matter it's for) or from the type's group. A scale for weeks, months and quarters. Arrows join a journey's steps in order (MV5, below). From the Timelines plan, still right: moving one thing never moves anything else by itself, and a "today" line is off unless the person turns it on, since a bar ending left of it would read as late.

### MV5: what comes after what is a journey

Proposed first as a view of its own, "Flow": boxes joined by arrows from tasks' Comes after, a link field an admin chose, and a journey's steps, with the longest chain (the critical path) marked. **The owner, 2026-11-28, cut it back.** "Flows" are journeys (flows were folded into journeys in v1.22.48), so there is no Flow view. Their words:

- "what comes after would be it's own 'journey'. And link fields - that would be in the mapping of journey steps."
- "So isnt this like a timeline view? that you have item arrow next item. are we over complicating how this works?"

A timeline already shows one entry, then the next, and a journey already holds its steps in order (its numbered trail on an entry's page draws them). A journey's steps are a list in order, so its longest chain is just the journey itself. What stays: **arrows on the Gantt** from each journey step to the next one on the chart, which the owner kept, for when several journeys' steps share lanes. Tasks' own Comes after becoming a journey of its own is the owner's direction, on the Roadmap, not built.

### Journey map

Another way to draw a timeline, no different underneath from the chronology: the set-up's entries as columns, in date order, and the fields it shows as rows (what happens, where it happens, what the person noticed, ideas), each entry's date on top. The fields are the type's own, set up in Structure. For one person, a journey map is their own: their clients' path through their business, or their own path through something (a move, a court process, a renovation). A set-up over a journey's step types draws a journey this way too.

### Cards (built)

Today's view stays as it is, the first view of every timeline made before.

## Boards across modes (proposed)

- **A board for any set-up:** columns by its types' stage (by meaning where Tasks looks after the stages) or by a list field. A card moves between columns as the person, so Structure's move rules apply, as they do on the Board now.
- **A board over time** (the "Kanban timeline" in the references): a Gantt whose lanes are the stages, or a board whose columns are months. Later.

## The Tasks page and Admin › Tasks

- **The Tasks page:** the bar at the top lists the modes, each shown or hidden by its switch (built in v1.22.95). How the bar shows views is an admin setting: two rows (the modes, then the open mode's views in a row under it, the way the calendar already offers Month, Week, Day and List; the default) or one row (the views in a menu on the open mode). Which view a mode opens on is the admin's choice, and the person's last one is remembered in the browser. The Tasks page still opens on Today, which stays as it is until day planning gives it a page of its own.
- **Admin › Tasks:** the **Views** tab becomes **Modes**. Inside, a list for each mode, as now: its switch for the Tasks page, then the set-ups shown in it (a set-up in more than one mode is listed under each, naming the others). A set-up's sheet gains, for each mode, the views offered and the first.
- **Entries' pages:** Structure's (the [Connections, Not Parents Plan](/modules/tasks/connections-plan/)). A set-up's tab or widget, where an admin configures one, offers each view (a widget under Structure's **Display**, as it offers What's coming up, The latest and The month now). If Structure lets a module draw an Entries list (a Structure field showing the entries linked to an entry), the same drawings are offered there too.

## Calm rules for every view

- No counts, no percentages, no bars filled to show how far along something is. (One exception, the owner's choice: the table says how many rows it shows, as the starter's shared table does.)
- Nothing red and nothing marked late; a past date looks like any other. The words overdue, priority, must and failed never appear.
- Colour is never the only sign: lanes, groups and types are named, markers have borders.
- A chart is never the only way to read it: every view's entries can be read as the Table or the Cards, and each view is reachable by keyboard.
- Every view prints: just the view, never dark, its colours kept, the buttons left off the paper (as a schedule's planner prints since v1.22.33), so the person can hand it to someone or save it as a PDF from the print window.

### MV1 as built (v1.22.97)

- **The bar:** **Today** (on its own), then **Doings**, **Calendar**, **Timelines** and **Schedules**, each shown or hidden by its switch and, for Calendar and Schedules, once there's something to show. Under them, the open mode's views, where it has more than one (so far only Doings: **Board**, **List**, **Done**, **Waiting**). An admin's **A mode's views show in** puts them in a menu on the open mode instead (`tasks_config.settings.views.bar`: `rows` or `menu`).
- **Doings:** opens on the admin's **Doings opens on** view (the Board unless changed; `views.doings.first`), then on the view the person used last (their browser keeps it, as the person's own choice, gone when they sign out). The **Board** is Now, Next, Later and the board by stage as one view, with **Now, Next, Later | By stage** at its top; **The Board opens with** is the admin's (`views.doings.columns`). Each layout keeps its own page, so sidebar links to either keep working. The Board's switch (`onPage.board`) now covers both.
- **Calendar and Schedules** keep their views in their own toolbars (Month, Week, Day, List, Side by side, At a glance; the planner, On real dates), which entries' pages share. A calendar's sheet gains **Opens on** (`calendar.first`, the month unless chosen; one the calendar stops offering opens on the month). On the Calendar page it then opens on the person's last view there.
- **Timelines** had one view, the cards, so no row of views. **Print** is on My tasks and on a timeline on the Tasks page.
- **A set-up's modes** are still stored as `layouts` (`chronology` is the Timelines mode, `calendar` the Calendar mode), so every set-up reads as before. A mode's own views and first are stored per mode as they arrive (the calendar's now; the timeline's with the table).
- **Admin › Tasks › Modes** (`?tab=modes`; old `?tab=views` links, and its Boards and Now, Next, Later lists, open Modes › Doings): **Doings** (how the bar shows views, Doings' first view, the Board's switch and columns, Now, Next, Later's how many), **Calendar**, **Timelines** and **Schedules**, each with its switch.

### MV2 as built (v1.22.98)

- **Where the switch is:** a timeline's views are its own (one set-up can offer the cards, the table or both), and its tab on an entry's page shows them too, so **Cards | Table** sits beside the timeline's Filter, as a calendar keeps Month, Week, Day and List in its own toolbar. The bar's row of views stays for Doings.
- **The set-up:** `timeline` `{ views: ('cards' | 'table')[], first }` (the cards alone for a set-up saved before; a new one offers both and opens on the cards; never none). Each type's `columns` (up to twelve of its own fields, never a secret one; null = the same as **Also show**); columns of different types with the same name are one column. Rows carry the column values with the cards' (`item.fields`).
- **The person's side:** the starter's shared table: sort by any column, search the words, show or hide columns (kept in the browser as the person's own, forgotten at sign-out), a row opens its entry ready to edit. The last view (cards or table) is kept per timeline on a Tasks page. Dates and types come from the timeline's Filter, shared with the cards.
- **Sharing:** **Print** (the table as shown, landscape, its toolbar left off) and **Save as a spreadsheet** (a CSV of every row loaded for these dates with every column; a cell that would start like a formula is saved as text).
- **Left for later:** filters on a list field's values (the search finds them meanwhile), and a table for My tasks.
- **One count:** the starter's shared table always says how many rows it shows ("12 entries"), and its filter menus show counts, so the table offers none of its own filters (the timeline's Filter does that job). The owner chose to keep the row count (2026-11-28: "Keep the count").

### MV3 as built (v1.22.99)

- **The view:** **Gantt** joins Cards and Table in the timeline's own switch. A set-up's `timeline.views` may hold `gantt`, never alone (with neither cards nor a table, the cards come too), and `timeline.scale` (`weeks` | `months` | `quarters`, months unless chosen) is where it starts; the person changes the scale on the chart.
- **Lanes:** each type's `lane` (a single link, a list or a text field; null = the type's group). Rows carry `lane` `{ key, label }`, named on the server as the viewer reads it (a linked entry's name, a list choice's label, the text); an entry with no value sits in its group. Overlapping entries in a lane take a line each.
- **Drawing:** bars for entries with an end (the last day whole unless it has a time), diamonds for those without, each a link to the entry ready to edit with its title, when and lane as its accessible name. Colour is a stripe, never the only sign. The scale's columns are whole weeks from the calendar's week start, months, or calendar quarters ("Jan–Mar 2026"). **Show today** is off unless the person turns it on.
- **Print:** the whole chart on one landscape page (`printPart` now unclips a part marked `data-print-unclip` and shrinks a part wider than the page to fit).
- **Left for later:** dragging (Later), and a remembered scale. (Arrows came in MV5, for a journey's steps.)

### MV4 as built (v1.22.100)

- **The view:** **Milestones** in the timeline's switch; `timeline.views` may hold `milestones`, never alone (with the Gantt, a chart), and `timeline.milestoneScale` (`weeks` | `months` | `years`, months unless chosen) is where it starts.
- **Which types:** each type's `milestone` (on unless the admin unticks it).
- **Drawing:** cards above and below the line in turns, placed by date on the scale; a card that would overlap steps away from the line. Each card is a link to its entry (date, title, type). Under a container width of `@3xl` (a phone) it is the cards down one line. Print fits it to one landscape page. The day and scale sums are shared with the Gantt (`utils/time-scale.ts`).
- **A look of Cards:** `timeline.cardsLook` (`line` | `sides`): either side of the line from `@3xl`, down one line under it.

### MV5 as built (v1.22.101)

- **Rows carry their journey steps:** `item.journey` `[{ run, step, order }]` from the entry's marks (a follow-up isn't a step); `order` is the step's place in its journey, worked out for the page in one read of the viewer's own runs (`journeyStepOrder`), null otherwise.
- **The Gantt:** **Join a journey's steps** (shown only where the chart has two steps of one journey; off unless turned on) draws a grey arrow from each step's bar to the next step on the chart, curving where there's room and routing round the bars where the next step starts before this one ends. Each bar's name for a screen reader says which step it comes after. Arrows print with the chart.

### MV6 as built (v1.22.102)

- **The view:** **Journey map** in the timeline's switch (`map` in `timeline.views`). The owner's words decided its shape: "just timelines ... a different visual presentation". It is the table turned on its side: the same rows of data and the same columns (`columns`, merged by name, shared with the table through `columnsOf`), each entry a column with its date and title on top, no settings of its own.
- **Reading it:** a real `<table>` with a heading per entry and per field, so it can be a timeline's only view (`READABLE_VIEWS`: cards, table, map; a chart needs one of them). An entry's title opens it ready to edit. Print fits it to one landscape page.

### MV7 as built (v1.22.103)

- **Where it is:** a board is one more of a timeline's views (`board` in `timeline.views`, readable, so it may stand alone), "a board on a timeline" in the owner's words, rather than a mode of its own: the same set-up, the same entries. The bar's Board stays Doings' own.
- **Columns:** each type's `boardColumns`: its list's choices where the admin chose a list (`boardField`), else its stages; keyed by a stage's meaning where Tasks looks after the stages and only one stage has it, otherwise by name, so types share columns. Rows carry `column` `{ key, label }`; an entry with neither is **Not set**.
- **Moving:** **Move to…** offers the card's own type's other columns; `PUT /views/:viewId/items/:nodeId/column` (`moveOnBoard`) writes as the person (`viaHttp`): a stage along the type's `pathTo` (Structure's move rules; a refusal in words where none leads there), a list its new choice. Only the person's own entry. Action `my-feature.entry.moved`.
- **Later:** dragging cards, and a board over time (lanes by stage on a Gantt, or columns by month).

## Questions to settle first

All settled on 2026-11-28: Doings is a mode (first in the bar), Today waits for day planning, journey maps are timelines with no feeling line, and the rest below. The proposed answers the owner didn't change stand ("yes go ahead").

6. **Phases:** bands over a stretch of time (from the Timelines plan's R2). Still wanted, and as a view of their own or over the Cards, Milestones and Gantt? *Proposed: over them, later.*
