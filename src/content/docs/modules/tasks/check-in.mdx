"How are you today?" is optional, and it just helps size today's suggestions. The feeling you choose (and, if you like, how it felt) suggests the kind of day: survival, steady or full-energy. The feelings are an admin's list (since v1.22.59).

---

## A bit more, if you like

P6 of the [Task Parts Plan](/modules/tasks/task-parts-plan/), in v1.22.67, borrowed from the owner's "How am I, really?" sheet. After the feeling, **A bit more, if you like** opens four more questions, each a word to choose, each optional:

| Question | The words |
|---|---|
| **Mood** | Heavy · Low · Okay · Good · Light |
| **Energy** | Empty · Low · Some · Good · Full |
| **Mind** | Scattered · Busy · Okay · Settled · Clear |
| **What I need most** | Rest · Space · Connection · Comfort · Clarity · Action |

**I don't know what I need** helps with the last one: I'm exhausted (Rest), I feel stuck (Clarity), I feel alone (Connection), I feel overwhelmed (Space), I feel hurt (Comfort), I keep avoiding something (Action).

The kind of day still comes from the feeling. These answers change nothing by themselves: **Today's rules** can test them ("when I need Rest, one at a time"; "when my mind is Scattered, only Blink and Brief"). See [Setting Up Today's Rules](/modules/tasks/today-rules/).

<Aside type="note" title="Yours alone">
A check-in is one person's private record. The answers are never shown to anyone else (admins included), never written to the server's log, and leave with the person's own data export.
</Aside>

---

## Setting it up (an admin)

- **Admin › Tasks › Suggest & capacity › The check-in's feelings**: the feelings, how each felt, and the kind of day each suggests.
- **Admin › Tasks › Suggest & capacity › A bit more at the check-in**: Mood, Energy, Mind and What I need most, each offered unless switched off.
- Today's rules list the four as facts about the day (**Mood at the check-in** and the rest), and **Try it** can try them.
