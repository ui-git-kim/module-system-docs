Today suggests a few tasks at a time, sized to the kind of day. **Today's rules** let an admin change how it chooses, with short "when this, then that" rules. Added in v1.21.0, as release SR1 of the [Suggest and Capacity Rules Plan](/modules/tasks/rules-plan/).

<Aside type="note" title="Nothing changes until an admin changes it">
The starting rules are exactly what Today has always done. An app that never opens the tab behaves as before.
</Aside>

---

## Where

**Admin › Tasks › Suggest & capacity.** The rules are listed in plain words. **Change the rules** opens Structure's own rule builder, the same Conditions dialog as Structure's fields, with Tasks' choices in it.

<Aside type="caution" title="Needs Structure 1.88.0 or later">
The rule builder is Structure's (v1.88.0). On an older Structure the rules are shown but can't be changed, and Today follows the starting rules.
</Aside>

---

## Two kinds of rule

**A rule on The day** shapes Today as a whole:

- **When** the kind of day is Survival, **then** only essentials, lightest first.
- **When** the day of the week is Saturday or Sunday, **then** prefer the category Garden.
- **When** the time of day is Morning, **then** how many at a time: 2.

**A rule on Each task** changes what happens to one task, and can test the task's own fields:

- **When** the kind of day is Survival **and** Energy is Low-energy win, **then** suggest it.
- **When** the kind of day is Survival **and** Energy is Deep focus, **then** leave it out.
- **When** the kind of day is Full energy **and** Energy is Deep focus, **then** offer it from Later.

### What a rule can test

| About | Tests |
|-------|-------|
| **The day** | Kind of day; Feeling at the check-in; How it felt; Checked in today; Day of the week; Time of day (by the person's own clock). |
| **A task** (rules on Each task) | Size; Essential; Days until it's due (from the person's own today); Category (its name); Now, Next or Later; Kind of entry; and **any field of its kind of entry**: text, numbers, choices from a list, tick-boxes and dates (never a secret). |

A rule on Each task can test the day too. A rule on The day can't test a task, and saying so is refused when the rules are saved.

### What a rule can do

| On | Actions |
|----|---------|
| **The day** | Suggest light / medium / big tasks (Yes or No); Only essentials; Order (lightest first, biggest first, nearest due date first, oldest first); How many at a time (1 to 5); Also offer one from Later; Prefer or leave out a category (by its name) or a kind of entry. |
| **Each task** | Suggest it (even where the day's sizes or essentials-only would leave it out); Leave it out; Bring it forward; Put it after the others; Offer it from Later. |

Some things no rule changes: a task with no size yet, a finished task, one waiting on someone, and one put off with **Not now** are never suggested.

---

## How rules combine

Rules are read from the top.

- **Every rule that holds applies.** For each of the day's settings, **the highest rule that says something wins**. So "Survival: only essentials" and "Weekends: prefer Garden" both apply on a survival Saturday.
- **Prefer and leave out gather**: every rule that holds adds its category or kind.
- **For a task, leave it out beats suggest it**, and the highest rule that moves it (forward or after the others) decides where it goes.
- **Anything no rule says** comes from the kind of day, as before.

---

## The starting rules

Four rules, which are what Today has always done:

1. **Survival:** only essentials; light, medium and big; lightest first.
2. **Steady:** light and medium; nearest due date first.
3. **Full energy:** light, medium and big; biggest first.
4. **Always:** not only essentials; three at a time; nothing from Later.

**Put back the starting rules** returns to them after a confirmation.

---

## Try it

Under the rules, **Try it** describes a day (the kind of day, the date, the time of day, and the check-in) and shows, with your own tasks:

- each of the day's settings and the rule that set it, or "the kind of day" where none did;
- the rules on The day that held;
- what Today would suggest first;
- what rules on Each task did, and to which tasks.

Nothing is saved. Structure's rule builder has its own **Try it** too, which tries one rule with facts you fill in.

## Good to know

- **Today's words follow the rules.** With two at a time, Today offers "Show me the next two".
- **A rule that no longer fits** (it tests a field that was removed, say) is left out until it's changed, and the tab says why under **Needs a look**.
- **Dates in your own fields** use Structure's "today" tests, which go by the server's date. **Days until it's due** goes by the person's own date, so prefer it near midnight.
- **Categories are each person's own**, so rules name a category by its name.
- **Every person's Today follows the same rules.** Letting people set their own is a later release (SR3).
