Today suggests a few tasks at a time, sized to the kind of day. **Today's rules** let an admin change how it chooses, with short "when this, then that" rules. Added in v1.21.0, as release SR1 of the [Suggest and Capacity Rules Plan](/modules/tasks/rules-plan/).

<Aside type="note" title="Nothing changes until an admin changes it">
The starting rules are exactly what Today has always done. An app that never opens the tab behaves as before.
</Aside>

---

## Where

**Admin › Tasks › Suggest & capacity.** The rules are listed in plain words. **Change the rules** opens Structure's own rule builder, the same Conditions dialog as Structure's fields, with Tasks' choices in it.

<Aside type="caution" title="Structure's rule builder">
The rule builder is Structure's (added in Structure v1.88.0; Tasks needs Structure v1.89.14 or later since v1.22.41, so it is always there). Before v1.22.44, on an older Structure, the rules were shown but couldn't be changed. Now, if the rule builder hasn't loaded on the page, the rules are shown but can't be changed until it does, and Today follows the rules shown.
</Aside>

---

## Two kinds of rule

**A rule on The day** shapes Today as a whole:

- **When** the kind of day is Survival, **then** only essentials, lightest first.
- **When** the day of the week is Saturday or Sunday, **then** prefer the category Garden.
- **When** the time of day is Morning, **then** how many at a time: 2.

**A rule on Each task** changes what happens to one task, and can test the task's own fields:

- **When** the kind of day is Survival **and** Energy is Low-energy win, **then** suggest it.
- **When** the kind of day is Survival **and** Energy is Deep focus, **then** leave it out.
- **When** the kind of day is Full energy **and** Energy is Deep focus, **then** offer it from Later.

### What a rule can test

| About | Tests |
|-------|-------|
| **The day** | Kind of day; Feeling at the check-in; How it felt; Checked in today; Day of the week; Time of day (by the person's own clock). |
| **A task** (rules on Each task) | What it takes (its size); Essential; Where it sits; How hard it feels; Time sensitive; When it suits; Needs travel (the [measures](/modules/tasks/measures/), since v1.22.63); Days until it's due (from the person's own today); Category (its name); Now, Next or Later; Kind of entry; and **any field of its kind of entry**: text, numbers, choices from a list, tick-boxes and dates (never a secret). |

Since v1.22.67 the day's facts include the fuller check-in's answers: **Mood**, **Energy** and **Mind at the check-in** and **What I need most** ([The Check-In](/modules/tasks/check-in/)).

A rule on Each task can test the day too. A rule on The day can't test a task, and saying so is refused when the rules are saved.

### What a rule can do

| On | Actions |
|----|---------|
| **The day** | Suggest Blink / Brief / In a sitting / Multi-session / Deep dive tasks (Yes or No, v1.22.63; Brief, In a sitting and Multi-session are the old light, medium and big); Only essentials; Order (lightest first, biggest first, nearest due date first, oldest first); How many at a time (1 to 5); Also offer one from Later; Prefer or leave out a category (by its name) or a kind of entry. |
| **Each task** | Suggest it (even where the day's sizes or essentials-only would leave it out); Leave it out; Bring it forward; Put it after the others; Offer it from Later. |

Some things no rule changes: a task with no size yet, a finished task, one waiting on someone, and one put off with **Not now** are never suggested. Nor are a Scheduled task before its day, a task still waiting on one it [comes after](/modules/tasks/steps/#comes-after), a [regular task](/modules/tasks/regular-tasks/) on a day its rhythm doesn't fall on (or once it's done for the day), and a journey's steps that aren't shown yet.

## How rules combine

Rules are read from the top.

- **Every rule that holds applies.** For each of the day's settings, **the highest rule that says something wins**. So "Survival: only essentials" and "Weekends: prefer Garden" both apply on a survival Saturday.
- **Prefer and leave out gather**: every rule that holds adds its category or kind.
- **For a task, leave it out beats suggest it**, and the highest rule that moves it (forward or after the others) decides where it goes.
- **Anything no rule says** comes from the kind of day, as before.

---

## The starting rules

Four rules, which are what Today has always done:

1. **Survival:** only essentials; all five sizes; lightest first.
2. **Steady:** Blink, Brief and In a sitting; nearest due date first.
3. **Full energy:** all five sizes; biggest first.
4. **Always:** not only essentials; three at a time; nothing from Later.

**Put back the starting rules** returns to them after a confirmation.

## Try it

Under the rules, **Try it** describes a day (the kind of day, the date, the time of day, and the check-in) and shows, with your own tasks:

- each of the day's settings and the rule that set it, or "the kind of day" where none did;
- the rules on The day that held;
- what Today would suggest first;
- what rules on Each task did, and to which tasks.

Nothing is saved. Structure's rule builder has its own **Try it** too, which tries one rule with facts you fill in.

## Check-in feelings

The feelings the check-in offers are an admin's list, in **Check-in feelings** on the same tab (since v1.22.59). It starts as the six the check-in has always had (Energetic, Calm, Joyful, Angry, Tired and Anxious, with how each felt), so nothing changes until you edit it. For each feeling:

- **Its words** and **its icon** (from the app's icon picker; with none, a plain one).
- **It suggests**: the kind of day it suggests. It's only a suggestion, and each person can still change what a feeling suggests for themselves in their Tasks settings.
- **Offered at the check-in**: switched off, it isn't offered, and check-ins already made with it still read. **Remove** takes it out altogether (those check-ins then no longer name it), so switch it off to keep their words.
- **How it felt**: the more specific feelings under it, offered once it's chosen.

The list holds up to 12 feelings, each with up to 10 ways it felt.

The order of the list is the order the check-in shows them (**Move up**, **Move down**). A feeling's short name is made from its first words and never changes, so check-ins keep pointing at it when its words change. **Put the starting feelings back** returns to the six. Today's rules test the feelings by their short names, so a rule about a feeling follows it through a rename. The list goes with **Settings** in Export and Import.

These are the check-in's own feelings, for your own day.

## Check-ins about people

A check-in about someone else (a child, say) uses the same feelings, but it's an ordinary Structure entry filed under that person, and it never changes anyone's Today (since v1.22.60).

1. In **Check-ins about people** on this tab, choose what they're **Kept as**: a kind of entry you've made in Structure for them (a Check-in, say). Tasks adds its fields to that kind: **Feeling** (the feeling, then how it felt, as one chained select of the feelings above), **When**, **Time of day** (morning, afternoon, evening or night) and **Energy** (Empty, Low, Some, Good or Full, never shown as a score). You can place, relabel and show them however you like on the kind's Fields tab, including Structure's **Cards with sub-options inside**; their options follow this list.
2. In Structure, allow that kind under the person's kind (its **Hierarchy** tab).
3. Add the **Check-ins** widget to the person's page in Structure's page designer. **Check in** asks how they are (the feeling and how it felt, the time of day, energy and when), and the widget shows their recent check-ins, newest first, with **Show more**.

Choosing **Not used** stops it; the fields stay on the kind as its own, with every check-in's values.

## Good to know

- **Today's words follow the rules.** With two at a time, Today offers "Show me the next two".
- **A rule that no longer fits** (it tests a field that was removed, say) is left out until it's changed, and the tab says why under **Needs a look**.
- **Dates in your own fields** use Structure's "today" tests, which go by the server's date. **Days until it's due** goes by the person's own date, so prefer it near midnight.
- **Categories are each person's own**, so rules name a category by its name.
- **Every person's Today follows the same rules.** Letting people set their own is a later release (SR3).
