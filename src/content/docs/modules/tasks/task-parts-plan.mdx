This plan was **approved** on 2026-11-20. The owner set out the ideas that day, with reference images (a book page on input, process, output and outcome, and planner-pad sheets on waiting-on lists, decision logs, weighing tasks and checking in), answered two rounds of questions (see [What's settled](#whats-settled)), and said to build it ("smash this out"). Where a question was left, the proposed answer is the one built. **P1, Bite sized, is built (v1.22.62), and P2, the measures (v1.22.63).**

The owner's starting point: "we need bite sized jobs we can do", and every task has an **input** (what's needed), a **process** (how it gets done), an **output** ("what the user is wanting to achieve, and why") and an **outcome** (marking it done, what's next, and a debrief).

<Aside type="note" title="How this fits what's built">
Since v1.22.60 a kind switched on for tasks gets Tasks' fields as real Structure fields (Size, Essential, Due, Happened on, Done on). Everything new here would arrive the same way: fields Tasks supplies to each task kind, which an admin can lay out on the kind's Fields tab, switch off per kind, or point at a field of the kind's own instead (decision [TASK-022](/modules/tasks/decisions/#task-022--today-suggests-tasks-and-reads-dials)). Which kinds of entry are tasks stays the admin's choice in Structure and Entry types, so there is no separate "category" or "task type" field.
</Aside>

## A task in four parts

The book's idea of focusing on the output (the win) rather than the outcome you can't control fits Tasks' no-guilt rules well: Tasks asks what *done* looks like, never whether it went well enough.

| Part | The owner's word | Name on screen | What it holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Input | "What's needed" | **What it needs** | Tasks that come first, things I need (resources), decisions to make, and what it's waiting on |
| Process | "Process" | **How it gets done** | Its rhythm (once, regular, or comes next), its bite-sized pieces, a checklist if the person adds one, and its journey |
| Output | "we need a more fun term" | **The win** | What done looks like, and **why it matters** |
| Outcome | "outcome or debrief" | **Debrief** | Marking it done, what's next, and review, reflect, adjust |

On the task's page in Structure, an admin lays these out like any other fields. In Tasks' own task editor they show as four sections, closed unless they hold something, so a task with just a name stays as light as it is today. Each part can be switched off per kind in Entry types.

## What it needs (input)

- **Comes after**: the tasks this one waits for (a link to tasks). Until they're done, it waits quietly: Today doesn't suggest it, and the task it waits for shows "Then: …" under it. When they're done, it joins the pool by itself. This is the owner's "flow task" (a task that flows on from another one), so it is also the third rhythm under [How it gets done](#how-it-gets-done-process).
- **Things I need**: resources, as a short list of words ("the car", "Sam's birth certificate").
- **Decisions to make**: links to [decisions](#decisions) not made yet, or a few words.
- **Waiting on**: see [The Waiting list](#the-waiting-list).

## How it gets done (process)

### Its rhythm

| Rhythm | What it means | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Once** | The usual | As now |
| **Regular** | Comes round each day, week or chosen days ("a daily task that needs to be done") | The repeat rule calendars already use, on the task. Today shows it on its days, and ticking it done marks that day without making a new entry each time. A release of its own (see [Releases](#releases)). |
| **Comes next** | Flows on from another task | **Comes after** (above) |

### Bite sized

For when a task feels too big to start. A **Bite sized** button on a task: in the task editor, on its row on Today and in lists, and in the task's page header.

**With AI** (an app that has AI Core):

1. Tasks sends the task to AI Core and asks for small, concrete pieces. It sends what the task says about itself: its name, the win, what it needs, and its checklist if it has one. Nothing else about the person goes with it.
2. The pieces come back as a list the person can change: rewrite, remove, reorder, or add their own. **Walk me through instead** is always there.
3. Only the pieces the person keeps are made, when they say so. Nothing is made from the AI's answer by itself.

**Without AI** (or when the person picks Walk me through), a **walk-through**, one calm question at a time:

1. "What's the very first thing you'd do?"
2. "And then?" (as many times as it takes; **That's enough** ends it)
3. "Is there anything you need before you start?" (offered as a piece of its own, or as Things I need)

**Either way:**

- Each piece becomes a **task of its own**, filed under this one as a subtask, so it goes into the pool for Today and planning the day. Each starts **Blink** or **Brief** unless changed.
- The pieces keep their order. While a task has pieces still open, Today suggests its pieces, not the whole task: the first piece still open, so there's one clear next thing (both are admin settings; see [Questions](#questions-still-open)).
- When the last piece is done, Tasks asks quietly whether the whole task is done too.
- Pieces are made as a kind of task Structure lets sit under the task (its own kind first, as subtasks are now). Where no kind can, Bite sized says exactly what to change on a Hierarchy tab in Structure (Tasks never changes a type's hierarchy itself).
- If AI Core can't answer (it isn't installed, it's switched off for this, or it fails), the walk-through opens instead, with no error to deal with.

<Aside type="caution" title="This changes TASK-021">
TASK-021 says Tasks holds nothing for the AI. Bite sized would be one narrow exception: Tasks asks AI Core for one job, by name, through AI Core's own service, and keeps only what the person chooses. AI Core's own settings (which model, what's kept by the provider, spend limits) apply, and an app without AI Core loses nothing but the suggestions. On approval this would be written as a new decision, TASK-028.
</Aside>

### The checklist

The checklist stays, as something the person chooses: **Add a checklist** in How it gets done. It's for small steps that belong to one go at the task (pack the bag, find the form) and stay with it. A checklist item can still be made a task of its own. The difference from bite-sized pieces: pieces are tasks and go to Today; checklist items don't.

### Its journey

A task a journey made already shows its journey. Nothing new here.

## The win (output)

- **What done looks like**: a few words ("the form posted", "the washing in").
- **Why it matters**: a few words, for the days it's hard to start.

Both are optional and never asked for when adding a task. The AI can read them when it finds bite-sized pieces.

---

## Debrief (outcome)

Marking a task done stays one tap. After it, an optional **Debrief** is offered, never required, with the owner's three words as its headings:

| Heading | Prompts (an admin's list, these to start) |
|---|---|
| **Review** | What happened? What went well? |
| **Reflect** | What was hard? How did I feel? Why did it matter? |
| **Adjust** | What would I change next time? What's next? |

- An admin keeps the prompt list, as with the check-in's feelings, and chooses which kinds offer a debrief.
- The answers are kept as fields on the task, so they show on its page and in Structure's lists.
- **What's next** offers to add it as a task that comes after this one.

## The measures, in words

Every measure is a field Tasks supplies, shown as words and never as a score. An admin keeps each list of words (these are Tasks' starting words), and Today's rules can test any of them.

### Where it sits

The owner's five, for how much a task matters and how much room it has. One measure, so nothing is set twice.

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Bound** | Tied to a time or a promise; it can't move (an appointment, a hearing, a commitment) |
| **Pivotal** | Important, essential: other things hang on it, and doing it moves things forward |
| **Routine** | Keeps things going (upkeep, the everyday) |
| **Flexible** | Matters, and can happen whenever it suits |
| **Bonus** | Nice if it happens; nothing rides on it |

"Must finish" and "Priority" can't be used: Tasks' no-guilt rules keep "must" and "priority" out of every screen, and Where it sits says the same thing more kindly. It is the firmness dial of TASK-022, so it settles the word that dial was waiting for. The word for a date that matters ("deadline") is a separate question and stays open; **Time sensitive** (below) says whether a date matters at all.

**Essential:** today's on-or-off switch would be read from Where it sits, so a survival day still shows the bare minimum. Pivotal is the essential, important one (the owner); which words count is an admin setting (Bound and Pivotal to start). A task with Essential on and nothing chosen for Where it sits still counts, so nothing changes for tasks people already have.

### What it takes

The owner's five, instead of Size, for how long a task takes without counting minutes.

| Word | Roughly |
|---|---|
| **Blink** | Done before you know it |
| **Brief** | A few minutes |
| **In a sitting** | One go |
| **Multi-session** | A few goes |
| **Deep dive** | Needs real time and focus |

The field Tasks supplies keeps its stored values, so every task keeps its size: Light shows as Brief, Medium as In a sitting and Big as Multi-session, and Blink and Deep dive are added. Today's kinds of day carry on as now, with the two new words placed beside their neighbours: a survival day offers the lightest first (Blink first), a steady day offers Blink to In a sitting, and a full-energy day offers all five, Deep dive first. An admin can change any of it in Today's rules. A task with nothing chosen is still "unsorted" and is never suggested.

### The others

| The owner's idea | Name | Starting words | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| How hard it is for the person | **How hard it feels** | Easy · A bit of a push · Hard to start · Really hard | About the person, not the task: hanging out washing can be really hard while a complicated project is easy. Kept apart from What it takes. |
| No deadline | **Time sensitive** (on or off) | | Off: the task has no date that matters and shows **When it suits** instead. On: its due date shows as now. |
| A preferred time | **When it suits** (choose any) | Morning · Afternoon · Evening · Late at night · Any time | "After I've done this" is **Comes after**. Today can lean towards what suits the time you check in. |
| Who it's for | **Who it's for** | A link to an entry | A client, a child, you, or the court: whichever kinds the admin picks, never decided by a kind's name. |
| Travel | **Travel** | Needed (on or off), **Where** (words, or a link to a place entry), **Travel time** (A short trip · About an hour · Half a day · All day) | Later, capacity can count travel time on a day. |
| Reminders | **Remind me** | How (in the app, a notification, a text message later) and when | Waits for Notifications in the starter; the in-app prompts already exist. A later release. |

## Statuses

The owner's seven, as a **starter template** an admin can apply, as Now, Next, Later is (stages stay the admin's own, and nothing changes for an app until an admin applies it). Each stage needs a meaning, since meanings are what Tasks reads:

| Stage | Meaning | Today |
|---|---|---|
| **Parked** (where a new task starts) | Not started | Suggested once it has What it takes, as Not started is now |
| **Active** | Doing | Suggested first, as Doing is now |
| **Waiting** | Waiting | Not suggested; on the Waiting list |
| **Scheduled** | **New: Scheduled** (it has its day) | Not suggested before its day; on its day it shows as fixed, beside the three suggestions |
| **Delegated** | Done (handed to someone else) | Not suggested; on the done list, with who has it and an optional follow-up |
| **Completed** | Done | Shown on the done list |
| **Archived** | No longer needed | Not shown; kept on record |

- **Parked** takes the place of "not started" with none of its weight: a parked task is just where it's sitting for now. The Roadmap's Parking lot (somewhere unsorted tasks can sit) is still to be designed with care of its own; Parked could be where it lands.
- **Delegated** means Done: the task is finished as far as you're concerned, because someone else has it (the owner: "since these are solo apps - its a done status"). It isn't on the Waiting list. Moving a task into it asks "Who has it?" and offers a **Follow up** day, which brings back one quiet prompt (or a task) on that day.
- **Scheduled** is the one new meaning. The other choice is to leave it as Not started and show "Scheduled" wherever a task has its day. See [Questions](#questions-still-open).
- **Archived** takes the No longer needed meaning, never worded as cancelled or dropped. Completed tasks stay Completed.

## The Waiting list

From the owner's waiting-on sheet:

- **Waiting on** on a task: **Who** (a link to an entry, or words), **What for**, **Asked on** (a date) and **Check back** (a date).
- Moving a task to a stage that means Waiting asks "Who or what is it waiting on?", and nothing has to be filled in.
- A **Waiting** page in Tasks, beside Today and All tasks: a row per task with Who, What for, Asked on and Check back, in check-back order.
- On the check-back day, one quiet prompt naming what it's for ("Check in about the repair quote?").

Calm rules: no "follow up now", no red, and a check-back day that has gone is just a date. Nothing on the page counts anything.

## Decisions

The owner: "i think decisions might need to be their own node type".

- **A Decision kind in Structure**, made by the admin with the sheet's fields: Decision, Reason, Date and People. Pros and cons are Structure's own fields (two lists, or a field group), so they need nothing from Tasks.
- **A decision log on a task**: the decisions filed under it, or linked to it, listed in a section on the task.
- **Follow-ups make tasks**: Tasks supplies **Follow up** fields to the decision kind (what, and when: in so many days or weeks). Saving one makes a task dated then, filed under the decision's task, the way a journey's follow-ups are made.
- **Decisions to make** (in What it needs) lists the decisions not made yet. This also covers the "decide or do" sheet: a task waiting on a decision can say so.

## The check-in

Your own check-in would borrow from the "How am I, really?" sheet, beside the feelings an admin keeps (v1.22.59):

- **Mood**: Heavy to Light, five steps in words.
- **Energy**: Empty to Full (the same words check-ins about people already use).
- **Mind**: Scattered to Clear.
- **What I need most**: Rest · Space · Connection · Comfort · Clarity · Action.
- **"I don't know what I need"** opens the helper from the "needs check" sheet: I'm exhausted → Rest, I feel stuck → Clarity, I feel alone → Connection, I feel overwhelmed → Space, I feel hurt → Comfort, I keep avoiding something → Action.

Each question is optional and an admin's setting, like the feelings list. Today's rules could test them ("when I need Rest, suggest only Blink and Easy"). The kind of day still comes from the feelings. This belongs with SR2 of the [Suggest and Capacity Rules Plan](/modules/tasks/rules-plan/) (the check-in configured). A check-in stays the person's own private record, and is never sent to the AI.

## Ideas kept for later

- **Task migration** (Reschedule · Simplify · Hand off · Remove): a calm weekly tidy for tasks that keep sitting, beside "Not now". Simplify could open Bite sized.
- **Review, reflect, adjust for the week**, using the same words as the debrief. The reference sheet counts tasks and percentages, which Tasks never does, so only its words and questions carry over.
