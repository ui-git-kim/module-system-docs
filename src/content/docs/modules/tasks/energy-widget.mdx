The **Energy** widget puts the energy part of Tasks on a person's own profile page in Structure: the optional check-in ("How are you today?"), the kind of day, and today's three suggestions. An admin places it on a Profile type's page, and each person sees it on their own profile only. Added in v1.3.3.

<Aside type="note" title="Only ever your own">
A check-in is one person's private record. The widget shows only when the profile on screen is linked to the person signed in. On anyone else's profile, or on one linked to no account, it shows nothing at all, for admins too.
</Aside>

---

## What it needs

- **A Profile type in Structure.** On the type's **Basic** tab, **Type kind** is **Profile (people and organisations)**, with **Entries can be linked to user accounts** switched on. A type that inherits from a Profile type counts too.
- **The person's own entry linked to their account.** Each person opens their own profile entry and switches on **This is me** (an admin can rename that switch on the type).
- Structure v1.77.0 or later.

---

## Placing it

<Steps>

1. In Structure's **App Structure**, open the Profile type and go to **Pages**, then **Detail page**.
2. Choose **Add a widget…** and pick **Energy**. It is offered on Profile types only.
3. Drag it where you want it (the main area or the **Side column**) and choose its size.
4. Save the type.

</Steps>

---

## What it shows

- **The kind of day**: survival, steady or full-energy, one tap to change.
- **Check in**, when the person has the check-in switched on in their Tasks settings. It opens the same check-in as the Today page, inside the widget. **Change how I'm feeling** once they have checked in.
- **Today's three**: the suggestions sized to the day, as on the Today page. They aren't acted on here.
- **Open Today**, which goes to the Today page to start, finish or put off a task.

At the smallest size it keeps to the kind of day and the check-in.

The calm rules apply here as everywhere: it shows what is suggested, never what is left, and no counts.

---

## Good to know

- **It can show by itself on an Overview tab.** On a Profile type that still uses Structure's older page with an Overview tab, Structure shows every widget registered for the type, so Energy appears there on a person's own profile without being placed. To switch it off for a type, use the type's **Integrations** tab. A way to say "only where it has been placed" has been asked of Structure (see the [Roadmap](/modules/tasks/roadmap/#needs-from-structure)).
- **A new Profile type** gets the widget in its **Add a widget…** list as soon as Structure has loaded the type, with no reload needed.
- **Nothing is stored on the profile entry.** The check-in stays in Tasks' own table, per person and per day.
