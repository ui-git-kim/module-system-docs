A **journey** is the usual way something goes, set up once by an admin and started on an entry: a hearing's preparation, a photo booth booking, a website job, filing a document, a big decision. Its steps make new entries, move an entry on to a stage, or wait for something and then do something. Added in v1.22.48; it replaces flows, which belonged to a calendar. The plan and the decisions behind it are the [Journeys Plan](/modules/tasks/journeys-plan/).

<Aside type="note" title="A suggestion, never a rule">
Steps can be skipped, dated, re-dated or added to. Nothing is enforced, scored or counted: no progress bars, no "3 of 10", no red, and a past date is just a date.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note" title="Order first">
A step has no timing unless the admin gives it some, so a journey can be just an order ("Talk to the accountant, then the bank, then decide"). **Next** always means the next step in order. Where timing helps, a step is timed from another step, a date on the entry, the day it started, or the day the entry moved into a stage.
</Aside>

The module ships with no journeys. The examples below show what an admin could set up for their own app.

---

## Set up a journey (an admin)

In **Admin › Tasks**, open **Schedules & journeys**, then **Journeys**, and choose **New journey**. The journey opens in a sheet; nothing is saved until **Save**.

### The journey

- **Its name**, and a **short name** for links and the configuration file (it can't be changed later).
- **What it's for** (optional).
- **Can be started**: switched off, nobody can start it; ones already going carry on.
- **Shown on** (since v1.22.55; it was **Where it lives**):
  - **Pages of a kind of entry**: choose one or more kinds under **The pages of** (a Court proceeding, say). The journey shows on the page of each entry of those kinds, on its **Journeys** tab and in the **Journey steps** widget where you've placed it, and that's where it starts. Several kinds work when they share the dates and stages the steps use.
  - **Its anchor step's page**: choose **Its anchor step**, one of its steps for an entry of a kind. It shows on the page of the entry that fills that step (a Hearing, say), and the steps before and after are timed from its date.

  Those kinds' dates and stages are what steps can be timed from.
- **Its steps' colour**: the numbered circles on the Journey steps widget and the Journeys tab, from the app's palette or any colour (**Use the app's main colour** goes back). Unchosen, they're the app's main colour. A change shows on journeys already going.
- **How it unfolds**:
  - **All at once, next shown** (the default): every step is made at the start, but only the next one (or the number in **Steps showing ahead**) shows on Today, in task lists and on calendars. The rest wait on the journey.
  - **All at once, all shown**: every step is made and shows everywhere.
  - **As they come up**: a step is made once the step before it is done or not needed, once its date is within **Made this many days before its date** (7 unless changed), or once the entry moves into the stage it sits under. Steps not made yet show as names under **Coming up**.
- **When a date it hangs off moves**: **Move the steps, and say so** (the default), **Ask first**, or **Leave them**.
- **More than one at a time**: off unless switched on, so the same journey isn't going twice on one entry.

### Steps

**Add a step** for each, in order (the arrows move one up or down). Each step has a name and does one of three things:

- **An entry of a kind**: a slot for one entry. Choose **A slot for a** (any kind the app has: a Court event, a document, a task) and **How it's filled**:
  - **The person links or adds one** (the default for a new step). On the journey, the step offers **Link a** (the kind) (choose one that already exists, such as a hearing that's already listed) and **Add a** (the kind) (Structure's own form, here, filed under the journey's entry where it can be; it's linked once saved). A linked entry keeps its own date: **Its date is read from** names its date field, so steps can be timed from it, and Tasks never writes into it. **Unlink** takes it out again. **It also takes** has two tick boxes, **Task kinds** and **Event kinds**: tick one or both and the slot can be filled with any kind that is a task, or an event, as well as its own (a Task as well as a Court event, say). What each kind is comes from **What it is** on Entry types, and the kinds each box stands for are named under it. **Link** offers all of them, and **Add one…** asks which kind first.
  - **Tasks makes it** when its turn comes (as "Send the quote" might be). It's filed under the journey's entry, in its kind's usual first stage unless **It starts in** says otherwise. **Its date also goes in** writes the step's date into one of its date fields (a kind used for tasks starts with its due date chosen); a date typed there later comes back as set by hand. **When it's given a date, move it to** moves it to a stage the first time it gets a date.

  The anchor step of a journey shown on its anchor step's page is filled by that entry.
- **Moves an entry on to a stage.** **It moves** the journey's own entry, or what an earlier step made; **To the stage** is one of that kind's stages. It counts as done when the entry reaches that stage or one after it, whether someone ticks the step or moves the entry themselves. **While it's next, its date goes in** can put the step's date on that entry while it's the entry's next move.
- **If this, then that.** It **watches** an entry (optional), and **What it waits for, and what it then does** opens Structure's rule builder. The If can test where any other step stands, days since a step was done, days since a step's date, where the watched entry stands, and its fields. The Then can **Make the follow-up**, **Mark a step not needed**, **Move** the watched entry, or **Start another journey**. It acts once, as soon as its If holds, and is quietly ticked.

Then, for steps that make or move:

- **Timing**:
  - **No timing**: dated when someone knows (the usual for a court event that isn't listed yet).
  - **Days before or after a date**, with a time of day if wanted: "4pm, 7 days before Hearing date". The date can be another step's date, the day another step is done, a date field every kind it starts on has, the day it started, or the day it moved into a stage.
  - **A share of the way between two dates**: "Halfway between Booking date and Event date".

  The words under it say what the timing will read like on the journey.
- **What it needs from the person**: a note shown with the step.
- **Points to**: a kind of entry holding the step's details (a hearing script). The step offers to open the one under the entry, or add one.
- **Ask a question afterwards**: see [Prompts](#prompts-after-an-event).
- **Optional** (any step but an if-step or the anchor step): it shows with angled lines in its circle and the word **Optional**, and it never holds the journey up. It isn't **Next** while another step is open, the steps after it don't wait for it, and the journey finishes without it (an optional step still open then is set aside). A change shows on journeys already going.

### What each kind is

Tasks keeps its own note of what each kind of entry is: a **Task**, an **Event**, a **Schedule** or a **Journey**. Set it with **What it is** in each kind's panel on **Admin › Tasks › Entry types** (the table has a **What it is** column and filter too). Left unsaid, Tasks works it out from what uses the kind: used for tasks is a Task, a kind of schedule is a Schedule, a kind a journey is shown on is a Journey, and a kind shown on timelines or calendars is an Event. Only Tasks reads it; Structure's Type kind (Task) still says only that Tasks looks after the kind.

### Permissions

Who can change a journey once it has started, each **Admin only**, **User and admin** (the default) or **User only**:

- **Add steps**: a step of their own, in between the others (**It goes**: after a step) or at the end. A step someone adds is theirs to edit, skip and take out. **What people can add** has the same two tick boxes, **Task kinds** and **Event kinds** (neither ticked: the kinds its steps use). A task is made straight away; an event is a slot to link one or add one.
- **Edit steps**: rename a step, change its note or its date, or go back to the journey's date.
- **Skip steps**: mark a step not needed, and bring it back.
- **Delete steps**: take a step out of a journey that's going. Its entry stays.

**Who can change this step**, on each step, says otherwise for that step alone: lock it with **Admin only** for edit, skip and delete, and the person can still add and skip the steps around it. A locked step shows a lock on the journey, and its buttons aren't offered to anyone it doesn't allow. Only a journey's own person changes it today, so **User and admin** and **User only** work the same until admins can act on other people's journeys.

The anchor step (where a journey is shown on its anchor step's page) shows an anchor, in the editor and on the journey.

### Triggers

A trigger starts the journey by itself, wherever the change comes from (Tasks, Structure's own forms, an import, an AI chat action). **Add a trigger**:

- **When a** kind of entry **is added**, **has a date set for the first time** (choose the date), or **moves into a stage** (choose the stage).
- **Only entries where…** (optional): Structure's own condition rows.
- **Then start the journey**, or **offer it the next time someone opens the entry** ("Start Hearing preparation?" with **Start it** and **Not now**, which dismisses it for good).

A trigger fires once for the same thing, never starts a second copy of a journey that's going (unless it allows more than one), and switches off when its kind, date or stage has gone; the journey's row says why.

### Checks on save

Saving says in plain words what couldn't work: where it lives not chosen, a kind Structure won't file what Tasks makes under, a kind Tasks can't make by itself because another field has to be filled in (let the person link or add one instead), timing that points at itself or loops, a share outside 0 to 100%, a stage or date not every kind it starts on has, a move of something no earlier step makes, an if-step that tests a step that isn't there, a follow-up that isn't a kind used for tasks, and so on. Up to 10 journeys, 30 steps each, and 10 triggers.

## Three examples

**A court process** (shown on Court process pages; All at once, all shown):
1. **First mention**, **Directions hearing**, **Interim hearing**, **Final hearing**: each a slot for a Court event, filled by the person, its date read from the event's When.
2. **Prepare the affidavit**: Tasks makes a task, 14 days before the Final hearing's date.

Started on the Court process for a matter, the hearings already listed are linked into their slots; the rest wait, with **Add a Court event**, until they're known. When the court moves the final hearing, the affidavit's date follows.

**Hearing preparation** (shown on the Hearing's page, its anchor step; All at once, next shown):
1. **Notes**: Tasks makes a Preparation, its date going into Due, timed 4pm, 7 days before Hearing date, note "The key points".
2. **Summary**: 12pm, 1 day before Hearing date.
3. **Script**: 1 day before Hearing date, pointing to Hearing script, with a question afterwards "How did the hearing go?".

Started on a hearing on Tue 1 Dec, Notes reads "Tue 24 Nov, 4pm · 4pm, 7 days before Hearing date". Move the hearing to Tue 8 Dec and the three steps move, with a note: "The Hearing date changed, so 3 steps moved." **Put them back** restores the old dates.

**Website job** (shown on Website job pages, with stages Enquiry, Quote, Design and Launch; As they come up):
1. **Move to Quote** (moves the journey's own entry to Quote).
2. **Send the quote** and 3. **Follow up the quote** (each makes a To do).
4. **Move to Design**.

Moving the job into Quote by hand ticks "Move to Quote" and makes the two steps under the **Quote** heading. Finishing them doesn't move the job; ticking "Move to Design" does.

**Proofing, with an if-step** (shown on Matter pages):
1. **Make the proof** (makes a Proof, whose kind has a Who field and a Sent stage).
2. **Send the proof** (moves that proof to Sent).
3. **If there's no reply in two weeks** (watches the proof): If *days since Send the proof was done* is more than 13 and *where the proof stands* is Sent, then **Make the follow-up**: a Task titled "Check in with `{Who}` about `{step}`", light.

Two weeks after the proof was sent, one task appears: "Check in with the printer about Send the proof", dated that day, ready for Today. When the proof is approved, the follow-up quietly moves to no longer needed.

## Prompts after an event

A prompt asks once, the first time the person comes in after something has happened, to add what happened.

- **On a journey step**: **Ask a question afterwards** on the step. It applies to the entry the step makes or moves, and asks once that entry's date has passed or it has moved to a stage that means done.
- **On a kind of entry**: **Admin › Tasks › Prompts**, **New prompt**: **On every** (a kind: every changeover, say), **The question** ("How did the handover go?"), **It has happened when** (a date field has passed, or it moves to done), **Add it now opens** (the entry itself, ready to edit, or a new entry of a kind, filed under it in Structure's form, filled in), and **Stop asking after** (3 days unless changed).

The person sees one dialog a visit, wherever they come in: the question, the entry and its date, and **Add it now**, **Later** (a task "Add notes: …" goes into their tasks, and it doesn't come back) or **No need**. Closing it counts as Later. A prompt nobody answers stops asking, quietly, once its days are up. Answers are each person's own.

## Starting a journey (everyone)

- **Its steps show before it starts** (since v1.22.55). On an entry where a journey can be started and isn't going, the **Journeys** tab and the **Journey steps** widget lay its steps out as they'd be, each slot with **Add a** (the kind) and **Link one**. Linking an event you already have, adding one, or adding a step starts the journey there and then. Nothing is written until you do one of those.
- **Start** (the journey's name) is offered under its steps when starting it does more than fill slots (a step Tasks makes, a move or an if-step).
- **Start a journey** is in an entry's header and under **Not scheduled yet** on a calendar that belongs to the entry, and on the **Journeys** tab for a second copy of a journey that allows more than one. It starts straight away; it asks only for a date on the entry that its steps are timed from and that isn't set yet (fill it in, or leave it).
- A journey a trigger offers shows at the top of the Journeys tab: **Start it** or **Not now**.

## The Journeys tab

Each journey on the entry shows:

- **Its name and why it started**: "Started by you on 3 Nov", "Started when this hearing was added", "Started when Hearing date was set", "Started when it moved to Quote".
- **Quiet notes** when a date it hangs off changed: what moved (**Show me**, **Put them back**), a question (**Move these**, with the steps ticked except ones set by hand, or **Keep the dates**), or dates that stayed (**Move them**).
- **Next**: the next step first, with what it needs and its date with the reason.
- **The rest**: done steps quietly ticked, not-needed ones dimmed, a stage's name as a heading for the steps under it, if-steps in plain words, and **Coming up** for steps not made yet.
- On each step: **Done**, **Not needed** (or **Back to do**), **Change date** (a date you set stays put: "Set by hand"), **Use the journey's date** to go back, and **Open** or **Add** the kind it points to.
- **Add a step**: a one-off step on this journey only.
- **On a calendar**: tick a calendar that belongs to this entry. The journey shows there as a group of its own in the key (people can hide it where groups are hideable), **Only the next step** or **Every dated step**, and its steps' entries aren't shown again under the calendar's own kinds.

When every step is done or not needed, the journey folds away under **Finished journeys**.

## The Journey steps widget

For a page that shows the journey at a glance, add the **Journey steps** widget to a kind's page in Structure's page designer (it shows only where an admin places it, on any kind). It lists every journey going on the entry, and each one that can be started here and isn't going, its steps in order, joined by a line down the side:

- **A numbered circle** for each step, in the journey's colour: at half strength until the step has its entry, with angled lines for an optional step, a tick once it's done, quiet once it's not needed. An if-step is a small dot with its words.
- **The step's name**, and under it **its entry and date** ("Wed 27 Jan 2027, 10am: Interim hearing"), **Not scheduled yet** for a slot nobody has filled, or the step's date.
- **Add a** (the kind) and **Link one** on a slot still empty, two buttons alike (Link one is for an entry that already exists), and **View** and **Edit** once it's filled. The anchor step is the page's own entry, so it has neither.
- **+ Add a step** across the entry under each step (a line either side; it shows when you hover there or reach it with the keyboard, and all the time on a touch screen): add a step of your own there. It's a task, made straight away, or an event kind's slot to link or add one, as the journey's **What people can add** allows. On a journey not started yet, adding a step starts it.
- **Open the Journeys tab** under it, for everything else (ticking steps, dates).

Like Tasks' other widgets it draws no card or heading of its own, so Structure's heading and frame settings apply.

## Calm rules

- No progress bars, counts of what's left, percentages of progress, streaks or red. A past date is a plain date.
- "Overdue", "priority", "must" and "failed" appear nowhere.
- An if-step and a prompt act once; nothing repeats or nags. Reminders wait until the starter's Notifications can keep them rare and dismissible.
- A step waiting on someone else is never suggested on Today.

## Good to know

- **Journeys are worked out** when one starts, when someone acts, when Structure reports a change to their entries, and each time the person comes into the app (at most once a minute). Nothing runs in the background, so a follow-up due on a day nobody opens the app appears the next time they do, dated its own day.
- **Changing a journey** never changes one already going: each keeps a copy of the journey as it was when it started. Removing one leaves the ones going, and their entries.
- **Structure's own lists** still show the steps a journey isn't showing yet: they're real entries. Tasks' own lists (Today, All tasks, the Board, Now Next Later, the Tasks tab, Not scheduled yet) leave them out.
- **The day an entry moved into a stage** is noted by Tasks when it hears the move, so a move before the journey started isn't known.
- **Export and import**: journeys and prompts on kinds are part of **Admin › Tasks › Data**. A person's started journeys, trigger offers and prompt answers leave with their account data download, and go with their account.
- **For other modules**: Tasks' journeys service (`tasks.journeys` in the service registry) lists the journeys a kind can have, starts one as the entry's owner, and reads a started one.
- **Not clicked through yet.** Journeys are tested and type-checked; see the [roadmap](/modules/tasks/roadmap/#journeys-havent-been-clicked-through-in-a-running-app).
