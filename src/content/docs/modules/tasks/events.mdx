The owner, 2026-11-25: "Tasks and Events are different — an event is a calendar item, a task is what we put together beforehand. Now an event can include tasks, and be in a journey."

An **event** is something on a calendar: it has a day, and a time unless it's all day (an appointment, a hearing, a school camp). A **task** is something to do, often before an event. Tasks can sit under an event, and an event can be a step of a [journey](/modules/tasks/journeys/).

This page covers **E1** (built in v1.22.74) and the plan for E2 to E5.

---

## E1: event kinds and the calendar (built, v1.22.74)

### Making a kind an event

On **Admin › Tasks › Entry types**, open a kind's **Edit** and set **What it is** to **Event**. Then:

- Tasks gives it **Starts**, **Ends** and **All day** as real fields in Structure, like the fields a task kind gets. People fill them in on the kind's own form, and change them there.
- Where the kind already has a date field of its own (a Hearing date, say), choose it under **As an event** for Starts or Ends, and Tasks doesn't add its own for that one.
- A kind that's only *worked out* to be an Event (because it's on a timeline) gets nothing until you say it is: its panel offers **It's an Event**.

<Aside type="note">
A kind that stops being an Event keeps Starts, Ends and All day as its own fields, values and all, so nothing anyone entered is lost. You can remove them in Structure. The same happens to Tasks' own Starts when you choose one of the kind's own fields instead.
</Aside>

Starts and Ends are the same fields a schedule gives the kinds it uses, so a kind used both ways never gets two of each.

### A whole day

With **All day** ticked, an event shows on a calendar as whole days, and its time doesn't count. Its **Ends** is its last day: a camp from Friday with Ends on Sunday shows Friday to Sunday.

### Showing it on the calendar

Each Event kind has **Show on the calendar**, on its panel or as a row action in the list. It puts the kind on the **default calendar**, read by its Starts and Ends.

- With no calendars yet, Tasks makes one called **Calendar** (the main calendar) when the first kind goes on it. It's a calendar like any other: change its look under **Views › Calendars**. It goes away when the last kind comes off, and is made again when one goes back.
- **Views › Calendars** has **Default calendar**: the main one unless you choose another. The Calendar page opens on it, and Show on the calendar puts kinds on it.
- A calendar you made keeps its last kind, since it's yours to remove there.

<Steps>

1. On **Entry types**, set **What it is** to **Event** for Daily Life (say). Starts, Ends and All day appear on its form in Structure.
2. On the same panel, switch **Show on the calendar** on.
3. Give an appointment its Starts (and Ends) in Structure. It's on the Calendar page.

</Steps>

## The plan for what comes next

From the owner's answers on 2026-11-25. Each is built only once the one before is out, and each choice that could go more than one way is an admin's setting.

| Release | What it adds |
|---|---|
| **E1 · Event fields and the calendar** (built, v1.22.74) | Starts, Ends and All day on every Event kind (or its own); the main calendar; the admin's default calendar; Show on the calendar per kind |
| **E2 · Events on a person's page** | A widget for any entry's page (a person, a child, a Daily Life entry): its upcoming events, and **Add** an event for it, through Structure's own form, filled in. Found by a link field (Who for, say) or by where it's filed. Tasks under an event, from its page |
| **E3 · Where, repeating, how long** | **Where**: words, or a link to entries of kinds the admin chooses (a place, a person). **Repeats**: the calendars' own repeat rule as fields people fill in. **How long**, where an admin chooses it instead of Ends |
| **E4 · Reminders and my calendar** | **Reminder** on an event, and **Add to my calendar**: the event goes to the person's own Google or Microsoft 365 calendar through [calendar sync](/modules/tasks/calendar-sync/), with its reminder |
| **E5 · Tasks on the calendar** | Dated tasks beside events on the default calendar: an admin's setting (off, on, or each person chooses) |
