The owner, 2026-11-25: "Tasks and Events are different — an event is a calendar item, a task is what we put together beforehand. Now an event can include tasks, and be in a journey."

An **event** is something on a calendar: it has a day, and a time unless it's all day (an appointment, a hearing, a school camp). A **task** is something to do, often before an event. Tasks can sit under an event, and an event can be a step of a [journey](/modules/tasks/journeys/).

This page covers **E1** (built in v1.22.74), **E2** (built in v1.22.75), **E3** (built in v1.22.76), **E4** (built in v1.22.77) and **E5** (built in v1.22.78): the whole plan below.

## E1: event kinds and the calendar (built, v1.22.74)

### Making a kind an event

On **Admin › Tasks › Entry types**, open a kind's **Edit** and set **What it is** to **Event**. Then:

- Tasks gives it **Starts**, **Ends** and **All day** as real fields in Structure, like the fields a task kind gets. People fill them in on the kind's own form, and change them there.
- Where the kind already has a date field of its own (a Hearing date, say), choose it under **As an event** for Starts or Ends, and Tasks doesn't add its own for that one.
- A kind that's only *worked out* to be an Event (because it's on a timeline) gets nothing until you say it is: its panel offers **It's an Event**.

<Aside type="note">
A kind that stops being an Event keeps Starts, Ends and All day as its own fields, values and all, so nothing anyone entered is lost. You can remove them in Structure. The same happens to Tasks' own Starts when you choose one of the kind's own fields instead.
</Aside>

Starts and Ends are the same fields a schedule gives the kinds it uses, so a kind used both ways never gets two of each.

### A whole day

With **All day** ticked, an event shows on a calendar as whole days, and its time doesn't count. Its **Ends** is its last day: a camp from Friday with Ends on Sunday shows Friday to Sunday.

### Showing it on the calendar

Each Event kind has **Show on the calendar**, on its panel or as a row action in the list. Once there's a default calendar, the panel names it: **Show on ‘Calendar’**, say. It puts the kind on the **default calendar**, read by its Starts and Ends.

- The default calendar is always one with **a page of its own**: the Calendar page in Tasks. A calendar that belongs to an entry (a matter's, say) stays on that entry's page and is never the default (since v1.22.80).
- With no such calendar yet, Tasks makes one called **Calendar** (the main calendar) when the first kind goes on it, whatever calendars entries already have. It's a calendar like any other: change its look under **Views › Calendars**. It goes away when the last kind comes off, and is made again when one goes back.
- **Views › Calendars** has **Default calendar**: the main one unless you choose another calendar with a page of its own. The Calendar page in Tasks is this calendar and nothing else (since v1.22.82), and Show on the calendar puts kinds on it.
- **It shows the space open in the header.** It reads as you, so Structure decides what's in it: the entries in the space you've chosen in the header, your entries that are in no space yet, and kinds no space keeps. Choose another space in the header and it reads again.
- A calendar you made keeps its last kind, since it's yours to remove there.

<Steps>

1. On **Entry types**, set **What it is** to **Event** for Daily Life (say). Starts, Ends and All day appear on its form in Structure.
2. On the same panel, switch **Show on the calendar** on.
3. Give an appointment its Starts (and Ends) in Structure. It's on the Calendar page.

</Steps>

## E2: events on an entry's page (built, v1.22.75)

The owner: "I want to be able to add that to a widget or something in the person/child pages."

The **Events** widget goes on any kind's page: a person's, a child's, a professional's, anything an event points at. Add it in Structure's page designer for that kind (it's offered on every kind, and shows only where it's added).

- **What it lists:** the entries of Event kinds that link to this entry through one of their own link fields (Who for, With who…), or are filed under it. From today on, soonest first: the first five, then **Show the rest** (up to 30 in all). Past events aren't listed. Ones with no Starts yet are listed apart, under **Not dated yet** (up to 10).
- **Each one** says when ("Fri 4 Dec, 9:30 am to 10:15 am", "Fri 4 Dec to Sun 6 Dec", "Fri 4 Dec, all day") and what kind it is, and opens its own page.
- **Add** opens Structure's own form for an Event kind, filled in with this entry: through the kind's link field that names this entry's kind (Who for, say, on a child's page), or filed under it where a Hierarchy tab names the pair. Nothing is made until Create. A kind that could go anywhere isn't offered on every page, and a kind the person can't create isn't offered.
- A kind a space keeps is read inside this entry's space. While that space is open in the header, your events in no space yet (made at the top level and linked to this entry, say) are listed too, as Structure shows them (since v1.22.81).

**Tasks under an event:** an event's page shows the Tasks tab, with Add task, when a kind used for tasks can sit under the event's kind and its **A Tasks tab on entries these can sit under** is on (Entry types, the task kind's panel).

## E3: where, repeating and how long (built, v1.22.76)

Each of these is set on the kind's panel under **As an event**, and each is a field people fill in on the kind's own form in Structure.

### Where

- **Tasks' own Where, in words** (unless you choose otherwise): a text field.
- **Tasks' own Where, a link to an entry**: choose the kind it links to under **Where links to** (a Place kind, say, or a person). People choose one of their entries of that kind. Only kinds for everyone can be chosen.
- **One of the kind's own fields** (a text, list or link field it already has): Tasks adds no Where of its own.
- **No Where.**

Changing from one to another hands the old field to the kind as its own, values and all, as with Starts and Ends.

### How long

**When it ends** is **Ends** unless you choose **How long it takes**. Then people choose how long (15 minutes to 4 hours) instead of typing an Ends, and Tasks works Ends out from Starts. Ends still shows on the form, but only Tasks writes it. How long needs Tasks' own Ends, so it can't be chosen while the kind uses one of its own fields for Ends. An event with no time, or ticked All day, keeps whatever Ends says.

### Repeats

**Repeats** is on unless you switch it off. It adds two fields:

- **Repeats**: every day, week, fortnight, month (the same day of the month) or year. Empty means it doesn't repeat.
- **Repeats until**: its last day. Empty means it keeps repeating.

Tasks turns them into the calendars' own repeat rule, so the event is still one entry, never one per time. On the default calendar the kind's repeats show. On another calendar they show where its admin has switched repeats on for the kind. A repeat changed on a calendar (every second week, say) stays as it was set there, until someone changes Repeats or Repeats until on the form.

<Steps>

1. On Daily Life's panel, set **When it ends** to **How long it takes**.
2. Give a swimming lesson Starts on Tuesday 1 December at 4 pm, How long 1 hour, Repeats every week, Repeats until 22 December.
3. It's on the calendar every Tuesday from 4 to 5 pm, to 22 December.

</Steps>

## E4: reminders and your own calendar (built, v1.22.77)

The owner: "reminder, add to "calendar" - so the integration stuff".

### Reminder

**Reminder** (on unless you switch it off, on the kind's panel) adds a Reminder to the kind's form: at the time, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes before, 1 or 2 hours before, 1 or 2 days before, or a week before. On an **all-day event** the reminder comes the day before (or the days before it says) **at a time of day**: 9 am unless the admin chooses another usual time, and each person can choose their own on their **Your calendar** card (v1.22.79; the owner: "that's something to ask the user"). Neither calendar can remind once the day has begun, so a reminder shorter than a day ("At the time" to "2 hours before") comes the day before at that time too.

Tasks doesn't send reminders itself: the reminder goes with the event to the person's own calendar, which reminds them (see Add to my calendar). **Usual reminder** is the one a new event starts with (none unless you choose one); people can change it.

### Add to my calendar

Where someone has [calendar sync](/modules/tasks/calendar-sync/), their events can go to their own Google or Microsoft 365 calendar. On the kind's panel, **Add to my calendar** is one of:

- **Each person ticks the ones that go** (unless you choose otherwise): an **Add to my calendar** tick box on the form, unticked to start.
- **Each person chooses, ticked to start**: the same tick box, ticked on a new one.
- **Every one goes**: no tick box; each one with a Starts goes.
- **None go.**

An event that goes:

- **At its times**, in the person's own time zone: to its Ends (or How long), or a quarter of an hour where it has no end. A timed event shows as **busy**; an all-day one as **free**, over all its days, as the calendars show a whole day.
- **With its Reminder**.
- **Repeating as it does**: its repeat goes as a repeating event, in the other calendar's own words. A repeat the other calendar can't say the same way goes as its first time only: one that pauses or runs only during periods, starts at another time on some days, or follows Easter; and on Microsoft 365, one with days left out, on a fifth weekday or the last day of the month, or on a day some months (or years) don't have.
- It **follows the event**: a new time, name, reminder or repeat changes it; unticked, or removed, it comes off. A change to how it repeats makes the event afresh.
- **Moved in the calendar**, it moves in Tasks too (an admin's switch): its Starts, and its Ends where it has one of its own (with How long, Tasks works Ends out again). A repeating event moved there stays as moved there.

The admin's **Events can go too** (Admin › Tasks › General › Calendar sync) switches it off for everyone.

<Aside type="note">
Switching a kind to **Every one goes** or **None go** hands the tick box to the kind as its own field, values and all, as with Where and Ends. Switching back keeps it as the kind's own, and it still works.
</Aside>

## E5: tasks on the calendar (built, v1.22.78)

The owner: "Admin can configure, and users might be able to decide depending on admin config."

On **Admin › Tasks › Views › Calendars**, under Default calendar:

| Setting | What it does | Unless changed |
|---|---|---|
| **Dated tasks on the default calendar** | **Not shown**, **Shown for everyone**, or **Each person chooses**: off to start, or on to start | Not shown |
| **Which tasks** | **Every one with a due date**, or **Only ones whose date matters** (time sensitive, or in a stage that means Scheduled) | Every one with a due date |

Where they show, a person's own open tasks with a due date in the days on screen appear on the default calendar as **My tasks**: a group in the legend, each task on its due day as a whole day, opening its own page. Done and set-aside tasks never show, someone else's never do, and a past due date looks like any other day.

- **Where each person chooses**, the calendar's **On this calendar** card has **Show my tasks with a due date**. Their choice is kept for them (on every device), and stays when the admin changes "off to start" to "on to start".
- **Only the default calendar**, which always has a page of its own.
- **The space open in the header** applies, as for the calendar's own kinds: a task of a kind a space keeps shows while its space is open (since v1.22.80).
- **Nothing twice:** a task kind the calendar already shows by itself isn't shown again as My tasks, and a journey's step the journey shows later isn't shown yet.
- A **regular task** (one with a rhythm) shows on its due date, if it has one, not on each day of its rhythm.

From the owner's answers on 2026-11-25. Each is built only once the one before is out, and each choice that could go more than one way is an admin's setting.
